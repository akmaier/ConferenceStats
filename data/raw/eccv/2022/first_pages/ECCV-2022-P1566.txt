As mentioned in the manuscript, Cluster Feature vector (CF vector) is a commonly used data structure for summarizing large amounts of data. In this part,
we explained its incrementality and additivity properties in detail (For the convenience of reading, we start with the definition of CF vector).
The CF vector has three components: K, the number of data objects, LS, the
linear sum of the data objects, and SS, the sum of squared data objects. The
structures LS and SS are n-dimensional vectors. These three components allow
to compute cluster measures, such as cluster mean µ (Eq. (1)) and radius σ (Eq.
(2)).
