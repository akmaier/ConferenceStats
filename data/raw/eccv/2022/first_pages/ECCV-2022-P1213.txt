Analysis on Long Videos.

To further prove the rationality and effectiveness of our dynamic memory updating strategy, we show the qualitative results on long videos in Fig. 1. In general,
the most common practice for updating memory in practical applications is to
retain the most recent memory frames. However, this approach is difficult to
deal with object appearance changes or scene changes. As shown in Fig. 1, only
retaining the most recent memory frames may cause the memory bank losing
perception of the target object but our dynamic updating strategy allows for
superior segmentation effect.
