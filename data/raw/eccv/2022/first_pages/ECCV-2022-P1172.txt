Number of PTIE Layers

Multiple PTIE models were trained with varying number of layers for the encoder
and decoder noting that both have the same number of layers. The results of
the latency and weighted average accuracy are shown in Table A1. The PTIE
models show a trade-off between accuracy to number of parameters and latency.
All the PTIE models are competitive/outperform other recent works that are
open source in terms of accuracy.
