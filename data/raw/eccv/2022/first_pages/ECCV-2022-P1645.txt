Appendix A

Dataset Comparison

To fully evaluate BodySLAM, we required video sequences of human motion
captured by a moving camera where camera intrinsics, ground truth camera trajectories and ground truth human body parameters were available. As shown in
Table 1, none of the existing human motion datasets include all of these required
elements. For this reason, we captured our own dataset of video sequences where
the camera and human subject are both in motion, and used a motion capture
system to obtain ground truth values.
