Fig. 1: An illustration of UniHead detailed architecture.

A detailed illustration is given in Fig.1. First, K scattered points are generated through an multi-layer perceptron and we use bilinear interpolation to
extract point representations. Then, together with a learnable embedding vector (optional), they are fed into transformer encoders. Finally, we use different
MLPs to get the final results, where “task pred” means predictions from the
task token, like IoU token.
