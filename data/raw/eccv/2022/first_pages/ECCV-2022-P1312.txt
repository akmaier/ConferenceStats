2. DISCUSSION ON COMPUTATIONAL COST
Fig. S2 compares our method with others on reconstruction quality versus computational cost on
the test set of Tanks-and-Temples. We plot two versions of our method (the proposed version in
the main paper and a lightweight one with lower input resolution and fewer neighbor views).
We see that our method has comparable or better quality-cost tradeoffs than prior works on
the intermediate set of Tanks-and-Temples, but is significantly more memory-efficient on the
advanced set of Tanks-and-Temples.
