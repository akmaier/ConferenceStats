Mathematical Analysis of Relative Contrastive Loss

1.1

Detailed Formulation Analysis of Relative Contrastive Loss.

We start by denoting two different images x and x′ . Given a criteria Mi , we
define their label as Yi (x) and Yi (x′ ), respectively. Inspired by BYOL [5] and
Simsiam [3], the predictor layer aims to predict the expectation of projections
z under transformation Ti , i.e., ETi (z), where Ti is semantic-invariant on Mi ,
i.e., Yi (Ti (x)) = Yi (x).
To analyze the formulation of our relative contrastive loss given two images
x and x′ , we start with its individual component in Eq. (2) of the main paper,
i.e.,
  \label {eq:relative_contrastive_loss} \small \mathcal {L}_{RCL}\left (\mathbf {x}, \mathbf {x}', \theta ; \{\mathcal {M}_i\}_{i=1}^H\right )=\sum _{i=1}^H \alpha _i \mathcal {L}(\mathbf {x}, \mathbf {x}', \theta ; \mathcal {M}_i), 

(1)

where αi is the trade-off parameter among different criteria. The loss L(x, x′ , θ; Mi )
for criterion Mi can be defined as
  \label {eq:single_relative_contrastive_loss} \small \mathcal {L}(\mathbf {x}, \mathbf {x}', \theta ; \mathcal {M}_i) = -\log \left [\frac { \mathbb {I}\left [\mathcal {Y}_i(\mathbf {z})=\mathcal {Y}_i(\mathbf {z}')\right ] \times \exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {z}'/\tau )}+ \mathbb {I}\left [\mathcal {Y}_i(\mathbf {z}) \ne \mathcal {Y}_i(\mathbf {z}')\right ]}{\exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {z}'/\tau )}+\sum _{k=1}^K \exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {s}_k/\tau )}}\right ]. 
(2)

When Yi (z) = Yi (z′ ),
  \label {eq:single_relative_contrastive_loss_positive} \small \mathcal {L}^{+}(\mathbf {x}, \mathbf {x}', \theta ; \mathcal {M}_i) = -\log \left [\frac { \exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {z}'/\tau )}}{\exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {z}'/\tau )}+\sum _{k=1}^K \exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {s}_k/\tau )}}\right ], 

(3)

which pulls the query predictions qMi and projection z′ together.
When Yi (z) ̸= Yi (z′ ),
  \label {eq:single_relative_contrastive_loss_negative} \small \mathcal {L}^{-}(\mathbf {x}, \mathbf {x}', \theta ; \mathcal {M}_i) = -\log \left [\frac {1}{\exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {z}'/\tau )}+\sum _{k=1}^K \exp {(\mathbf {q}_{\mathcal {M}_i}^\top \mathbf {s}_k/\tau )}}\right ], 

which pushs the query predictions qMi and projection z′ apart.
