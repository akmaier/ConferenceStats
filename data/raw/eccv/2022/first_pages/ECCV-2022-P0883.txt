Architecture

We tested our proposed framework using: 1) ResNet-based architecture, and 2)
UNet-based architecture. Detailed architectures are shown in the following. The
main difference between these two architectures is the skip connection. Through
skip connection, the decoder layers have access to feature maps at higher levels,
thereby preserving finer detail information.

Fig. 1: ResNet-based architecture. Res-9 includes 4 basic blocks and Res-13 includes 5 basic
blocks for encoder. As shown, most convolutional operations are performed in 2D, with the final layers
being 3D. Res-9 encoder performs 8x downsampling and Res-13 encoder performs 16x downsampling.
