6.1

Positional Encoding

In the Transformer module of LSparse-Net and SDense-Net, we apply the 2D
extension of positional encoding following [1] as:

  {\rm PE}(x,y)^i:= \left \{ \begin {aligned} \sin (\omega _k\cdot x)&,i=4k\\ \cos (\omega _k\cdot x)&,i=4k+1\\ \sin (\omega _k\cdot y)&,i=4k+2\\ \cos (\omega _k\cdot y)&,i=4k+3\\ \end {aligned} \right . , 

(7)

positional encoding, and i is the index for feature channels.
