Implementation Details

We use the benchmark dataset PCPNet [1] to train the proposed network and
test it. The training set contains eight shapes: four CAD objects (boxunion, cup,
fandisk and flower) and four high-quality scans of figurines (armadillo, bunny,
dragon and turtle). All shapes are modeled as triangular meshes and densely
sampled with 100 k points. To augment the training set, we add Gaussian noise
with zero mean and varying standard deviation σ ∈ {0.012, 0.006, 0.00125}, with
respect to the diagonal length of the bounding box for each model. Then we
attain 32 point clouds for training. The test set has 19 shapes, including CAD
objects, figurines, and analytic shapes.
The proposed network is trained and implemented by the Pytorch framework
on a Nvidia Tesla v100 GPU, using the Adam optimizer[4]. The batch size and
initial learning rate are equal to 256 and 1e-3, respectively. We train the network
600 epochs in total, and the learning rate decays to 10% of the initial value at
epoch 200 and 500. The overall training loss is defined as
