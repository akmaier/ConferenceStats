Improve ShuffleNet v2 with Cyclic Shuffle

As mentioned in the main-body of the paper, the proposed cyclic shuffle shows
its well performance on group convoltuions for quantized ResNet-18 and ResNet34. Since we assume that it can serve as a complement of channel shuffle, here
we explore the performance of cyclic shuffle on ShuffleNet v2.
As we didn’t find the official training codes of ShuffleNet v2, we re-implemented
the ShuffleNet v2 training algorithm under Pytorch framework. We adopt an
SGD optimizer with the momentum set to 0.9. The weight decay is set to 1e-4,
and the learning rate initialized by 0.1 is adjusted with a cosine learning rate
decay strategy. All models in this subsection are trained from scratch for 300
epochs with a batch size of 512.
As shown in table 1, we can achieve +0.48% higher top-1 accuracy by adding
one cyclic shuffle module at the beginning of stage3 and stage4 in ShuffleNet
v2. This indicates that our proposed method can be applied on the deep neural
networks that contains group convolutions or channel splitting modules to obtain
higher accuracy.
