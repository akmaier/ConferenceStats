Non-target Slices as Motivation

For the non-target slice set defined in the paper, the images are derived from
those slices in CT or MRI scans whose ground truths are all black. As shown in
Fig. 1, these non-target slices contain rich anatomical knowledge, which can be
utilized as background information to help the contrastive learning and guide
the segmentation of the query image in the few-shot tasks.
