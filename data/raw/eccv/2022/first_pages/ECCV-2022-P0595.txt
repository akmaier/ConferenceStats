More Swapped Results

We show more swapped faces on FaceForensics++ [3] and CelebA-HQ [3] in
Fig. 1 and Fig. 2. MegaFS [10] fails to produce realistic facial texture due to the
directly blending post-processing operation. Compared to the FaceShifter [4] and
SimSwap [1], our results share the local (i.e., mouth and eyes areas) and global
(i.e., facial textures) identity information with the source faces much better
while keeping the attributes of the target unchanged. Besides, thanks to the
sufficient identity-related feature interaction and powerful face generator, our
method does not introduce inappropriate cues from the source and generates
more realistic results, i.e., fewer artifacts and higher sharpness.
