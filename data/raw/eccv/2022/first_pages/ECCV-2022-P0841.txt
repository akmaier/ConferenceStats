Rationality of Transformation for Concatenation Layers

To explain the rationality of transformation for concatenation layers, we can
prove the following proposition.
Proposition 1 The output of a concatenation layer followed by a convolution
layer is equivalent to the sum of separate convolutions on the inputs.
Suppose we have M features Xm ∈ RN ×Cm ×H×W , m ∈ [1, M ]. By concatenatingPtheses features along channel dimension, we can obtain aPnew feature X ∈
M

RN ×( m=1 Cm )×H×W . A convolution with weights θ ∈ RCo ×( m=1 Cm )×K×K is
then applied on the concatenated feature X. Im2col operation transfers convolution to matrix multiplication,
PM and we get the input and weight matrix X̃, θ̃, as
shown in Fig. 1. X̃ · θ̃ = m=1 X̃m · θ̃m , which is the sum of M multiplications
of small matrix, where X̃m · θ̃m is exactly the convolution on input feature Xm .
Consequently, Proposition 1 is proved.
