In order to assess the performance of FedLTN and other FL algorithms on a small
neural network (feasible to be deployed on resource-constrained edge devices) we
use a custom-built CNN with the architecture shown in Figure A.1. It has two
convolutional layers with max-pooling followed by three fully-connected layers.
Consequently, the model size is a fraction of ResNet18, with the custom CNN’s
62,006 parameters only taking up 240KB of memory.
