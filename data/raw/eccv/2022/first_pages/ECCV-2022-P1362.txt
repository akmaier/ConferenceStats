Comparison with point-based baselines As an additional ablation we compare
out method with point-based state-of-the-art segmentation models capable of
processing complete ScanNet scenes at once. We choose RandLa-Net [22] and
SCF-Net [14] as baselines and use the official authors implementation and hyperparameters for both methods. Our method outperforms both approaches with
our language-guided pretraining on this challenging large-vocabulary task. The

Effect of contrastive distance metric. Table 5 additionally considers alternative
distance metrics of ℓ1 and ℓ2 in comparison with our used cosine distance metric.
