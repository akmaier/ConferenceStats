SH Environment Estimation

Recall that our method represents the target illumination through SH coefficients. For experiments in Sec. 5 we estimate the SH coefficients from a 360
environment photo using least squares, inline with Yu et al. [2]. Here, the 360
environment maps allows us to perform numerical evaluations against ground
truth (see Sec. 5). We also show in Sec. 5 interactive relighting application where
the SH coefficients are directly controlled by the user. Furthermore, the supplemental video (at 3:31-3:43) shows results under novel lighting using externally
defined SH coefficients.
