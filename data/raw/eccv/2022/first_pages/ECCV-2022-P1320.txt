Derivation for silhouette matching

Given a known 3D shape and the current pose parameter λ to be optimized, we
denote the 2D boundary of the silhouette as Ĉ. A convenient way to derive the
gradient of the energy with respect to the pose parameter is to divide integral
region into two parts, one Ri inside Ĉ and the other Ro outside it. We may then
rewrite the energy as follows,
  \label {eq:region_formula} E(\lambda ) = \int _{R^i}(1 - B)^2d\mbf {x} + \int _{R^o}B^2d\mbf {x}

(1)

Note the added square evaluations here would not affect the result comparing
to the absolute evaluations as the quantities are either 0 or 1. We can further
simplify the equation (1) as
  E(\lambda ) &= \int _{R^i}(1 - B)^2d\mbf {x} + \int _\Omega B^2d\mbf {x} - \int _{R^i} B^2d\mbf {x} \notag \\ &= \int _{R_i}(1 - 2B)d\mbf {x} + \int _\Omega B^2d\mbf {x} \label {eq:simplified_region_f}
(2)
Following equation (4) in the reference [1] and changing the order by the triplet
product formula, we can reach to equation (7) in the main paper.
