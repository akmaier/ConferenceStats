How to Reduce the Noise Level of a Dataset Two approaches are commonly used
to reduce the noise level of a dataset: re-weighting samples and label correction.
Sample re-weighting reduces the noise level by eliminating noisy samples during
the model training, whereas label correction directly cleans up the dataset. Recently, label correction methods have shown notable results compared to sample
re-weighting methods. [86, 105, 121, 67, 11, 58, 84] claim that re-weighting might
show sub-optimal performance by filtering out noisy samples, which might aid
in training feature extractors.
