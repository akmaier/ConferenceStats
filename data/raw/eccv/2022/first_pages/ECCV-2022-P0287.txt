Fig. 1. The Annotation Interface for OmniBenchmark. The meta information of
each concept consists of a tag, description, and reference images. Annotators determine
whether the unlabeled image conforms to the concept. The yes option will be marked
if the annotator provides a positive answer to the question above.

We illustrate the annotation interface for building OmniBenchmark in Fig. 1.
