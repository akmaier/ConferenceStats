S1

Visualization of Upsampled Features

We visualize the upsampled feature maps w.r.t. CARAFE and FADE in SegFormer. We select one checkpoint for every 100 iterations in the range from the
100-th to 3000-th iteration. We also highlight the 320-th, 330-th, 340-th, 350-th,
and 360-th iteration, because we observe fast variances of the feature maps during this period. We compute the average response along the channel dimension
and normalize it to [0, 255]. From Figures S2 and S1, we can see that the two upsampling operators have different behaviors: FADE first learns to delineate the
outlines of objects and then gradually fills the interior regions, while CARAFE
focuses on the interior initially and then spreads outside slowly. We think the
reason is that the gating mechanism is relatively simple and learns fast. By the
way, one can see that there is ‘checkerboard artifacts’ in the visualizations of
CARAFE due to the adoption of Pixel Shuffle.
