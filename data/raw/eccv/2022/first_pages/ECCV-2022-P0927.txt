Fig. 1. Our method can handle topologically varying scenes and estimate physical
motion for all points in the space. Topologically varying means that the topology of
the scene can change, such as a new person entering the scene in (a). All points in the
space are tracked, such as the ball in (b). Only the sequence of images to be analyzed
is used and no prior knowledge is required in our framework.
