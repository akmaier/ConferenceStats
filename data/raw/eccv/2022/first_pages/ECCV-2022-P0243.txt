In this supplementary material, we provide additional details and results to
the paper. In Sec. A, we first present the architecture details of some modules in our proposed LEDNet. Sec. B presents further analysis and discussions
on our proposed LEDNet network, consisting of more analysis and results on
CurveNLU, and loss function. In Sec. C, we provide more analysis on our data
synthesis pipeline and LOL-Blur dataset. Finally, more visual comparisons on
both LOL-Blur and real-world images are provided in Sec. D. In addition, we
present a video demo to show the effectiveness of proposed LEDNet for dealing
with real-world dark blurry videos.
