Details for Data Augmentations

In Sec. 4.3, we ablate the data augmentations in self-supervised learning. The
combinations of data augmentations we used are the same as the symmetric
version proposed in [3], including random cropping, resizing, horizontal flipping,
color jittering, gray scale converting, Gaussian blurring, and solarization. The
probability and parameters of the data augmentations are detailed in Tab. 1.
