Ablation Study

In this section we provide the ablation experiment results on the 7-Scenes
Dataset [35], the Cambridge Dataset [22] and the Oxford Robotcar Dataset [27],
respectively. As introduced in the main paper, in all the ablation studies, we use
GTCaR[removed-module] to denote the network without the specific module.
