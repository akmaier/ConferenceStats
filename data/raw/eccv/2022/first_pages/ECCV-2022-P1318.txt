B. Method
In the following, we provide more details about our proposed method, specifically
the Shape Integration Module which updates the shape grid Sti .
Shape Integration Module. The shape integration module takes as input
a depth map Dti from sensor i ∈ {0, 1} at time t ∈ N, with known camera
calibration Ptc→w ∈ SE(3) from camera to world space and intrinsics Kt ∈ R3×3
and performs a full perspective unprojection to attain a point cloud Xw in the
world coordinate space. Each 3D point xw ∈ Xw is computed by transforming
each pixel (u, v) of the depth map into the camera space xc according to (1),

  \textbf {x}_{c} = D_t^i(u, v)K_t^{-1}\begin {bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end {bmatrix} \label {eq:im_cam} 

(1)

and then into the world camera space according to (2).

  \textbf {x}_w = P^{c \rightarrow w}\begin {bmatrix} \textbf {x}_c \\ 1 \end {bmatrix} \label {eq:cam_world} 

(2)
