2.2

Data Sampling Rate

Data from four OptiTrack Prime Color cameras is captured at 1080p resolution
at 60 Hz, and 185x105 pressure images are collected from the Sensel Morph at
approximately 115 Hz. Due to the large size of the dataset, the data is subsampled to 15 Hz for all experiments.
