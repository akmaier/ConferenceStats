Abstract. In this supplementary materials, we provide the analysis for
the additivity property of output distortion. We show a mathematical
derivation for the additivity property by linearizing the output distortion
using Taylor series expansion.

Additivity of Output Distortion

The output distortion δ, caused by quantizing all weight channels and activation
layers, equals the sum of all output distortion due to the quantization of each
individual weight channel and activation layer
