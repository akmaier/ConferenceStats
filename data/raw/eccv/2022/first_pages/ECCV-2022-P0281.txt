Statistics of the RSBlur

Table 1 shows a statistical comparison with the RSBlur and other real-world blur
datasets. The proposed RSBlur dataset consists of 13,358 real blurred images,
which make the dataset the second largest real-world dataset. While the RealBlur [19] and BSD [25, 26] datasets consist of real blurred images and groundtruth sharp images, we provide real blurred images and sequences of nine sharp
images to enable analysis on the blur generation process between real blurred
and synthetic blurred images. In terms of image resolution, the RSBlur dataset
provides the largest resolution. We also report the estimated noise levels using
a single image noise estimation method [5] for comparing the amounts of noise
in the real-world blurred datasets.

and BSD [25, 26], their coverage is limited. The real-world blurred images in
the RSBlur dataset can also serve as an additional benchmark dataset that
complements the existing benchmark datasets in terms of coverage.
