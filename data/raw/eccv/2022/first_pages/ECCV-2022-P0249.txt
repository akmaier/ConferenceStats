Light network training weights

For the weights of the light network, we used the Comet Machine Learning3 tool
with a Bayesian optimization on the validation set. The weights for each one of
the losses are: ambient=0.95, ambient angular=0.23, depth=0.85, intensity=0.03,
intensity angular = 0.27, size=0.65, translation=0.33.

Broader impact

We recognize that any lighting estimation method for virtual object insertion
method can be employed for malicious use. By providing intuitive controls over
the estimated lighting representation, our paper enables the creation of more
realistic images, thereby making it more difficult to detect them. We condemn
misusing these technologies, and promote solutions such as digital authentication.
