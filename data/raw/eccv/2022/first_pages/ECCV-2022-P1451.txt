Training and inference algorithms

For clarity, we report the main pseudo-code algorithms characterizing delta distillation. More specifically, Alg. 1 illustrates the optimization of delta distillation
objective Ldd . Furthermore, Alg. 2 describes the inference schedule alternating
between teacher (providing representations) and student (updating them). We
however recall that the full optimization of a delta distillation model, described
in Sec. 3.3, also entails the optimization of the task objective Lt and of the
sparsity objective Ls , that we omit to avoid cluttering Alg. 1.
