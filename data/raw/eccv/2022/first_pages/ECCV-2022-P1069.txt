A.1

Different Local Training Epochs

Tuning the number of local epochs affects accuracy-communication trade-off for
most federated learning algorithms. Prior studies [32,51,60] attempt to reduce
the number of local training epochs to mitigate the disparity of local models. The
default number of local training epochs is set as E = 10 in the main manuscript.
We further test different numbers of local training epochs E = {1, 5} in Tab. 5.
When E is set as 1, we increase the total number of rounds to 500 to ensure
convergence. As is seen from Tab. 5, our method consistently improves the base
algorithms with various numbers of local training epochs.
