Details of Network Architecture

Our proposed hourglass attention network (HAN) is built upon the framework
of generative adversarial net. Specifically, the proposed HAN plays a role as a
generator in the framework, and we adopt a PatchGAN[8] with the spectral
normalization [5] network as our discriminator. We provide the details of the
architectures of HAN and the PatchGAN in Table 2 and Table 1, respectively.
Besides, “Resblock” and “Feed forward” of the hourglass attention structure
details are shown in Figure 1.
