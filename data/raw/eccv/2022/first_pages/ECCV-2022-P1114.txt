A

Overview

This document provides additional technical details and more visualization results. Concretely, in section B, we first elaborate our pipeline with detailed convolutions. Next, we define the metrics used in paper in section C. Then we
conduct additional experiments in section D. Finally, in section E, we provide
more visualizations of the final depth predictions on KITTI and NYUv2.

B

Detailed Pipeline

As illustrated in Fig. S1, we provide the detailed convolutions of each layer in
our repetitive image guided network. Fig. S2 demonstrates the detailed components of our repetitive guidance module (RG), including the efficient guidance
algorithm (EG) and adaptive fusion mechanism (AF).
