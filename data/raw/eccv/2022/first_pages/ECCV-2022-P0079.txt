Abstract. We present the ﬁrst method that automatically transfers
poses between stylized 3D characters without skeletal rigging. In contrast
to previous attempts to learn pose transformations on ﬁxed or topologyequivalent skeleton templates, our method focuses on a novel scenario to
handle skeleton-free characters with diverse shapes, topologies, and mesh
