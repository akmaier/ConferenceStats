Characters

We use 17 realistic RenderPeople 3D human models (commercially available) [2],
i.e., nine female and eight male. Table 9 summarizes the RenderPeople models
that we use for UnrealEgo. These models are rigged and skinned based on the
default 3D human skeleton of Unreal Engine [3] as shown in Fig. 9. Readers are
referred to RenderPeople web page [2] for more details on their human models.
