Outline

The following document is constructed in the following way. Section 2 describes how to access our model’s code, and Section 3 provides additional media,
namely figures that have not been included in the main paper due to page limitation, and a description of video files that are available at our project page1 . In
Section 4 we describe an improvement in the skeleton structure, and in Section 5
we detail the internals of our architecture. Section 6 thoroughly describes the
datasets and various data aspects of our work, and finally, Section 7 presents
technical details related to camera parameters.

Code

Our code, together with usage instructions, is available on our project page1 .
The reader is encouraged to run the code and witness the reproducibility of our
model.
