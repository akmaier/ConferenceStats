Comparison of SegPGD with other Segmentation Methods

We report the robust accuracy of adversarially trained (PGD3-AT) models under different attacks, namely, SegPGD, DAG and MLAttack. In DAG method,
we apply projected gradient descent as the underlying optimization method and
only focus on the correctly classified pixels. In MLAttack, three losses are considered for each input image, i.e., the segmentation loss in the output layer, the of
in the last layer of encoder and the MSE loss of features multiple Note that the
MSE loss is computed as the MSE between the features on the clean input and
the ones on current adversarial examples. For each of the three losses, the input
gradients are computed to update the input examples. For fair comparison, we
compare the segmentation methods with the same number of gradient propagation passes. As shown in Fig. 1, our SegPGD achieves better attack effectiveness
and converges faster than other segmentation methods.
