Anchor-Free Detection Head

We describe the details of our anchor-free detection head that works across views
and formats. The key is to abstract away from the specific views and formats,
and think about the individual elements. The elements are individual voxels /
pixels / pillars under the voxel / perspective / pillar view.
A.1

Training Phase

The detection head has two sequential jobs: finding the centers, and regressing
parameters from them.

elements, we use V (e) to represent its Cartesian coordinates, which can be either
