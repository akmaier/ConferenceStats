Potential Application

We envision that our proposed Category-Agnostic Pose Estimation (CAPE) may
make many positive broader impacts and have some potential applications, e.g.
pose data (pseudo-)labeling, keypoint-based object tracking, contour-based instance segmentation, and graph matching.

Fig. 1: Example of pose data labeling. Each row shows one support image with
manually annotated keypoint definition, and four query images with pseudolabeled poses predicted by our CAPE model. Our CAPE model generates pose
predictions in accordance with the annotations of the support images and fits in
all kinds of keypoint definitions.
