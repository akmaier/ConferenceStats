Pseudosketches Dataset

To build the Pseudosketches dataset, we start with the 12.5K images from
Sketchy DB [6], and sample additional images for each category (or similar categories based on WordNet distances to the original category) from ImageNet
[1]. After running the automated pseudosketch-extraction pipeline on all gathered images, we display the pseudosketch-image pair and its class label, and ask
Amazon Mechanical Turk (AMT) workers to rate how well the pseudosketch represents its corresponding image on a scale of 1 (worst) to 5 (best). We visualize
paired examples for each AMT score in Fig. 1.
