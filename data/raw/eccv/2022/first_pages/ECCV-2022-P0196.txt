Inertia Matrix. Following Featherstone et al. [1], the inertia matrix M is derived as:
  \text {M} = \sum _i^{N_j} m_i J_{v_i}^{\mathsf {T}} J_{v_i} + J_{\omega _i}^{\mathsf {T}} I_{c_i} J_{\omega _i}, 

(1)

where Ici denotes the inertia tensor of the i-th body joint and Jωi ∈ R3×(3Nj +6)
denotes the angular Jacobian matrix that relates angular velocity to pose velocity. Jωi can be computed recursively:
  J_{\omega _i} = J_{\omega _j} + J_{\omega _{j \rightarrow i}}, 

(2)

where j = P(i) is the parent index of the i-th joint and Jωj→i represents the relative angular Jacobian matrix that can be computed from q. The linear Jacobian
matrix of the i-th joint Jvi can be computed based on Jωi :
  J_{v_i} = J_{v_j} - [\Delta r_i]_{\times } J_{\omega _i}, 
where [∆ri ]× is the skew-symmetric matrix of the body-part vector ∆ri .
