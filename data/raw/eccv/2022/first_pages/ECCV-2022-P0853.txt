Proof of the uniqueness of the COMBO decomposition

We recall here the COMBO decomposition of the ground truth flow vector w∗ (x)
between a physical part wp∗ (x) fulfilling the Brightness Consistency (BC) assumption, an augmented term wa∗ (x), and a BC uncertainty term α∗ (x):
  \w ^*(\x )= (1-\alpha ^*(\x )) ~ \w _p^*(\x ) + \alpha ^*(\x ) ~ \w _a^*(\x ). \label {eq:decomposup} 

(1)

Since the decomposition in Eq. (1) is not necessarily unique, the COMBO
decomposition (wp∗ , wa∗ , α∗ ) is defined as the solution of the following constrained
optimization problem:
  \underset {\w _p, \w _a}{\min } ~~~ \left \Vert (\w _a,\w _p) \right \Vert ~~~~~~~ \text {subject to: ~~~~~~} \label {eq:optpbsup} \\ \begin {cases} (1-\alpha ^*(\x )) ~ \w _p(\x ) + \alpha (\x ) ~ \w _a(\x ) = \w ^*(\x ) \\ (1-\alpha ^*(\x ))~ | I_1(\x ) - I_2(\x +\w _p(\x )) | = 0 \nonumber \\ \alpha ^*(\x ) = \sigma \left ( |I_1(\x )-I_2(\x +\w ^*(\x ))| \right ). \\ \end {cases}

We detail here the uniqueness guarantee of the COMBO decomposition in
Eq. (2). An unconstrained decomposition would be written as follows:
  \w (\mathbf {x}) = (1-\alpha (\mathbf {x})) ~ \w _p(\mathbf {x}) + \alpha (\mathbf {x}) ~ \w _a(\x ). \label {eq:decomp_naive} 

(3)

It is clear that the naive decomposition in Eq. (3) admits multiple (wp (x), wa (x), α(x))
tuples. We highlight here the effect of the different constraints in Eq. (2) :
– α∗ (x) = σ(LBC (x, w)) specifies a unique value for α∗ (x), but their remains
an infinite number of (wp (x), wa (x)) tuples.
– (1 − α(x)) |I1 (x) − I2 (x + wp (x))| = 0 specifies a set of BC minimizers for
wp , that we denote FBC .
– By minimizing ||wp ||, we limit wp (x) ∈ FBC to obtain wp (x) ∈ FBC ∩ Cp ,
where Cp is the circle of radius minwp′ ||wp′ ||2 (orange in Fig. 1).
