A

Visualization Video

We include a video here to provide more comprehensive visualizations of 3D
human motion prediction. These visualizations show that indeed our approach
produces more diverse sequences, which we attribute to our STARS strategy,
where anchors can explicitly locate diverse modes. We further show the visualization of controllable motion prediction and illustrate the motion variation at
both spatial and temporal levels, suggesting our novel manipulation of future
motion in the native space and time.
