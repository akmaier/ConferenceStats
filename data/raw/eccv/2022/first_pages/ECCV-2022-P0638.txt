1

Position Embedding

We concatenate positional embedding to Ftgt and Fref before computing their
attention scores:
  F^{i,pos}_{tgt}=[F^i_{tgt},{\rm pos_i}],F^{i,pos}_{ref}=[F^i_{ref},{\rm pos_i}], 

(1)

where 0 ≤ i < M . The position embedding for the coarsest scale posM −1 is
a learnable tensor with the same spatial dimension as F M −1 . For upper levels
(0 ≤ i < M − 1), position encoding posi is therefore generated with learnable
upsample-convolution-nonlinear blocks based on posi+1 :
  {\rm pos_{i}}={\rm LReLU}({\rm Conv}({\rm Up}({\rm pos_{i+1}}))), 

(2)

where LReLU denotes leaky ReLU activation function.
