The key hyperparameters are listed in Table 1. We use the same hyperparameters
for our unsupervised meta-learning tasks. Notice that on Omniglot, we use the
5-ways-1-shot and 20-ways-1-shot meta-tasks for training, and then we test these
models on four meta-tasks. On Mini-Imagenet [9] and Tiered-Imagenet [10], we
only train the meta-model using 5-ways-1-shot task. The model architecture is
the same as the 4-block convolutional network used by [4] for their Omniglot
experiments. We denote this architecture as 4-conv. We use 4-conv as the base
model for all experiments in this paper. The outer optimizer is Adam [6], and
the inner optimizer is SGD.
