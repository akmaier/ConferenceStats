we report quantitative performance with respect to different levels of four variation factors (viewpoint, scale, truncation, and occlusion) on SPair-71k benchmark [1], as summarized in Table S1. Large PCK gains for all levels of image
pairs indicate the robustness and effectiveness of our method.
