2

Comparison with LoRA [1]

LoRA, a closely related work, shows that formalizing the weight changing as
a low-rank matrix can also improve the fine-tuning performance. Therefore, we
compare with Align+LoRA to verify the effectiveness of the proposed Decomposition method. As illustrated at Table 1, by applying the LoRA with Align,
the performance could improve by 0.4%. However, it is still weaker than the
proposed DnA with an obvious margin of 0.5%.
