A

More Detailed Benchmark Construction Steps

A.1

COCO-EE

We built COCO-EE based on MSCOCO [7], which contains 123,287 images,
and 5 ground-truth captions for each image. To ensure criteria 1, we selected all
Ref-Caps and GT-Caps in COCO-EE from MSCOCO captions. Specifically, we
constructed each editing instance following these steps:
1. Image-Caption Similarity Filter. To guarantee criteria 2, for each image labeled with 5 captions, we used a pre-trained CLIP [12] model to filter
300 captions from all the rest captions in its respective split set ( training/validation/test) based on the CLIP score, where a higher score indicates
higher similarity between the image and the caption. Meanwhile, to save the
computation cost in the rest filtering steps, we then randomly selected 30
captions from them as Ref-Cap candidates, and we treated all the 5 groundtruth captions as the GT-Cap candidates.
