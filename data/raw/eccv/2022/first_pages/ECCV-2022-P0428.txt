Explanation to empirical observation

We find that the Zipf’s prior could help generate non-uniform supervision for
non-target classes in a one-pass way. In this section, we provide a simple intuition to explain why Zipf’s law should occur for predictions from multi-class
classification.
We postulate that one main source of the non-zero network predictions is the
inevitable non-orthogonality of the inter-class feature vectors as more and more
classes are packed into the finite-dimensional feature space. In a simplified model,
we assume that the decision vectors corresponding to each class are uniformly
distributed on a high-dimensional unit sphere. Then for another random query
vector on the sphere, their inner-products with it distribute in the shape of a
Gaussian when the dimension is high enough.
