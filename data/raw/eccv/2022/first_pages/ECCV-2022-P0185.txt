Related Work about Trojan Attacks

In this paper, we mainly consider a class of Trojan attacks in which attackers
fully control the training processes of a classifier. We refer to these attacks as
“full-control” attacks. There is another less common type of Trojan attacks
called “clean-label” attacks [37,41,55]. These attacks assume a scenario in which
people want to adapt a popular pretrained classifier C (e.g., ResNet [14]) for their
tasks by retraining the top layers of C with additional data collected from the
