In this document, we present additional experiments that provide further
insight and evaluations. Namely, in Sections 1 and 2, we quantitatively compare
with additional competing methods: three encoders pSp [7], e4e [9], ReStyle [2]
and two optimization-based methods Pivotal Tuning [8] and HyperStyle [3]. We
show more challenging examples and qualitative results of all tested methods.
Section 3 shows the automatic segmentation and corresponding images for both
cases of our method, with and without the regularization. Finally, in Section 4
we demonstrate additional examples of interactive image editing and application
of our method to the image interpolation task.
