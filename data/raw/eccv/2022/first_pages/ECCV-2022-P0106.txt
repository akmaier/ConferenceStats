Network Architecture

Fig. 1. Overview network architectures for classification (top) 3D shape part segmentation (middle), and scene semantic segmentation (bottom). Specifically, in the
encoders, ’Sampling’ denotes random downsampling, while in the decoders, it denotes
feature interpolation (point upsampling).

Fig. 1 shows our network for different tasks, where diffConv is performed
hierarchically to capture multi-scale point features and avoid redundant computation.
