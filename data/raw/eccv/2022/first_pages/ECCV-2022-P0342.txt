Data Efficiency

Although there is no strict definition of data efficiency, it has been studied under
various contexts [16,10,15,6]. As described in Section 1 of our main text, the
requirement of more training data brings two issues: (a) more human labors are
needed to collect and label enough training data; (b) more computational costs
are required to train the model. In this paper, we aim to alleviate both these
two issues of existing detection transformers. Thus, most of our experiments are
performed on small-size datasets and implemented with a short training schedule
of 50 epochs.
