A. More Dataset Details
• Cityscapes-Seq [2] is a widely used real dataset that contains 2,975 and
500 video sequences for training and evaluation, respectively. Specifically, each
sequence involves 30 consecutive frames with resolution of 1024 × 2048, while
only one frame among the sequence is fully annotated.
• SYNTHIA-Seq [6] consists of 8,000 simulated video frames with the resolution of 760 × 1280 and pixel-level annotations automatically produced by
game engine. Similar to [3], we evaluate on the 11 classes in common with the
Cityscapes-Seq.
• VIPER [5] contains 133,670 synthesized video frames with the resolution of
1080 × 1920. The full annotations in VIPER are available for all frames, which
are collected by a virtual moving object in diverse ambient conditions. Following
the setup of [3], we use the 15 classes in line with Cityscapes-Seq.
