In the derivation above, we make Markov assumption for the parameters θ,
which indicates that current ϕt is only relate to θt , having nothing to do with
earlier value of θt−1 . Therefore, we assume that ∇ϕ θt−1 ≈ 0 in the above derivation process, which simplifies the complexity of optimization. The assumption is
also proved to be reasonable and effective in our experiment results.
