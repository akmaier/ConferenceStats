In this section, we provide the complementary versions of Fig. 3, Fig. 4 from the
paper, i.e. we present the sources and targets from Fig. 3 manipulated with the
encoder, and the sources and targets from Fig. 4 manipulated with the optimizer.
Fig. 1 complements Fig. 3 from the paper, and Fig. 2 complements Fig. 4 from
the paper. The Dumbledore target in Fig. 1 is slightly different than the one used
for the optimizer since the target we used for the optimizer cannot be aligned,
which is a precondition for our encoder to process an image. As can be seen in
both Fig. 1 and Fig. 2, both our methods produce meaningful manipulations, and
successfully transfer notable semantic attributes from the targets to the various
sources, while preserving the identity of the source images.

Fig. 1: Examples of using our encoder with the same sources and targets as in
Fig. 3 in the main paper. The manipulations are consistent and induce the same
semantic manipulation for a wide range of diverse sources.
