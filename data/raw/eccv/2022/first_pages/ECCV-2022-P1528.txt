Background

In this section, we give a brief introduction to the original story visualization
task and auto-regressive transformers for text-to-image synthesis.
1.1

Story Visualization

Given a sequence of sentences S = [s1 , s2 , ..., sT ] forming a narrative, story visualization is the task of generating a corresponding sequence of images X̂ =
[x̂1 , x̂2 , ..., x̂T ], following [8]. The sentences form a coherent story with recurring
plot and characters. The generative model for this task has two main modules: story encoder and image generator. The sentence encoder Ecaption (.) takes
word embeddings {wik } for sentence sk at each timestep k and generates contextualized embeddings {cik }. These embeddings are then used to generate the
corresponding images. The terms caption and sentence are used interchangeably
throughout the paper.
