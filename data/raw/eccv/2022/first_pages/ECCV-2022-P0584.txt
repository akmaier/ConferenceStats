BRDF Implementation

We use the standard microfacet Bi-directional Reflectance Distribution Function [11] while introducing some approximations used in the BRDF implementations of the Blender [3] rendering engine. R(x, ω i , ω o , n(x)) is defined for the
3D location x, incident lighting direction ω i , outgoing reflectance direction ω o ,
and surface normal n(x)) as:

  R(\boldsymbol {x}, \boldsymbol {\omega }_i, \boldsymbol {\omega }_o, \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x})) = \frac {\boldsymbol {A}(\boldsymbol {x})}{\pi } + \frac {D(\boldsymbol {h}, \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x})), \gamma (\boldsymbol {x})) \cdot F(\boldsymbol {h},\boldsymbol {\omega }_i) \cdot G(\boldsymbol {h}, \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}), \boldsymbol {\omega }_o, \gamma (\boldsymbol {x}))}{4(\boldsymbol {\omega }_o \cdot \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}))(\boldsymbol {\omega }_i \cdot \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}))},

  D(\boldsymbol {h}, \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}), \gamma (\boldsymbol {x})) = \frac {\alpha ^2}{\pi ((\boldsymbol {h}\cdot \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}))^2(\alpha ^2 - 1)+1)^2}, 
  F(\boldsymbol {h}, \boldsymbol {\omega }_i) = F_0 + (1-F_0)(1-(\boldsymbol {h}\cdot \boldsymbol {\omega }_i))^5, 
  G(\boldsymbol {h}, \boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x}), \boldsymbol {\omega }_o, \gamma (\boldsymbol {x})) = \frac {\boldsymbol {h} \cdot \boldsymbol {\omega }_o}{\boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x})\cdot \boldsymbol {\omega }_o}\cdot \frac {2}{1+\sqrt {1+\alpha ^2 \tan \theta }}, 
  \alpha = \gamma ^2(\boldsymbol {x}), \qquad \boldsymbol {h} = \frac {\boldsymbol {\omega }_o + \boldsymbol {\omega }_i}{||\boldsymbol {\omega }_o + \boldsymbol {\omega }_i||}, \qquad \tan \theta = \frac {1- (\boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x})\cdot \boldsymbol {\omega }_o)^2}{(\boldsymbol {n}(\boldsymbol {x})\cdot \boldsymbol {\omega }_o)^2}, 
where A(x) is the diffuse map, γ(x) is the specular roughness. In our implementations, we set Fresnel coefficient F0 = 0.04.
