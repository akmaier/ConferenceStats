Object Filtering Details. We used category-based filtering for ReplicaCAD,
and AB datasets (e.g. sofa, bikes, etc) to remove unhelpful objects. Then, we
removed objects if any of their dimensions exceeded 50 meters. We also used
some manual filtering in order to remove very small objects (e.g. keychains).
