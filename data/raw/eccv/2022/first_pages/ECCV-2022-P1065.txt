A

DAM with Unconditional Latent Flow

To evaluate the effectiveness of our flow-based prior formulation, we run an
ablation by ignoring the LiP-encoders. To this end, we tie a standard Normal
prior N (0, I) to the DAM-encoder (Q) via an unconditional normalizing flow and
compare this to the DAM trained via KL-D (see Fig. 9). We follow the same
formulation with LiP-Flow except that we use a standard Normal prior instead
of a conditional learned prior and an unconditional normalizing flow model F̃ .
