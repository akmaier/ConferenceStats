Computing MOTCOM. We have evaluated four averaging methods for combining the sub-metrics into MOTCOM. The four methods are the arithmetic,
quadratic, geometric, and harmonic means and they are presented in Equation (1),
Equation (2),Equation (3), and Equation (4), respectively.

 \label {eq:arithmetic} \mathrm {arithmetic} = \frac {1}{n} \sum ^{n}_{i=1} m_i 

(1)

 \label {eq:quadratic} \mathrm {quadratic} = \sqrt {\frac {1}{n} \sum ^{n}_{i=1} m_i^2} 

(2)

 \label {eq:geometric} \mathrm {geometric} = \sqrt [n]{\prod ^{n}_{i=1} m_i} 

(3)

 \label {eq:harmonic} \mathrm {harmonic} = \frac {n}{\sum ^{n}_{i=1} \frac {1}{m_i}} 

(4)
