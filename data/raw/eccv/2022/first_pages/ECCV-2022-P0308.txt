Abstract. In this supplemental material, we introduce the derivation
of the formula of the normal vectors [⃗t, ⃗b, ⃗l, ⃗r] of the planes that the
neighboring sides of each RBFoV lie on. We also describe the details of
how to convert the planar rectangle to the spherical rectangle in the ERP
image. Finally, we describe the details of how to obtain the minimum
external bounding BFoV according to the RBFoV.

1

Normal Vector of RBFoV

We first need to obtain four normal vectors [⃗nt , ⃗nb , ⃗nl , ⃗nr ] of the planes that the
neighboring sides of each RBFoV (θ, ϕ, α, β, 0) lie on. As shown in Fig. 1(a), the
X ′ , Y ′ and Z ′ are the new axis of the coordinate system that we established
based on the azimuthal and polar angle (θ, ϕ) of the center point of the RBFoV
(θ, ϕ, α, β, 0).
 \label {equ:CELoss} \begin {split} \begin {cases} X' = [\sin (\phi ) \cdot \cos (\theta ), \sin (\phi ) \cdot \sin (\theta ), \cos (\phi )]^{\top } \\ Y' = [-\sin (\theta ), \cos (\theta ), 0]^{\top } \\ Z' = [-\cos (\phi ) \cdot \cos (\theta ), -\cos (\phi ) \cdot \sin (\theta ), \sin (\phi )]^{\top } \end {cases} \end {split}
