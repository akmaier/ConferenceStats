1.1

Deployment to FasterRCNN

The deployment includes two steps, the first step is to construct object bags and
the second step is to apply OA-MIL on FasterRCNN. Fig. 1 depicts the pipeline
of our method on FasterRCNN.
Bag Construction. We construct object bags based on the outputs of the
second stage of FasterRCNN. Specifically, we treat each inaccurately annotated
object as a positive bag, where instances are positive anchors (object proposals)
corresponding to a specific object. In addition, we treat each negative anchor as
a negative bag.
