B

Experimental Setup

For target images, we use the CIFAR10 [8], CelebA [9], Flicker-Face-HQ (FFHQ) [6],
and ImageNet [2] datasets. We use the validation split of the datasets or the test
split only if the validation split is not provided. For evaluation of generative models, we generate images using the pre-trained models as follows: DCGAN [10]
trained on the CIFAR10 dataset, ProGAN [5] trained on the CelebA dataset,
BigGAN-deep-256 [1], ADM [3], E-VDVAE [4], trained on the ImageNet dataset,
and StyleGAN [6], StyleGAN2 [7], and E-VDVAE [4] trained on the FFHQ
dataset.
