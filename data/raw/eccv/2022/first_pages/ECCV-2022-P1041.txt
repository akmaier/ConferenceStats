A.2

DKT

Next, we adapt Deep Knowledge Tracing (DKT) [5] to our setting. We deviate
from the original DKT method in two main ways. First, the types of queries (e.g.
math problems) in educational datasets do not allow for instance-level representations. Instead, skills (i.e. question types) were jointly encoded with information
about whether the problem was answered correctly by the learner. Second, the
output of DKT was the learner’s probability of being correct for each skill, not
a particular question instance.
