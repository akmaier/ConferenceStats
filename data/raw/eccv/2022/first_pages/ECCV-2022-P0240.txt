consisting of four 27” LCD monitors mounted in portrait mode. (c): Frontal

In Fig. 1, we present schematics of the two proposed desktop capture systems
– the first one employing portable mobile devices (tablets and smartphones), and
the second one employing larger LCD monitors for piece-wise continuous illumination on a subject’s face. The displays are arranged to illuminate a subject’s
face from a frontal hemispherical zone of directions (Fig. 1), spanning β < ±90◦
along the longitudinal direction, while spanning α < ±45◦ along the latitudinal
direction.
