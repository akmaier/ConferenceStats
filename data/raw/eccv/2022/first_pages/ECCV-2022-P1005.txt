Baselines and default settings

The main task that we consider in this paper for the evaluation of our training
procedure is image classification. We train on Imagenet1k-train and evaluate
on Imagenet1k-val, with results on ImageNet-V2 to control overfitting. We also
consider the case where we can pretrain on ImageNet-21k, Finally, we report
transfer learning results on 6 different datasets/benchmarks.
