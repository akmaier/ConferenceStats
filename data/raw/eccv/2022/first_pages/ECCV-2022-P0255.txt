Comparison to methods using images or events.

In this section, we give more comparative results with the depth estimation
methods using images and events. We compare with an existing depth estimation method, RAM-Net [1], which uses the modalities of image and event
from “DENSE” dataset. We train our Spike-T using spikes from the synthetic
“DENSE-spike” dataset. As reported in Tab. 2, our Spike-T achieves comparable
performance with RAM-Net [1] trained on event and image.
