MPII [1] dataset is a popular dataset for evaluating pose estimation models.
It contains 40k person samples, each labeled with 16 joints. We followed the
standard train/val/test split as in [3] and use PCKh@0.5 for evaluation. The
training settings are the same as we use for the MS-COCO dataset. Table 1 shows
the performance improvement on the MPII dataset, where HRNet-W32 and
HRNet-W48 with the input size of 256 × 192 are adopted as the baselines. From
the table, we can observe that PoseTrans consistently boosts the performance of
the baselines.
