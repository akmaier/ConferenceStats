In this supplementary material, we give further qualitative results for our
Progressive Transformer SLP model. We share snapshot examples of produced
sign pose sequences in Figures 1 and 2, showing (a) input spoken language
alongside (b) produced sign pose sequence, with (c) ground truth pose and (d)
original video provided for comparison.
und nun die wettervorhersage für morgen sonntag den sechsten september
(trans: and now the weather forecast for tomorrow friday the 21st of may)

mittwoch und donnerstag wechselhaft hier und da fällt etwas regen oder schnee der zunehmend auch in den niederungen möglich ist
(trans: Wednesdays and Thursdays changeable here and there a little rain or snow that is increasingly possible in the lowlands)
