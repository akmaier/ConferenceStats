CSM, shown in Fig. 1, senses the original image by gradually compact the image
size through a sequence of filters. Compared with conventional sensing strategies,
which sense the image block by block through a single shared weight matrix
multiplication, this new approach has the power to preserve the spatial features
thanks to the sparse local connectivity nature of convolution operations.
