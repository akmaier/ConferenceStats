A.2

Supplemental network architecture design

Conditional content generator Gcg . The overall architecture is shown in
Fig. 1 in which we visualize two stages. In practice, we implement Gcg to be threestage, which synthesizes 256 × 256 images. The implementation of the geometry
encoder Encg and texture encoder Enct is shown in Fig. 2. For the color encoder
Encc , we directly adopt the bidirectional LSTM based text encoder used in [25].
We also use the same training mechanism as in [25] for training Encc . The GAIN
ResBlk is implemented as in Fig. 3(a).
Gated Adaptive INstance (GAIN) normalization (Fig. 3(b)) is designed
based on AdaIN [10] (Fig. 3(c)) and SPADE [13]. We also visualize the implementation of SPADE w/ UniTex (Table 1) in Fig. 3(d).
Compositing model Gcc is implemented as in Fig. 4(a). We also show the
implementation of the image-to-image translation baseline Pix2pix in Fig. 4(b).
Discriminators are designed as in Fig. 5. Their functionalities in supervising
the generation model Gcg and the compositing model Gcc are presented in §A.3.
The comparison between the proposed classification based compositing discriminator Dcc (§4.3) and the segmentation based discriminator (§4.2) can be found
in Fig. 5(c) and (d). Their convergence comparison is presented in §A.3.
