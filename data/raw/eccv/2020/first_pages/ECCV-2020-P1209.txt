Model architectures and experimental setting

We summarize the experimental setting in Table 2.
For the MNIST dataset, we used the standard CNN architecture with three
convolution layers and three fully connected layers described in [2]. For the
CIFAR-10 dataset, we used two architectures in which one is the standard CNN
architecture described in [2] and another is the ResNet architecture used in [6].
The ResNet architecture has 5 residual units with (16, 16, 32, 64) filters each.
We choose the convolution layers as the Generator and the last fully connected
layers as the Classifier for ResNet architecture. The standard CNN architectures
are redescribed as follow:
CNN-4C3F(32) Generator: 2×Conv(32)→ Max Pooling→ 2×Conv(32)→
Max Pooling→ Flatten
CNN-4C3F(32) Classifier: FC(200)→ ReLU→ Dropout(0.5)→ FC(200)→
ReLU→ FC(10)→ Softmax
CNN-4C3F(64) Generator: 2×Conv(64)→ Max Pooling→ 2×Conv(64)→
Max Pooling→ Flatten
CNN-4C3F(64) Classifier: FC(256)→ ReLU→ Dropout(0.5)→ FC(256)→
ReLU→ FC(10)→ Softmax
