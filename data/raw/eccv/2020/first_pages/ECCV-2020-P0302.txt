t-SNE Visualizations

The representations learned using each method described in the paper are visualized here using t-SNE. Here, we consider adaptation scenario SVHN → MNIST,
i.e., labeled SVHN is used as source domain and MNIST as unlabeled target
domain. The Fig. 1 shows t-SNE plots for feature representation of known and
novel categories for MNIST. As we can see from the figure, Softmax, ALOCC and
GRL baselines have known and novel category features overlapped. Whereas, for
both ALOCC+GRL and Proposed approach, we can see that known and novel
category features are well separated.
