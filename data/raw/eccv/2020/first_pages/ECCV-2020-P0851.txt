and latter parts of each network correspond to the tentative reconstruction and
residual refinement, respectively. k is the kernel size. chns is the number of input/output channels. activ is the activation functions. input denotes the input
to each layer. “Images” means the images captured by a coded-aperture camera.
output corresponds to the reconstructed light field. Single and 2-S are the same
configurations as the counterparts used in Inagaki et al. [17].
