Experiment Details

The CorLoc measure used to evaluate the performance of di↵erent WSOL methods is
the ratio of correctly localized objects in each class and computes the mean over all
classes. A localization is correct if it has Intersection-over-Union (IoU) greater than
a threshold with the groundtruth object bounding box. We report with 0.5 and 0.7
thresholds in our experiments. All experiments are done on a single Nvidia GTX 1080
GPU and 3.2GHz Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU with 128 GB of RAM.
