Implementation Details

Network architecture Detailed architectures of pruning networks and matching networks are summarized in Table 1. For both networks, we employ an
encoder-decoder style architecture which has been adopted in many pixel-level
prediction tasks such as disparity estimation, optical flow, or semantic segmentation [3, 2, 10]. To deal with large geometric variations, both networks are designed
to incorporate the full set of pairwise scores by setting the kernel size of ‘conv1p ’
and ‘conv1m ’ along channel dimension to (h × w).
