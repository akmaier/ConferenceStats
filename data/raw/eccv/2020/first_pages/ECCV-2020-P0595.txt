I

Setting of [EOS] decay factor γ

We perform cross validation on MS COCO Caption Karpathy’s val split to
determine the value of γ. We set γ to different values for different levels in 4level LaBERT. Specifically, γ is set to 0.88 and 0.95 for the second and third
level of 4-level LaBERT, respectively. While for the first and the last level, we
find the model performs well without [EOS] decay, i.e., γ = 1.0.
