Quaternion Operations

A quaternion q = q0 + q1 i + q2 j + q3 k ∈ H is a hyper-complex number with a
real part (q0 ) and three imaginary parts (q1 i, q2 j, q3 k), where q0 , q1 , q2 , q3 ∈ R;
H denotes the algebra of quaternions. The products of basis elements i, j, and
k are defined by i2 = j 2 = k2 = ijk = −1 and ij = k, jk = i, ki = j, ji =
−k, kj = −i, and ik = −j.
Just like complex numbers, given two quaternions p = p0 + p1 i + p2 j + p3 k
and q = q0 + q1 i + q2 j + q3 k, a series of quaternion operations can be defined
as follows.
Addition: p + q = (p0 + q0 ) + (p1 + q1 )i + (p2 + q2 )j + (p3 + q3 )k.
Scalar multiplication: λq = λq0 + λq1 i + λq2 j + λq3 k.
Element multiplication: pq = (p0 q0 − p1 q1 − p2 q2 − p3 q3 ) + (p0 q1 + p1 q0 +
p2 q3 − p3 q2 )i + (p0 q2 − p1 q3 + p2 q0 + p3 q1 )j + (p0 q3 + p1 q2 − p2 q1 + p3 q0 )k.
