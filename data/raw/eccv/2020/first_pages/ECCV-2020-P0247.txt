Visualization

We visualized the activation map generated by our CIDC network and R3D-NL
network under 4 scenarios:
1. when there is no camera motion and slight action movement (Figure 1);
2. when there is slight camera motion and slight action movement (Figure 2);
3. when there is slight camera motion and significant action movement (Figure
3);
4. when there is significant camera motion and significant action movement
(Figure 4).
The results show that our proposed network is able to capture the activity related
features and ignore the irrelevant background information compared with R3DNL.
