The detailed structure of our model is given as follows. We use PCB(k, c, s, d) to denote a probabilistic contextual block (PCB) block, as illustrated in Figure 1(a). k, c,
s, and d denote kernel size, filter number, stride size, and dilation rate, respectively.
Similarly, residual block (RB) is defined as RB(k, c, s, d), which is given in Figure
1(b). Let DB(k, c, s) denote a convolution-LeakyReLU(0.2) layer, CB(k, c, s) denote
a convolution-ELU layer, Conv(k, c, s) denote a convolution layer, and ×4 denote
nearest-neighbor upsampling operator. Our visual consistency network consists of mask
prediction network (MPN) and region inpainting network (RIN).
