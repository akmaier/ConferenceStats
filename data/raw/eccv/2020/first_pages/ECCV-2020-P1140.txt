Further Analysis

In this section, we first analysis some detail settings of the proposed model. We then
use the proposed framework to extend some potential non-blind deblurring methods,
and we further discuss the effectiveness of the method which includes both the blind
and non-blind steps. At the end of this section, we give a possible extension of the
proposed non-blind method.
1.1

Running time comparisons

We further compare OID with other blind deblurring methods on blurry images with
different sizes in term of running times. As is shown in Table 1, OID performs favorably
against recent outlier handling methods [4, 14], and it also conducts one of the fastest
running time among recent optimization-based methods.
