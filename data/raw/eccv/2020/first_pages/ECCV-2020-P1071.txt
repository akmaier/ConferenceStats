Surface normal visualization

We provide more visualizations of surface normal prediction on the ScanNet [1]
dataset. In our implementation, we directly fit the surface normal from ground
truth depth annotation. Black pixels indicate invalid regions where no ground
truth depths are provided. Compared to MovingIndoor [3], our surface normal
estimation better preserves the boundary of the planar regions, thanks to our
superpixel constraint.
