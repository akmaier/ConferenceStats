Abstract. This document contains additional material concerning ECCV
2020 paper “Self-adaptive confidence estimation for stereo”. In order, we
first illustrate the color encoding used for both disparity and confidence
maps (Sec. 1), then reporting additional qualitative results concerning
the ablation experiments (Sec. 2), the generalization across datasets (Sec.
3) and the self-adaptation technique enabled by OTB (Sec. 4).
