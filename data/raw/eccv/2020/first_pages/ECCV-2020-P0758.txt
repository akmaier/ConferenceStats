Generator

We construct the noise generator by using six cascaded deconvolution layers,
finishing with tanh activation to map the noise map ∆ to the range of [−1, 1],
as shown in Fig. 1. “dec(11,11)” represents the deconvolutional layer that maps
Z to a feature map size of 11 × 11. “dec(2)” indicates a deconvolutional layer of
stride 2.
