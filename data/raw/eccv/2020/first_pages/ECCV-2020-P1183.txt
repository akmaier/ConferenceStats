Ideal sampling

In our context, ideal sampling of a manifold Σ ⊂ Rd is to find a dense enough point
cloud S such that a triangulation T of S could be constructed by interpolation such that
|T | is homeomorphic to Σ, and the Hausdorff distance between T and Σ is small. In fact,
in [2] the authors proved a theorem that by considering (, δ)-sampling, the weighted
Delaunay triangulation of point cloud faithfully recovers the manifold.
In our model, we assume that the input real image dataset is an ideal sampling of
real image manifold, and images are viewed as being i.i.d. uniformly sampled from the
image manifold.
