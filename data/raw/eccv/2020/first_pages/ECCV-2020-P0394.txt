Supplementary Material
Next, we present the Supplementary Material for the paper “Gabor Layers
Enhance Network Robustness”. In Section 6 we report the details for the choice of
the p parameter of the Gabor layers for the various experiments we present in the
paper; Section 7 shows the flip rates for all the experiments we reported; Section
8 presents the results for one experiment on ImageNet [40], both in terms of
adversarial accuracy and flip rate; in Section 9 we present several comparisons of
distributions of singular values of standard convolutional layers vs. Gabor layers;
finally, Section 10 depicts visualizations of the filters learned in the Gabor layers
when various architectures are trained on numerous datasets.
