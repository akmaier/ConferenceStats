Introduction

1. Cross-view examples and failure cases from the single-view pipeline
2. Application of our pipeline to other bird species

1

Cross-view examples and failure cases produced by the
single-view pipeline

Our single-view pipeline produces poses that are consistent across views (Table 2
in the main paper). In Figure S1 we present visual examples of meshes projected
onto views that were not used to obtain the mesh. The distributions of pose
and shape obtained from multi-view optimization provide a sufficient prior for
single-view pose estimation.
The keypoint detector sometimes fails completely for difficult poses (Figure S2, top row) or swaps left and right keypoints (bottom row), which cause a
faulty prediction by the pose regression network. Sixty percent of failure cases
were associated with bad keypoint detection. Even in cases with very few keypoints, however, the pipeline produces meshes that are consistent with the silhouette.
