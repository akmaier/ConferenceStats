Summary

This supplementary material is structured as follows: proofs of strong stability
preserving methods (section 2), proofs of variance analysis of SSP networks (section 3), comparison of non-TVD scheme and TVD scheme (section 4), reminder
of adversarial training (section 5), exploratory network analysis (section 6) and
suppression on perturbation growth (section 7).
