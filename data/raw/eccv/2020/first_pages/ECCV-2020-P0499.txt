Content

In this supplementary material we elaborate on the detailed structures of the networks used, including LSTU-Net, CooGAN and HR modified versions of STGAN
and AttGAN. We also give more generated images, including LR comparison results on facial attribute editing task, season transfer task and HR results of facial
attribute editing. The contents are given in the following sequence:
1. The comparison experiments and network structure of LSTU-Net. This section contains two sub-sections: 1) detailed network structure; 2) qualitative
comparisons of results on facial attribute editing tasks and season transfer
tasks.
2. The comparison experiments and network structure of complete CooGAN.
This part has two sub-sections: 1) details of network structure; 2) comparison
experiments and HR results demonstration.
Images are best viewed in color and zoomed in. Our source code will be made
available in https://github.com/XHChen0528/CooGAN.
