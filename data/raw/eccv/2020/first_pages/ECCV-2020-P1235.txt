Appendix A: Derivation of Dispersion Model

generation of spectral radiance as a sum of mass-spring oscillations driven by an
electromagnetic wave. This induces changes in the index of refraction, which governs the reflectance of the material with respect to light wavelength/frequency.
This is based on earlier work by Garbuny and by Spitzer et al. [5, 9].
