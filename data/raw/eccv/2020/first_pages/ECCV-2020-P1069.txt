Experiment on KITTI

We additionally provide results on KITTI datasets. To train our model, we follow
the settings described in the main paper. For the detailed description of training
procedure, please refer to Sec. 4 of the main paper.
KITTI dataset. KITTI [10] has a driving scene from the real world. This
dataset is provided in two sets: KITTI 2012 and KITTI 2015, and each has only
200 pairs of images with ground-truth flows. Thus, we train our model using
unlabeled images from the multi-view extension of KITTI without duplicated
images in the extended ones and the benchmark training/testing sets, following
the previous work [22].
Results on KITTI. Table ?? shows the results on KITTI 2012 and KITTI
2015. Similar to results on FlyingChairs and Sintel, using our similarity loss
can both improve EPE in a clear gap also on KITTI datasets. In KITTI 2012,
Fl measure increases by 0.29%, while ours improves Fl measure by 0.91% in
KITTI 2015. Additonally, we report EPEs from our base network. To train our
network on KITTI, we first fine-tune our FlyingChairs model with census loss
and occlusion handling, whose results are shown in the first row of the table.
We also implement our baseline and report the results in the third row, as well
as the results from the original paper [22] in the second row.
