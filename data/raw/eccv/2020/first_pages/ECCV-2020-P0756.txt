The results reported in our submission for non-uniform blur are only for 1%
additive white Gaussian noise. We show below (Table 1) for completness the
results for 1%, 3% and 5% noise levels. The experimental protocol and code
used in these experiments are exactly the same as in the main paper. The test
dataset is the non-uniform PASCAL benchmark presented in our submission.
The proposed (L)CHQS models embed 5 outer HQS iterations and 2 inner CPCR
iterations. The table shows that our method outperforms its competitors for all
noise levels, with, in particular, a very significant margin of about 0.9dB with
LCHQSF over HQS-CG and EPLL [9].
