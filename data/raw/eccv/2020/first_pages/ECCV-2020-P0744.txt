TRAcking of the DIstribution (TRADI) of weights of a neural
network

This section details the tracking of the covariance matrix of a shallow neural network
(see Section 3.2.1 of the main paper), gives the whole TRADI algorithm and explains
how its parameters are chosen.
1.1

Kalman Filtering

Bayesian recursive filtering [3] aims to estimate the state of a hidden Markov process,
observed through a state space equations system. Let {µk (0), ..., µk (t)} be this process,
where µk (t) is the state, and ωk (t) the observation. At time t the filtering equations in
the absence of a control model are:

µk (t) = ft (µk (t − 1))
(1)
ωk (t) = gt (µk (t))
The first equation is the state equation, with ft the transition function between times
t−1 and t and the second is the observation equation, with gt the measurement function.
Under the Markov assumptions, the probability of the current state given the immediately previous one is conditionally independent of the other earlier states, in addition,
the measurement at the kth timestep is only dependent on the current state. Moreover,
the Kalman model considers that these two equations are perturbed by white Gaussian
noises which are independent from each other. Finally, if ft and gt are linear operators,
than the Kalman filter is an optimal estimator.
