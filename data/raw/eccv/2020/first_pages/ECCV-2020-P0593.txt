Abstract. In this supplementary file, we first present our analysis on retouching operations (White-balancing, Saturation controlling, and Tone
mapping). Then, we conduct some experiments to support our analysis in
Section 2. Besides, we provide more visual results of our proposed CSRNet and other state-of-the-art methods. For ablation study, we explore
the condition network with different hyper-parameters. Finally, we show
more results on smooth transition between multiple styles and strength
control for image retouching.
