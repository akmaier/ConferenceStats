We curated a challenging dataset of 25 LTR (low temporal resolution) videos of very
016
complex fast dynamic scenes, as if recorded by a ‘slow’ video camera (30 fps with
017
full inter-frame exposure time). These videos contain complex scene dynamics, highly
018
non-rigid motions, severe motion-blurs and/or severe motion-aliasing. Fig. 1 (in this
019
document) shows a sample frame from each video, and Table 1 (in this document)
020
summarises the lengths and frame sizes of the HTR (high temporal resolution) ground021
truth videos.
022
The dataset was generated from 25 very different real videos recorded with high
023
speed (mostly 240 fps) consumer cameras. Some of these videos were downloaded from
024
the web, and some taken from the Need-for-Speed [4] benchmark. The LTR videos
025
were generated from these HTR videos by blurring and sub-sampling them in time
026
by a factor of 8 (by averaging every 8 frames). This is equivalent to a ‘slower’ video
027
camera recording the same dynamic scene, with full inter-frame exposure-time, at 1/8
028
framerate (30 fps). These LTR videos were fed as inputs to the different algorithms.
029
We further split our dataset into two groups: (i) 13 extremely challenging videos,
030
not only with severe motion blur, but also with severe motion aliasing and/or complex
031
highly non-rigid motions (e.g., splashing water, flickering fire, etc.); (ii) 12 less chal032
lenging videos, still with sever motion blur, but mostly rigid motions. This was done in
033
order to highlight the type of videos that benefit the most from Internal-Learning.
034
Table 2 (in this document) lists the average per-frame PSNR, structural similarity
035
(SSIM), and a perceptual measure (LPIPS [5]), computed for each method on each
036
video. To avoid boundary effects we did not include the first and last 30 frames of each
037
sequence. We also disregarded a 20-pixel boundary around each frame when computing
038
per-frame PSNR. This wide masking of the boundaries was done to accommodate large
039
margin that some of the other algorithms require.
