We provide in this document more experimental results and details of the
proposed method. Specifically, we show the structure of the customized networks,
the IDs of images used in the main manuscript, the top-5 predictions of the
composite image, the evaluation of the pointing game, and more visualization
results on the ImageNet dataset with a pre-trained VGG-16 model, We provide
figures of the sensitivity analysis to give a better illustration on the influence of
the hyper-parameters in our proposed method. We also provide a video to show
the learning process of attribution maps.
