Analysis of norm

To further analyze the difference between the proposed SEN dissimilarity measure and the Prototypical Network with ring loss (Ring PN), we trained a Ring
PN with target norm R = {113 (as found by SEN PN on the training set), 50,
100,...,350} on the FC100 dataset and with R = {107 (as found by SEN PN
on the training set), 50, 100,...,350} on Mini-Imagenet. Experiments were performed for Ring PN, both with the Euclidean distance and the cosine distance.
