Overview

This document is to provide a supplementary description of the main paper, especially concerning the quantitative and qualitative evaluation of our experimental
results in Sections 4.4 and 4.5. Additionally, visual materials such as trajectory
figures and maps illustrate the results for comparative analysis among the tested
datasets or applied methods.
