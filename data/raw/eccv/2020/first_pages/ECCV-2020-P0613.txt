Fig. 1. Ranked retreival results. The query attributes are shown above the retrieved
images. The green/red border represents correct/wrong selections respectively. The
attributes in bold correspond to false matches.

Fig. 1 visualizes the ranked results from Embed, Embed+adv, Embed+symb-adv
and SAL to qualitatively illustrate the performance of the three models. Although the models are able to pick out some correct images from candidates
in the top 10 ranks, SAL selected more with higher ranks. Checking the wrong
attributes of the false ranks, we can see that SAL was better able to discern
fine-grained attributes, such as different kinds of bags. Moreover, SAL picked
correct images of diverse appearances, which may indicate it has some ability to
overcome intra-class variation problems.
