(e) Model Configuration

Fig. S-1. The architecture details of our base model. Due to the anisotropy nature
of the electron microscopy (EM) image stacks, the resolution of the voxels along the
z-axis is about 4× lower than x, y resolution. Therefore we use 2D residual blocks at
the first two stages in the network while putting stacked 3D residual blocks for the
following stages after pooling modules make the voxels roughly isotropic. To ease the
computational burden of 3D convolutions, we fix the maximum number of filters at a
convolutional layer to be 256 (e.g., the 5th block).
