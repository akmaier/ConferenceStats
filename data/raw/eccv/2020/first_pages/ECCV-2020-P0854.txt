Proof of Proposition 1
Proposition 1. Suppose N1 (µ1 , Σ1 ) and N2 (µ2 , Σ2 ) are two Gaussian approximations of spherical distributions. If they are spherical-homoscedastic, then the
rotation matrix between them is spanned by µ1 and µ2 .
Proof. From the Definition 2, the rotation matrix from N1 to N2 is A and is
spanned by µ1 and one eigenvector v1 of Σ1 . Analogously, N2 is also sphericalhomoscedastic with N1 and the rotation matrix from N2 to N1 is A−1 , which is
spanned by µ2 and one eigenvector v2 of Σ2 . As A is orthogonal and the subspace for the rotation transform and its reverse is identical, the subspace spanned
by {µ1 , v1 } and the subspace spanned by {µ2 , v2 } are exactly the same. As µ1
and µ2 are linearly independent, so the subspace is also spanned by {µ1 , µ2 }. □
