A Overview
In this supplementary material, we first provide implementation details and more
ablation studies about LFE and RP design in Sec. B and Sec. C, respectively. In
Sec. D, the user study is conducted to further evaluate the visual quality of the
generated HR image. At last, we provide visualizations of feature searching, the
element in the reference pool, and the SR reconstruction results (4×) obtained by
the SRCNN [1], MDSR [4], SRGAN [3], SRNTT [9] and our proposed CIMR-SR
with a 4 upscaling factor in Sec. E.
