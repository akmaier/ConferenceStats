A.1

Cyclic Verification

As said in Section 4.2, we not only filter out correspondences using Eq. (4) (in the
submitted version of the paper) but we also remove correspondences which do not
pass the cyclic check of matching back on their source pixel. This is equivalent to
performing a mutual nearest-neighbor verification as it is done with D2-Net [5]
