A. Network architecture
The detailed architecture of backbone network is shown in Table 1. Backbone network has the
same structure with Resnet50 before Block2. In Block3 and Block4, we replace the down-sample
operation with a series of dilated convolutions. The dilations are set to 2 and 4 respectively in Block3
and Block4. Table 2 presents the detailed architecture of target location network. It is a fully
convolutional network for calculating similarity and locating the target.
