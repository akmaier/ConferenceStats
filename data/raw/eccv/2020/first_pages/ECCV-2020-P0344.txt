Objective functions

Our objective functions contain five losses: an image GAN loss, a video GAN
loss, a perceptual loss, a flow-warping loss, and a world-consistency loss. Except
for the world-consistency loss, the others are inherited from the vid2vid [5]. Note
that we replace the least square losses used in the vid2vid for GAN losses with
the hinge losses as used in SPADE [3]. We describe these terms in details in the
following.
