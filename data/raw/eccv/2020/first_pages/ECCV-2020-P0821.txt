Multi-level Prediction

We use five FPN pyramids to segment objects of different scales (Table 1).
Scales of ground-truth masks are explicitly used to assign them to the levels of
the pyramid. Multi-level prediction gives 6.8 AP gains on the single-scale SOLO.
