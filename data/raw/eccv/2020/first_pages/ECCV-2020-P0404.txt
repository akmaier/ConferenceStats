Architecture details

010

In our ‘encoder-decoder ’ architecture (same for both subnets) 11 layers are used
with an latent space of dimensions M B × 2 × 2 × 1024, where M B stands for
mini-batch size. The architecture is given in Table 1. As it can be seen in this
table, an extra layer with linear activation (colored in yellow) is added to the
original decoder in order to get the final image output. In the case of RGB
image denoising the output image has 3 channels, whereas RAW image denoising
outputs 4 channels (R-G-G-B).
