Here we provide a more detailed explanation of methods introduced in this
work and provide additional qualitative results demonstrating the efficacy of our
proposed model. In Section A we discuss the details of Semantic Image Retrieval
as mentioned in Section 6. In Section B we provide the implementation details
of our baseline model (ISL) and proposed model (JSL). In Section C we discuss
the model changes we make to JSL in order to create the Conditional Situation
Localizer as discussed in Section 6. Finally, in Section E we provide qualitative
results comparing the localization of ISL and JSL as well as qualitative results
visualizing the situations generated for the top-5 verbs predicted by JSL.
