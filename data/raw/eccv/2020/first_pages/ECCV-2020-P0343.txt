Registration Results of T-ICP

In this section, we demonstrate several registration results by our Texture constrained ICP (T-ICP) in Fig. 1, which utilizes the face texture, from both the
RGB-D image and the face model, as a constraint during closest point matching.
The T-ICP not only completes the depth image to a full 3D face but also gets
the topology-uniformed 3D geometry. The registration results are further used
in out-of-plane pose augmentation in the next section.

Fig. 1. The registration results of T-ICP from FRGC [4], BP4D [6] and CASIA-3D [3].
