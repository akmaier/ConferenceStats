Annotation Details

In this section, we describe additional details of the annotation process, including
the design of the verb and noun classes, as well as the verb patterns.

verbs for annotation based on the previous dataset that captures goal-directed
activities in the kitchen [5,3,1] and living room [8,6]. Specifically, we start from
the action verb vocabulary summarized in the EPIC-KITCHENS dataset [1]
