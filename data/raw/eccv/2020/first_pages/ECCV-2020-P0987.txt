Architecture Details

In the following we summarize the model architecture we used to train on
AMASS [4], SMAL [9], COMA [5] and MANO [6]. Our model consists of a
shape branch, a pose branch and a decoder. Each model component is a 4-layer
neural network with alternated spiral convolution layers and sampling layers. The
number of filters for convolutional layers and the sampling rates for sampling
layers are listed in Table 1.

Shape Space Exploration

of the same subject are consistent under swapping. We believe that imposing
the loss in mesh space gives the network more flexibility to choose the optimal
structure of latent space. It remains an interesting question how far are these
