Additional experimental details

Penn Action Dataset provides the training and testing splits. We further split
the training data into 90 percent training data and 10 percent validation data
for each action class. We use the validation set to tune the hyperparameters. We
used a weight decay of 0.00001 with the Adam optimizer. We also observed that
batch-normalizing the activations of the added linear layers improves performance
both qualitatively and quantitatively.
