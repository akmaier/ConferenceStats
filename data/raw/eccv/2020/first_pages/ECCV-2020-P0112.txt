This section gives the architectural details of the MemDPC components, including
the encoder f (.) and temporal aggregator g(.).
Architecture of encoder f (.). The detailed architecture of the encoder function f (.) is shown in Table S1. The size of the convolutional kernel is denoted by
[temporal × spatial2 , channel]. The strides are denoted by [temporal, spatial2 ].
We assume the input is 8 video blocks, 5 frames per video block, and the frame
has a resolution of 128 × 128. The column of ‘output size’ shows the tensor dimension after the current stage. Some pooling layers are omitted for clarity. We
will release all the source code after the paper decision.
Architecture of temporal aggregator g(.). The detailed architecture of the
temporal aggregation function g(.) is shown in Table S2. It aggregates the feature
maps over the past T time steps. Table S2 shows the case where g(.) aggregates
the feature maps over the past 5 steps. We use the same convention as above
to denote convolutional kernel size. The temporal aggregator is an one-layer
ConvGRU that returns context feature with same number of channels as its
input.
