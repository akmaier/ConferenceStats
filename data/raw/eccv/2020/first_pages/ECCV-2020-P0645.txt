Adversarial Example Visualization

Some adversarial ranking examples are presented in this section. Every figure
contains three rows of pictures. The first row shows c, r, c̃ = c + r for CA, or q,
r, q̃ = q + r for QA. The second row shows the query and the original retrieval
results, as well as the chosen candidate c and its immediately adjacent candidates. The third row shows the effects of the attack on the ranking list, i.e.,
either the chosen candidate c is replaced with c̃ for CA, or the query q is replaced
with q̃ for QA. The digits above every picture is the value of RankX (q, ·) − 1 1 .
Pictures with a “ F ” mark on the top-left corner are adversarial examples.
The “←” indicates the chosen candidate whose rank will be raised. The “→”
indicates the chosen candidate whose rank will be lowered.
