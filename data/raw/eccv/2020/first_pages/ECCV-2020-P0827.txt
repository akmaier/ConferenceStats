Theoretical Analysis

The core of gcp-sampling is to adaptively sample tasks during meta-training.
Hence, in this section, we theoretically analyze the advance of such a sampling
method in terms of generalization bound. We first provide a generic generalization bound for task sampling. Then, we connect the generalization bound to the
proposed task adaptive sampling (cp-sampling and gcp-sampling).
