Subdivision Surface Implementation Details

For each face in the mesh, we construct 16 control points for the limit surface
and 12 control points for two tangent vector fields. We denote the extraction of
the control points by the very sparse matrices Lf ∈ RN ×16 and L0f , L00f ∈ RN ×12
for all faces 1 ≤ f ≤ F . These matrices only depend on the mesh connectivity
and for a detailed review of their structure, we refer to [5]. U ∈ RN ×3 denotes the
matrix representation of T 0 where the columns contain the x,y and z coordinates
of T 0 . The respective limit control points are computed via
η (f ) = U T Lf ,

υ (f ) = U T L0f ,

ξ (f ) = U T L00f .
