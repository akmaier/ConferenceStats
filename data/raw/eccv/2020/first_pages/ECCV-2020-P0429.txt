Abstract. In this supplementary material, we visualize the curve fitting
results used to optimize the threshold values and provide the parameters
and commands used to achieve the paper results.

1

Curve Fit

To solve the optimization problem presented in the main text, each measured
a
curve was fitted with a sigmoid curve d + 1+e−bx+c
. Results for AlexNet, VGG16, and ResNet-18 with masks B and D are presented in Figures 1, 2, and 3,
respectively. Layer-wise ZAP analysis, together with curve fitting and threshold
optimization, is achieved by executing the following command:
python ./main.py -a [ARCH]-imagenet --phase EVAL_ZAP_ISOL
--mask_list [MASK]
--opt_th
Note that the indexes for masks A, B, C, and D are 6, 5, 4, and 3, respectively.
STATS ERR TO TH MIN and STATS ERR TO TH MAX were set in the Config.py file to
-1.3 and 1.3, respectively. STATS MASK VAL HIST MIN and STATS MASK VAL HIST MAX
were set, as well, to -1.3 and 1.3, respectively.
