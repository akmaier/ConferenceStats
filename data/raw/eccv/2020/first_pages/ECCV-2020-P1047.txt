We compare the average uncertainty, indicated by MI both between different
domains (inter-domain) and within the same domain (intra-domain) illustrated
in Figure S1. For Office-Home, we observe clearly that the visually most diverse
domain - Clipart yields the lowest MI even within itself. This is consistent with
the adaptation results, where we see that tasks involving the Clipart domain
are generally more difficult than others. Similar connections between the measured uncertainty and the task performance can be found in DomainNet too.
This visualisation clearly indicates the necessity of considering the difference of
distributional uncertainties in each task.
