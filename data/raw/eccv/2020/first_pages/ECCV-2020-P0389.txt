Overview
In this supplementary material, we provide additional information to complement the manuscript. First, we present details of Gridding, Gridding Reverse,
and Cubic Feature Sampling (Section 1). Second, we provide additional quantitative results on ShapeNet, Completion3D, and KITTI (Sections 2, 3, and 4).
Third, we present additional ablation studies (Section 5). At last, we present
more qualitative results compared to other methods (Section 6).
