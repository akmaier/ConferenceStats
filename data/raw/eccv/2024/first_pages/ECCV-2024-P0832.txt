Originality and Advantages over DDA

While DDA [13] and CloudFixer both use diffusion models for input adaptation,
their core ideas and methods are distinct. DDA uses an iterative generative
process while preserving low-frequency information, whereas CloudFixer uses
a parameterized 3D geometric transformation guided by the source diffusion
