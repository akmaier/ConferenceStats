In this document, we provide additional analysis, and visualization for the
Anonymous ECCV 2024 submission, "Nickel and Diming Your GAN: A DualMethod Approach to Enhancing GAN Efficiency via Knowledge Distillation".
We compare GAN-Slimming (GS) [5], Content-Aware GAN Compression (CAGC)
[3], Information-Theoretic GAN Compression (ITGC) [1], and NICKEL&DiME
(Ours). Our code is available at:
https://lait-cvlab.github.io/Nickel_and_Diming_Your_GAN/.
