A.1

Details of the Evaluation Metrics

Average Accuracy. The average accuracy AT assesses the classification accuracy on test data across all domains after completing training for all T domains.
AT is calculated as:
  A_{T} = \frac {1}{T} \sum _{i=1}^{T} B_{i,T}, 

(1)

where T represents the total number of domains, B ∈ RT ×T is a upper triangular matrix, and Bi,j denotes the test accuracy of the i-th domain after
training on the j-th domain. For example, in Tab. 1, the cell where “Train=D5”
and “Test=D3” denotes as B3,5 , indicating that after training on five domains,
S-iPrompts achieves a test accuracy of 54.47 on the third domain, while our
proposed method reaches an accuracy of 61.80.
Forgetting Degree. We follow [1] to evaluate the forgetting degree (FT ) of our
method as follows:
  F_{T}&=\frac {1}{T-1} \sum _{i=1}^{T-1} BWT_{i},\\ BWT_{i}&=\frac {1}{T-i} \sum _{j=i+1}^{T} (B_{i,j}-B_{i,i}),

(3)
where BW Ti represents the mean of backward transfer degradation. BW Ti and
FT are typically negative values. In general, the lower their absolute values, the
stronger the model’s ability to resist forgetting.
