1.1

Accuracy Comparisons

To further evaluate our model’s performance on general image classification, we
conduct experiments in the CIFAR100 [6] dataset. All trainable feature extractors (whenever applicable) are initialized with ImageNet [3] pre-trained weights
for a fair comparison. We use the CLIP [9] with ResNet50 [4] as the image
encoder for a fair comparison with prior CBM works. We also incorporate an
additional linear feature dimension adapter for the black box ResNet50 for a fair
comparison with our method. We set β1 = β2 = 1 in our method for simplicity.
Table 1 shows that our OpenCBM significantly outperforms the previous stateof-the-art CBM by 19.9 % and outperforms the black box ResNet50 [4] as well
as the CLIP [9] frozen ResNet50 image encoder by 1% and 14.9% respectively.
This observation is consistent with our results in the CUB dataset [13] in the
main paper, showing the generalization of our method in both fine-grained and
general image classification tasks.
