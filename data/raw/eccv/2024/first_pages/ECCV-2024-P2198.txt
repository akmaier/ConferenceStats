Methodology: More Details

A.1

Generating Torso and Limbs

In Fig. 3 of the main paper, we provided a figure to show a brief overview for
generating the abstract shapes. Here, we present more details on limb and torso
generation for both variants in Sec. 3.2.
Cube Variant All the vectors and points mentioned here are depicted in Fig. 3
of the main paper. Our limbs and torso are formed by cuboids with orthogonal
edges formed via appropriate cross-products; limbs have a long axis (a to b)
along the bone with a square cross-section, while the torso is longest along the
spine and has a rectangular cross-section. To generate the limbs, we obtain an
auxiliary vector, u1 = (b−a)×n, where n is any of the three axes (n ∈ {x̂, ŷ, ẑ})
and not parallel to the other vector (b − a). Using a series of cross-product with
u1 and b − a, we get our control points for each limb cuboid. For the torso,
we start by extracting the body centered coordinate system [7]. We set the
auxiliary vectors, u1 to the right vector and u2 to the forward vector – both
defined in accordance with [7]. From here, we can use scalar multiplication and
vector addition to find 4 end-points (p1 , p2 , p3 , p4 ) around the hip phip and 4
end-points (p5 , p6 , p7 , p8 ) around the neck pneck .
