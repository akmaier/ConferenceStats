Supplemental Experiments

comprehensive supplemental ablation experiments and analysis of the proposed
model. In Sections 1.1, 1.2and 1.3, we validate the IAFormer’s design on the
number of IAFormer/Encoder/Decoder Blocks and size of Interaction Knowledge Space; In Section 1.4, we analyze the efficiency of our model compared
with the existing methods via the Flops/Params measures.
