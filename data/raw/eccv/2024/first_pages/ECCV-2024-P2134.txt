Reproducibility

In order to promote further research and standardize the evaluations of new
defenses, we provide full cluster-ready PyTorch [23] implementations of SCA
and all benchmarks as well as replication codes for all experiments on our project
page at: https://sayantondibbo.github.io/SCA.
We provide full details of the cluster hardware and all parameter choices used
in our experiments in Appendix A.3 and A.4, and in Appendix Tables 1 and 2.
