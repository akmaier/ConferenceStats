Supplementary Material for AnyHome
This is the Supplementary Material for AnyHome. It includes additional
visualizations and experimental results, featuring video visualizations that navigate through the interiors of generated houses (Sec. 1), trajectory generation
visualizations (Sec. 2), layout refinement visualizations (Sec. 3), complex texture
generation (Sec. 4), diverse floorplans (Sec. 5), and a qualitative and quantitative
comparison with contemporary work (Sec. 7), highlighting our method’s uniqueness. We present the time consumption breakdown for each component (Sec. 6).
We also analyze the limitations of the current method (Sec. 8). Furthermore,
we provide details on our method’s settings, including implementation details
(Sec. 9), utilized prompts (Sec. 10), and the rules and algorithms for object
placement (Sec. 11).
