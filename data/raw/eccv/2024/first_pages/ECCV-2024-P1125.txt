Data Processing. The image and annotation processing involves three critical steps to ensure optimal data quality. Initially, we exclude images with a maximum resolution smaller than 250 pixels to prevent adverse effects on training.
Subsequently, we rigorously review each image’s annotations, discarding those
with discrepancies between actual and annotated sizes – a common issue with
internet-sourced images. Lastly, we validate the annotations to eliminate inaccuracies, focusing on refining bounding boxes and rectifying incomplete category
labels.
