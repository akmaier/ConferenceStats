Data Preparation Strategy

A.1

Adaptation of Public Datasets.

To collect the image-audio pairs from video-based datasets and adapt them to
our setup, we carefully choose one representative image from the video. We add
task-wise dataset details in Fig. 1. To this end, we design a semi-automated
strategy as explained later in each task section.
