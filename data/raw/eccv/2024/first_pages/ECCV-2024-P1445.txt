General Optimization Objective for MSVP

Here we provide the most general form of our proposed Maximum Singular
Value Penalization (MSVP). In this context, we employ three hyperparameters to flexibly adjust the strength of the maximum singular value penalties
for different attention branches. This aspect is crucial for achieving improved
performance and better adaptability within the self-attention mechanism, playing distinct and non-interchangeable roles for WQ , WK , and WV . Denote the
classification loss as Lcls such as cross-entropy loss, the overall general training
objective with MSVP is:
