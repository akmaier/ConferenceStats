Given the original dataset, denoted as T = (Xt , Yt ), where Xt ∈ RNt ×d and
Yt ∈ RNt ×c , DD aims to learn a much smaller synthetic dataset S = (Xs , Ys ),
where Xs ∈ RNs ×d and Ys ∈ RNs ×c , such that models trained on S and T
are of comparable performance. Here, Nt and Ns are the number of data in T
and S, respectively, and Ns ≪ Nt . d is number of features for each data, and c
represents the number of classes for classification task.
For better generalization ability of S, it always adopts multiple inner loops to
train a novel student model. Considering the small size of S, models trained on it
