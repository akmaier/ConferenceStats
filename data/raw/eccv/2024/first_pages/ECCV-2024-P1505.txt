CPL Analysis. While the proposed dynamic learning techniques in Sec. 3.3,
namely reliability based adaptive weighting and dynamic threshold, were originally inspired by the VOC dataset, we demonstrate their applicability on COCO
here. As shown in Fig. 2(left), the negative correlation between perplexity and
accuracy remains significant across various training time-steps, highlighting our
purposed perplexity estimation method can be used to penalize inaccurate regions in our CAM2Seg loss. Fig. 2(right) also suggests that the confidence distribution on the COCO dataset shows discernible clusters.
