predicate with the highest score for each pair, which is reflected in mR@K, it
might be more appropriate to utilize a no-graph constraint mean recall (ngmR@K) that considers all predicates. Table 1 shows that DPL exhibits significantly better performance compared to the existing the state-of-the-art models.
