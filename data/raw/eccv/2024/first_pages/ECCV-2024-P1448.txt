Datasets

In Table 1, we provide more detailed information about the publicly available
datasets we utilized. MCFS [9] is a motion-centered figure skating dataset, with
skeleton data extracted by Openpose [1] comprising 25 joints of 2-axis positions.
We utilize two subsets of this dataset: MCFS-22 and MCFS-130, categorizing
actions into 22 and 130 classes, respectively. PKU-MMD [8] is a large-scale multimodality action detection dataset, with skeleton data recorded by Kinect V2
sensors capturing 25 joints of 3-axis positions. We employ two partition settings:
X-sub, where the training set comprises 775 videos and the testing set contains
234 other videos; and X-view, where the training set consists of views from the
middle and right cameras, while the testing set is from the left camera. LARa [10]
is a typical warehousing activity dataset, with skeleton data recorded by optical
marker-based motion capture devices capturing 19 joints of 3-axis positions and
orientations. We downsample the action sequences of LARa from a sampling
frequency of 200 frames/second to 50 frames/second.
