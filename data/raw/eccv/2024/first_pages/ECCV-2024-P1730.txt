Estimating Class Numbers for Unlabelled Data.

In our implementation, we estimate the class numbers in unlabelled data with
Brent’s algorithm [10]. We first use pre-trained DINO weights for model initialization and conduct training with NGCN+CVCS for two epochs. The optimized
model is then used to extract features for class number estimation.
