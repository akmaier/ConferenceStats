Winograd Transformations

In the main paper the quantization-friendly Winograd transformation matrices
Θpso ≡ {Apso , B pso , Gpso } are derived by using the particle swarm optimization (PSO) algorithm. We have shown that CNNs utilizing full 8-bit quantized
Winograd convolutions based on the transformations Θpso achieve significantly
better quality performance compared to ones using the standard Winograd matrices Θstd ≡ {Astd , B std , Gstd }. Here we give the explicit expressions for the
PSO-derived transformations Θpso .
According to Eqs. (4-7) of the main text, the transformations for F m ≡
F (m, 3) Winograd algorithm can be defined by specifying the polynomial points
{(ξi , ηi ), i = 1 . . . m + 2} and the diagonal scaling matrices Sp∈{A,B,G} . The
polynomial points for F 4 and F 6 Winograd algorithms corresponding to the
transformations Θpso are given in Tab. 1. As the scaling matrices are not included
