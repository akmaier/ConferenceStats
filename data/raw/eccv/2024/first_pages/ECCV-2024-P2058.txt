Details of Loss function

As discussed in Sec. 4.3, we combine three different losses, including the focal
loss based on Cross Entropy (CE) loss, and the dice loss, which are given by:
  \begin {split} \mathcal {L}_{\text {ce}} = -\frac {1}{HW} \sum ^{HW}_{i=1} \gty _i \log (\phi (\mbf {Y}_i)) \\ + (1- \gty _i)\log (1-\phi (\mbf {Y}_i)), \\ \end {split}\\ \begin {split} \mathcal {L}_{\text {focal}} = -\frac {1}{HW} \sum ^{HW}_{i=1}\gty _i (1- \sigma (\mbf {Y}_i))^{\gamma } \log (\sigma (\mbf {Y}_i) )\\ + (1- \gty _i)\sigma (\mbf {Y}_i)^{\gamma }\log (1-\sigma (\mbf {Y}_i)), \\ \end {split}\\ \begin {split} \mathcal {L}_{\text {dice}} = 1 -\frac {2\sum ^{HW}_{i=1}\gty _i \mbf {Y}_i}{\sum ^{HW}_{i=1} {\gty _i}^2 + \sum ^{HW}_{i=1} {\mbf {Y}_i}^2}, \end {split}
