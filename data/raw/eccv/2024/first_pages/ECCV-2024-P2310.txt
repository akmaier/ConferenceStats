NTP performance on ResNet-18

We evaluate the NTP performance on ResNet-18 across diﬀerent pairs of datasets
on ResNet-18. Note that the baseline methods are only implemented for the VGG
structure, but we demonstrate the eﬀectiveness of NTP for ResNet-18. Similar to
the performance for VGG-11, NTP successfully reduces the model transferability
to a negative SLC-AUC level except for the source-target pair between the SD
(Synthetic Digits) and SVHN dataset.
