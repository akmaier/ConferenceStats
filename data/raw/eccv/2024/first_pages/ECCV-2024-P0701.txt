Policy Search Cost

Tab. 1 summarizes the policy search cost for different DAS methods. While
achieving better or comparable performance with all of those, FreeAugment finds
augmentation policies with a relatively small cost, especially considering that the
cost is reported for a single search, while some other methods have to repeatedly
apply it for several policy depth values. Some other methods are limited to
searching merely two consecutive transformations due to the exponential growth
of the cost with the policy depth. FreeAugment is the only one to eliminate that
entirely, requiring a single search to obtain all degrees of freedom, including
policy depth, without resulting in iterative solutions.
