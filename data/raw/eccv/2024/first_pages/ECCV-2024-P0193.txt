Introduction

GPSFormer is a novel transformer designed for point cloud understanding, addressing the challenge of capturing intricate shape information without external
data. It combines the Global Perception Module (GPM) with Adaptive Deformable Graph Convolution (ADGConv) and Multi-Head Attention (MHA) for
contextual representation, and the Local Structure Fitting Convolution (LSFConv) inspired by the Taylor series for learning geometric structures. GPSFormer
excels in shape classification, part segmentation, and few-shot learning by effectively integrating global and local structural information from point clouds.
To further verify the effectiveness of GPSFormer, we provide some additional
material in support of the main paper. The content is organized as follows.
