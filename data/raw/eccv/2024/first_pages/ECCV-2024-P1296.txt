Details of Slicing and 3D LUT transform

1.1

Slicing

The slicing is the interpolation on a bilateral grid [2] with query points retrieved
from a guidance map g ∈ RH×W . Our model uses each channel of an input image
as a guidance map and reconstructs a spatial feature map ϕ by the slicing operation. We select the trilinear interpolation for slicing after conducting ablation
studies in main paper. The slicing procedure is depicted in Fig. 1.
Qeury Point When we reconstruct spatial feature map value at specific spatial
coordinate ϕ(x, y), the query point can be denoted as
  \label {eq:qeury_point} \begin {split} P_b &= (p_{b_0},p_{b_1},p_{b_2}): \\ p_{b_0} &= x/(W-1), \\ p_{b_1} &= y/(H-1), \\ p_{b_2} &= g(x,y) \end {split} 
(1)

where (x, y) is spatial coordinate and is divided by (W − 1, H − 1) for normalization, g(x, y) is a value of guidance map at (x, y).
