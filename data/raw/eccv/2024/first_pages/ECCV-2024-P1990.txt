Gradient of log ZY (w)
  \nabla _w \log Z_Y(w) &= \frac {\nabla _w Z_Y(w)}{Z_Y(w)} = \frac {\int \nabla _w e^{-E(x,Y,w)} dx}{Z_Y(w)} \nonumber \\ &= \frac {\int e^{-E(x,Y,w)} \nabla _w \log e^{-E(x,Y,w)} dx}{Z_Y(w)} \nonumber \\ &= \int \frac {e^{-E(x,Y,w)}}{Z_Y(w)} \nabla _w \log e^{-E(x,Y,w)} dx \nonumber \\ &= -\int p(x|Y,w) \nabla _w E(x,Y,w) dx \nonumber \\ &= - \mathbb {E}_{p(x|Y,w)}\left [\nabla _w E(x,Y,w)\right ] \nonumber \\ &\approx - \frac {1}{M} \sum _{i=1}^M \nabla _w E(\Tilde {x}_i,Y,w),\nonumber

where x̃i ∼ p(x|Y, w), i ∈ {1, 2, ..., M }, and the last equation is obtained from
Monte Carlo sampling.
