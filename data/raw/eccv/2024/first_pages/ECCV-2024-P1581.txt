S1

Details about EBH dataset

In Section 3 of the main manuscript, we state the way to prepare our EBH
dataset. This supplementary section provides additional details.
S1.1

Camera ID

We utilize 7 RGB cameras (1 for blurry image, 6 for sharp images) and 1 event
camera. Specifically, we employ the Microsoft Azure Kinect DK (camera ID:
0x045E Microsoft) for RGB cameras and the Prophesee EVK4 (camera ID:
P50151) for the event camera.
S1.2

Camera Filter

To capture both blurry and sharp hand images, we employ RGB cameras with
different exposure times. Here, the camera’s detector with the longer exposure
time receives more photons, leading to higher pixel intensity and a brighter
image. To address this issue, we adjust intensity differences by attaching a neutral
density filter (Norman 810557) to the lens of the longer exposure camera.
