Additional details of CVGlobal

1.1

Distribution of CVGlobal

The distribution of the quantity in our proposed CVGlobal dataset is shown
in Figure 1. It comprises satellite-street view image pairs from seven different
cities around the world, with Brisbane featuring data across multiple years.
Simultaneously, each location is equipped with associated map data. Compared
to datasets such as CVUSA [2] and CVACT [1], CVGlobal offers a more robust
evaluation across a global scope.
