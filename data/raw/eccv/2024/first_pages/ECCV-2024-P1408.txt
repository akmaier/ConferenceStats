The Error Accumulation in Multi-step Approaches

Our multi-step approach is designed as a refinement process from coarse to
fine to address error accumulation. The Fig. 1 illustrates this process using real
examples. First, we use visual-text correlation in cross-attention to locate textrelated instances. Next, self-attention is employed to obtain a more accurate
mask through its self-correlation. Finally, SAM is used to refine the results.
