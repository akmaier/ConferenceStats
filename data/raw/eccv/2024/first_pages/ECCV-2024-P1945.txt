Theorem 1

Let fS , fT : X ∈ RD → K denote the true labeling functions on the source and
target domains. Given a distance metric ϵ that satisfies the triangle inequality,
the expected target error is bounded for ∀h ∈ H : X ∈ RD → K:
  \epsilon _T(h) & = \epsilon _T(h, f_T)\nonumber \\ & = \epsilon _T(h, f_T) - \epsilon _T(h, f_S) + \epsilon _T(h, f_S) + \epsilon _S(h, f_S) - \epsilon _S(h, f_S) \nonumber \\ & + \epsilon _S(h, f_T) - \epsilon _S(h, f_T)\nonumber \\ & = \epsilon _S(h, f_S) + (\epsilon _T(h, f_T) - \epsilon _T(h, f_S)) + (\epsilon _S(h, f_T) - \epsilon _S(h, f_S)) \nonumber \\ & + \epsilon _T(h, f_S) - \epsilon _S(h, f_T)\nonumber \\ & \leq \epsilon _S(h) + \epsilon _T(f_S,f_T) + \epsilon _S(f_S,f_T) + \epsilon _T(h, f_S) - \epsilon _S(h, f_T)=U_1(h)\nonumber \\ \label {s:2}
