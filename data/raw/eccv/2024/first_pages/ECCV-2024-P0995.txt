A.1

Related Works

Deformable Neural Radiance Fields. The deformable-NeRF [1, 4, 24, 30] is
for dynamic scenes reenactment by providing as input a time component, some of
imposing temporal constraints or scene flow, while the others [8, 26, 31, 36] using
a canonical frame/template to simulate motion by moving the camera position.
Some notable work, such as INSTA [49] adopts a bounding volume hierarchy
to accelerate point searching along rays. RAD-NeRF [32] and ER-NeRF [17],
following Instant-NGP [23], utilized a set of hash tables to reduce the number
of feature grids.
Facial Video Animation. The previous animation works employ morphable
model reconstruction, forward rendering with optimized textures [5,37], and image or video synthesis [9, 10, 35] through deep neural network [11, 12, 33, 34, 44].
Some of them [3, 14, 16, 40] utilizes rendered correspondence maps in conjunction with image-to-image translation networks to produce highly realistic videos.
Other methods [7, 46, 47] use implicit geometry and texture models to overcome
the limitations of texture quality by image translation network.
3D Gaussian-Splat Rendering. Recently, 3D Gaussian splatting [13] shows
its superior performance in synthesis quality and rendering speed. It leverage the
point elements as a discrete and unstructured representation to fit geometry with
arbitrary topology [41]. Some approaches [15, 18, 19, 25, 42, 48] extend Gaussian
representation to multi-view avatar reconstruction. However, these methods can
not be migrated to the monocular head avatar reconstruction setting.

A.3 Experiments
Extend Baseline. We extend the evaluation on six more state-of-the-art methods, including:
– IMAvatar [46]: We train the model for each video with 60 epochs. The image
size in it is rescaled to 512 × 512, while the original size is 256 × 256.
– Instant-Avatar (INSTA) [49]: We take training code with PyTorch. The Torso
part is retained for fair comparison.
– RAD-NeRF [32] and ER-NeRF [17]: We follow the official pipeline provided
by ER-NeRF [17] and RAD-NeRF [32]. However, their initial setting is audiodriven facial animation, which is different from ours. We replace the audio
features with the expression coefficients in the inputs. The model is trained
on each video with 60 epochs on face and 40 epochs on torso.
