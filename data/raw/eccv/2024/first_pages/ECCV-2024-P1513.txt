Details on Embedding Optimization

Ron et al . [4] introduce null-text inversion method to reconstruct an image for
2D diffusion models. In our work, we do not know the exact text prompt of the
inpainted reference image Iˆs . Thus, we implement unconditional DDIM inversion [1] to obtain the embedding for Iˆs . We uses per-timestamp unconditional
embeddings {et }Tt=0 , and initialize et with the embedding of the previous step
et+1 . In each timestamp t, et is optimized via:
  \mathop {\min }_{e_t} \parallel \bar {z}_0-\hat {z}_0(\bar {z}_t, e_t)\parallel _2^2, 

(1)

where z̄0 is the encoded latent of Iˆs , and ẑ0 (z̄t , et ) refers to the estimated clean
latent ẑ0 given z̄t and et . ẑ0 (z̄t , et ) is calculated using the Tweedie’s formula [3]:
  \hat {z}_0(\bar {z}_t, e_t) = \frac {\bar {z}_t}{\sqrt {\bar {\alpha }_t}}-\frac {\sqrt {1-\bar {\alpha }_t}}{\sqrt {\bar {\alpha }_t}} \widetilde {\epsilon }_\theta (\bar {z}_t, t, e_t, e_\emptyset ), 

(2)

where e∅ represents the embedding of a null text. ϵeθ (z̄t , t, et , e∅ ) is the classifierfree guidance [2] prediction:
  \widetilde {\epsilon }_\theta (\bar {z}_t, t, e_t, e_\emptyset ) = w \cdot \epsilon _\theta (\bar {z}_t, t, e_t) + (1-w) \cdot \epsilon _\theta (\bar {z}_t, t, e_\emptyset ), 

(3)

where w is the hyperparameter that balances the two terms.
