1.3

Diversity of Environments, Outfits, and More

We believe that clothing and environment variations are important to capture
in order for the model to generalize well to various use cases. Thus we define our
clothing diversity requirements to cover clothing types (hoodies, shirt, t-shirt,
jacket, sweater , cardigan, other), clothing patterns (solid, graphic, geometric,
stripe, floral, plaid, other), fit (regular one layer, regular multi-layer, relaxed one
layer, relaxed multi-layer, fit) and other diversity axis such as clothing length,
accessories, and footwear. We also strive for environment diversity by including
diversity axes on room type (dining, studio, living room, office, bedroom), floor
type (wood, carpet, concrete), rug pattern (geometric, stripe, solid, animal print,
plaid, floral, other). See the following Tab. 2 - Tab. 49 for details regarding
diversity on environments, outfits, and more.
