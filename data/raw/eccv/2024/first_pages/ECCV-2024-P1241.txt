Methods

We list the overview of existing 3D object detectors about their unifying
capability in Tab. 1. Related research on 3D universal detection still remains
lagging behind that of 2D. A universal 3D detector, we believe, should at least
have the ability to utilize any modality data to detect any category in any scene.
As such, it should achieve unifying across three levels: scene, modality, and category. Early research primarily focuses on the specific domains. Some recent
