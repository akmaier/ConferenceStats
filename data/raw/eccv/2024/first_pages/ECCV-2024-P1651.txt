Implementation Details

Overall Algorithms: For completeness, we quickly re-introduce our method.
Given N actors partitioned into two sets T and S, the joint policy is
  \label {eq:sup-multiagent-policy} \pi _{TS}(a_t^i | \vs _{\leq t}, \vm ) = \begin {cases} \pi _{T}(a_t^i | \vs _{\leq t}, \vm ) & \textrm {if } i \in \gT \\ \pi _{S}(a_t^i | \vs _{\leq t}, \vm ) & \textrm {if } i \in \gS . \end {cases} 

(1)

The objective of the teacher πT is
  R_T(\vs _1, \vm ) = \underbrace {C(\pi _{TS}, \gS )}_{\text {Challenging}} -\underbrace {C(\pi _T, N)}_{\text {Solvable}} + \beta \underbrace {(I_\data (\pi _T) + I_\data (\pi _{TS}))}_{\text {Realistic}}, \label {eq:sup-alice-objective} 

(2)

and the objective of the student πS is
  R_S(\vs _1, \vm ) = -C(\pi _{TS}, \gS ) + \beta I_\data (\pi _{TS})\label {eq:sup-bob-objective}. 

(3)

Finally, the modified policy where actions are replayed to ensure fair play is
  \hat {\pi }_{T}(a_t^i | \vs _{\leq t}, \vm ) = \begin {cases} \delta ( a_t^i - \bar {a}^i_t) & \textrm {if } i \in \gT \\ \pi _{T}(a_t^i | \vs _{\leq t}, \vm ) & \textrm {otherwise. } \end {cases} \label {eq:sup-replay} 

(4)

Architecture: Our transformer model uses a hidden dimensionality of 128, with 8
attention heads and a feed-forward dimensionality of 512. We use 6 transformer
blocks. Our map encoder uses the same hidden dimensionality of 128. For the
edge MLP in LaneGCN, the dimensionality is 64. Our action decoder is a 3 layer
MLP with hidden dimensionality of 64 as well.
