Details of the Objectives

This section reviews the objectives utilized for the training of audio-to-neutral
talking face (ANT) and emotional representation from audio (ERA).
A.1

Objectives for ANT

The reason for pre-training ANT is to ensure the performance of lip movement generation and realistic face video generation. Initially, we employ a facial
landmarks estimator [9] to encode and generate lip movement from audio. This
landmarks estimator serves the purpose of extracting lip landmarks from both
generated and ground truth (GT) videos. The Llip using two sets of landmarks
is defined as follows:
