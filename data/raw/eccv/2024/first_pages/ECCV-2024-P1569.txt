The Transformation between Plücker Coordinates and
Orthogonal Representation

The Plücker coordinate of the line are defined as: L = (n⊤ , a⊤ )⊤ with the
orthogonal representation parameters τ = [ψ1 , ψ2 , ψ3 , ϕ]⊤ . Where a ∈ R3 represents the direction vector of the line, n ∈ R3 represents the normal vector. We
have [2, 4]:
 \label {equ:p2o1} \mathbf {U} = \mathbf {Exp}([\psi _1,\psi _2, \psi _3]^{\land }) = \begin {bmatrix} \frac {\mathbf {n}}{\left \lVert \mathbf {n} \right \rVert } \quad \frac {\mathbf {a}}{\left \lVert \mathbf {a} \right \rVert } \quad \frac {\mathbf {n \times a}}{\left \lVert \mathbf {n \times a} \right \rVert } \end {bmatrix} 

(1)

The function Exp maps from so(3) to SO(3), and ψ = [ψ1 , ψ2 , ψ3 ]⊤ represents the rotation angles from the camera coordinate system to the line coordinate system around the x, y, and z axes, respectively. By utilizing equation
Eq. (1), we can obtain the first term of the orthogonal representation.
