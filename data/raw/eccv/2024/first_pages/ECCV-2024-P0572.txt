We present a comparison of our method NGP-RT (L = 2, LC = 512) with
two recently prominent techniques, 3D Gaussian Splatting [3] and MERF [5],
for real-time rendering of unbounded 360-degree scenes. The per-scene results
are summarized in Table S1, including metrics such as PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS for
rendering quality, and rendering time in ms at a resolution of 1080×1920.
Regarding the rendering time of MERF, we only provide the average time
across all views calculated from the fps number reported in the original paper,
