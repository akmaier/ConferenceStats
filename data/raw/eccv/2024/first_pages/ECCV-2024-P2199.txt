1.1

Evaluation Metrics

For the task of 3D BEV detection, we adopt the official evaluation metric, i.e.,
the mean Average Percision (mAP) as in nuScenes [1], which is computed by the
center distance on the ground plane for matching the predictions and ground
truth. In addition, nuScenes [1] presents five types of true-positive (TP) metrics , including Average Translation Error (ATE), Average Scale Error (ASE),
Average Orientation Error (AOE),Average Velocity Error (AVE), Average Attribute Error (AAE) for measuring translation, scale, orientation, velocity, and
attribute errors respectively. For illumination, weather, and scene adaptation
scenarios, the nuScenes Detection Score (NDS) [1] is adopted to measure the
overall performance of 3D detection task as follows,
  \text {NDS}=\frac {1}{10}\left (5\cdot \text {mAP}+\mathop {\sum }_{\text {TP}\in \mathbb {TP}}\left (1-\text {min}\left (1,\text {TP}\right )\right )\right ), 

(1)

where TP = {ATE, ASE, AOE, AVE, AAE} is the set of TP metrics.
In the context of camera configuration adaptation, due to the annotation
differences between Lyft and nuScenes datasets, we focus on their shared categories including car, truck, bus, motocycle, bicycle, and pedestrian. Under this
scenario, we adopted the evaluation metric *NDS [2] as follows:
  \text {NDS*}=\frac {1}{6}\left (3\cdot \text {mAP}+\mathop {\sum }_{\text {TP}\in \mathbb {TP}\text {*}}\left (1-\text {min}\left (1,\text {TP}\right )\right )\right ), 
where TP* = {ATE, ASE, AOE} is the set of TP metrics.
