Implementation Details

1.1

Cortex used for fMRI decoding

We use the same cortex as fMRI-PTE [6]. Specifically, early and higher VC RoIs
selected from the HCP-MMP atlas in fsLR32K surface space are shown in Fig. 1,
including “V1, V2, V3, V3A, V3B, V3CD, V4, LO1, LO2, LO3, PIT, V4t, V6,
V6A, V7, V8, PH, FFC, IP0, MT, MST, FST, VVC, VMV1, VMV2, VMV3,
PHA1, PHA2, PHA3”.
1.2

More Training Details

For both pretraining and finetuning, we initially use the AdamW optimizer with
a learning rate of 10−4 and a weight decay of 0.01. Additionally, to enable NeuroPictor to adapt to Classifier-Free Guidance [3], we randomly replace 5% of the
semantic features with unconditional CLIP embeddings corresponding to empty
characters at the end of each stage. During the stage where we replace 5% of
the semantic features, the learning rate is set to 10−5 , with 12,000 iterations
for multi-subject pretraining and 5,000 iterations for single-subject finetuning.
Stable Diffusion v2.1 serves as the initialization for the SD branch.
