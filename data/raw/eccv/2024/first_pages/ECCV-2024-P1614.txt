Overview

In the supplementary material, we discuss details of our rendering model (Sec. 2)
and diffusion model (Sec. 3). We demonstrate our method can be extended to
handle indirect lighting and metallic texture (Sec. 4). Experiments on the widely
used InvRender dataset [18] (Sec. 5) are provided for a more comprehensive
evaluation. We also conduct experiments that apply our priors to other baselines
with different material and lighting representations (Sec. 6). The implementation
details are discussed in Sec. 7. The quantitative and qualitative results of the
recovered environment maps are provided in Tab. 3 and Fig. 4, respectively.
Visualization of samples from diffusion prior on both synthetic and real data is
provided in Fig. 5. We also conduct ablation studies on real data and provide
results in Fig. 6. We provide more results and comparisons of our model on
real-captured data, including the DTU dataset and four objects captured under
natural illumination (Sec. 8).

Details on the Rendering Equation

We adopt the Cook-Torrance microfacet [1] for the specular shading,
  \begin {split} f_s &= \frac {D(\mathbf {h}) F(\mathbf {\omega _o}, \mathbf {h}) G(\mathbf {\omega _i},\mathbf {\omega _o},\mathbf {h})}{4(\mathbf {n}\cdot \mathbf {\omega _o})(\mathbf {n} \cdot \mathbf {\omega _i})}, \\ & \text {where} \ \ \mathbf {h} = \frac {\mathbf {\omega _o} + \mathbf {\omega _i}}{|| \mathbf {\omega _o} + \mathbf {\omega _i} ||}. \end {split} 
(1)

For the normal distribution function D which is related to the surface roughness
ρ, we chose formulation from [11]. For the fresnel effect F, we set F0 = 0.04
and use the formulation from [9]. We use formulation from [10] for the geometry
attenuation G.

Architecture of the Diffusion Model

We use the image I of size 256 × 256 × 3 as input for the diffusion model.
The image is encoded to latent space Il of dimension 32 × 32 × 4. Aside from
timestamp embedding, another direct input for the UNet is [xt |Il ] of dimension
32 × 32 × 8, which is the concatenation of xt and Il . We leverage the CLIP
image encoder to transform I into an image embedding of dimension 768 and
employ cross-attention with UNet intermediate features following [8].
