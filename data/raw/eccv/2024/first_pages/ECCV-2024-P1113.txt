More Dataset Details

1.1

Disease Type

Seven LN datasets are collected and curated. Among them, NIH-LN [1, 6] is
a public LN dataset, while the rest six are collected from five clinical centers.
Specifically, NIH-LN comprises of 89 lung cancer patients. Center1-HN includes
256 head & neck cancer patients. Center2 provides 91 esophageal cancer patients (denoted as Center2-Eso) and 97 lung cancer patients (denoted as Center2Lung). Center3-Eso consists of another 300 esophageal cancer patients. Center
1-3 plus NIH-LN are used as the internal data to develop and internally test
the LN detection performance. Datasets from Center4 and Center5 are used as
independent external testing data, where Center4-Mul. contains 184 patients of different types of diseases (35 lung cancer, 40 esophageal cancer and
109 infectious lung disease), and Center5-HN includes 50 head & neck cancer
patients.
