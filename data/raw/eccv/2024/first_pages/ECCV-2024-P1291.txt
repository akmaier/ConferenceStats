S1. Implementation Details
S1.1 Model architecture
We implement the proposed pipeline using PyTorch [31]. We utilize HRnetw32 [37] as the feature extractor for DepthNet. JointNet, RotationNet and KeypointNet share a ResNet-50 [9] network as the feature extractor.
