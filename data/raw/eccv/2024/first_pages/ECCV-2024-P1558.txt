Event-Based Motion Magnification:
Supplementary Materials
In this supplementary file, we provide the following materials:
– Video comparisons of different methods;
– Comparisons of different interpolation method;
– Quantitative results on real-world data;
– Representation visualization (referring to Section 3.2 in the main paper);
– Sub-pixel motion visualization;
– Explicit solution of event-based motion magnification (referring to Section
3.1 in the main paper).

1

Video Comparisons

We present the visual result of Figs. 1, 5 and 6 in the main paper. Due to the
characteristic of the problem, we provide supplementary video for better visualization. The video contains video comparisions with retrained REFID [3], Timelens [4]+PVMM [5], and Timelens [4]+LVMM [2]. One can see that our method
can well reconstruct rigid body motion with accurate frequency. Additionally,
the video shows results at varying magnification factors.

2

Interpolation method.

We follow LVMM [2] to use bicubic downsampler, and the model trained that
generalizes well to real-world scenarios. To provide a quantitative comparison,
we evaluate the model trained on bicubic downsampling and test it on the following two different downsampling methods. Tab. 1 shows that the interpolation
methods have minimum impact to the result.
