6.2

The Complexity of PoseSOR

We further include a complexity analysis of PoseSOR. Our PoseSOR has 220M
parameters and the inference time is 61ms per image at 768x768 resolution and
33ms per image at 512x512 resolution on a machine with an RTX4090 GPU and
an Intel i7-13700 CPU. We show detailed comparisons with PSR and OCOR
in Table 5. Overall, our efficiency is comparable, and it exhibits more favorable
SOR performance.
