Outline
As elaborated in the main paper, we proposed improve Knowledge Distillation (KD)
by regularizing feature direction and norm. Our three major technical insights
are (1) we take a novel perspective to improve KD by regularizing student
to produce features that are aligned with class-means features computed by the
teacher and have sufficiently large norms, (2) we study multiple baseline methods
to achieve such regularizations, and (3) we propose a novel and simple loss
that simultaneously regularizes feature direction and norm, termed dino-loss.
Experiments demonstrate that additionally adopting our dino-loss helps existing
KD methods achieve better performance.
In this supplementary document, We expand on the techniques of regularizing
feature direction and norm, including implementation details, additional ablation
studies and experiments.
