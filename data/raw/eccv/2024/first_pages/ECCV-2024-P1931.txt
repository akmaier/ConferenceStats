Metrics

In this section, we will provide a detailed introduction to all metrics mentioned in
the main paper. We categorize the metrics into two types: segment-level metric
and point-level metric.
1.1

Segment-level Metrics

The segment-level metrics are widely used by recently online HD map construction methods [3, 6, 7, 9–12]. These metrics can be used to evaluate segment-level
graph G = (V, E), where V are the vertices(centerline segments) of graph and
the edges E ⊆ {(x, y)|(x, y) ∈ V 2 } represent the connectivity among those centerline segments.
