Details of Base Loss Functions

In this section, we will elaborate on the base loss functions omitted in the paper,
including identity loss Lid and circle loss Lcir [7]. The identity loss transforms the
retrieval task of cross-modality ReID into an image classifier task, regarding the
person ID as the category of the corresponding images. It is defined as follows:
