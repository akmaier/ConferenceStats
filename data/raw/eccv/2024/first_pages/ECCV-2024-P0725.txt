Loss Function

The training loss of our model follows the DETR-like detectors, which is defined
as:
  \mathcal {L}= \mathcal {L}_{cls}+ \mathcal {L}_{box}+ \mathcal {L}_{d n} , 
(1)
where Lcls is the IoU-aware classification loss following RT-DETR [3], Lbox is
composed of L1 loss and generalized IoU loss for bounding box regression and
Ldn is the loss for denoising training [4]. In addition, we also calculate the loss
of each decoder layer as the auxiliary optimization loss.

Limitation

Our method is effective in addressing common misalignment in the majority of
cases. However, it may not handle extreme misalignment situations well, as the
object features of the modality will be lost when objects exceed the range of the
4D reference point.
