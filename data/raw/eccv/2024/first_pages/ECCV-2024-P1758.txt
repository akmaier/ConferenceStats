A

Details of Some Regularization Losses

In our main paper, we use an occlusion regularization loss to penalize the density fields near the camera [14]. Specifically, the occlusion regularization loss is
computed as:
  \mathcal {L}_o = \sum _{\mathbf {y}\in \mathcal {Y}} \frac {\boldsymbol {\sigma }_D(\mathbf {y})^\intercal \cdot \mathbf {b}_D}{D} = \frac {1}{D}\sum _{i=1}^D \sigma _i(\mathbf {y}) \cdot b_i, 

(1)

where σ D (y) denotes the density values of the D sampled points along the ray in
the order of proximity to the camera center and bD is a binary mask vector that
determines whether the density value of a sampled point should be penalized.
To penalize the density fields near the camera, only top M values of bD are set
to 1, and the rest are set to 0.
In addition, to investigate the effectiveness of our ray density regularization,
we compare it with the Emptiness loss on wi [13] and ray density entropy [4].
Specifically, the Emptiness loss on wi is computed as:
  \mathcal {L}_\text {emp} = \sum _{\mathbf {y}\in \mathcal {Y}} \frac {1}{N} \sum _{i=1}^N \log (1 + s \cdot w_i(\mathbf {y})). 

(2)

The ray density entropy loss is computed as:
  \mathcal {L}_\text {entropy} = -\sum _{\mathbf {y}\in \mathcal {Y}} \frac {1}{N} \sum _{i=1}^N p_i(y) \cdot \log p_i(\mathbf {y}). 

(3)
