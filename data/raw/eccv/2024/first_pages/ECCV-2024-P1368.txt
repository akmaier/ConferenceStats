Dataset Description and Evaluation Metrics

Details of number of patches for each dataset are presented in Table 1.
CAMELYON-16 focuses on detecting lymph node metastasis of the breast
cancer. It comprises 399 WSIs, 270 for training and 129 for testing. The average
ratio of tumor area over the whole tissue region for CAMELYON-16 is less than
10% [1]. We randomly select 20% of the training set as the validation set. And
the experiment is repeated five times.
TCGA-LUNG consists two caner subtypes, Lung Adenocarcinoma (LUAD)
and Lung Squamous Cell Carcinoma (LSCC). There are with 541 slides with
LUAD and 512 slides LSCC, a total of 1034 WSIs. We split the WSIs into 10
folds [2] and make sure WSIs from the same patient stays in the same fold. We
perform a 10-fold cross-validation to evaluate our method.
TCGA-BRAC includes two subtypes of breast cancer, 831 Invasive Ductal
(IDC) and 210 Invasive Lobular Carcinoma (ILC), 1041 in total. We also use a
10-folds [2] cross-validation protocol for both training and testing.
We report the Area Under Curve (AUC) to evaluate the mode classification
performance. We leverage class-wise performance metrics like Macro Accuracy
(ACC) and Macro F1 (F1) to eliminate the effect of class imbalanced training
samples. Mean and standard deviation are reported for each experiment.
