S1.1 Results on Aachen Day-Night V1.1 dataset
We run our pipeline on Aachen Day-Night V1.1 dataset [20], which is the extension of the original dataset with additional night-time queries. We follow the
same settings for Aachen Day-Night V1.0 dataset in Section 4.1. The complete
results are shown in Table S1. Compressing descriptor to dim=4 with SuperGlue
(SP+SG) framework brings about 3% accuracy drop for night queries under
(0.25m and 2◦ ), while the results under (0.5m and 5◦ ) are similar. Training our
model with LightGlue framework (SP+LG) closes the gap with un-compressed
model.
