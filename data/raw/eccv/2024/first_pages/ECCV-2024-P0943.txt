In this supplementary material, we include more implementation details of
the proposed Self-Cascade Diffusion Model (Section 1), more experiments on 16×
image scale adaptation (Section 2), and more visual comparisons and examples
on text-to-image and text-to-video synthesis on different base models, i.e., SD
2.1, SD XL 1.0, and LVDM [3] (Section 3). The code will be released.
