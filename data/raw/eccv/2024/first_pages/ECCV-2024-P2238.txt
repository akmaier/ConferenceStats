This supplementary material provides more details on our DailyDVS-200
dataset. We first introduced the detailed annotation method of the dataset and
provided an example of annotation. Then, we presented specific test results under different scenarios and supplemented the analysis of factors influencing the
scenarios. Moreover, we provided a detailed list of actions under our dataset,
as shown in Table 4. Finally, we validate the challenge of our dataset by applying the same experimental settings to an existing large-scale action recognition
dataset. And as shown in Fig. 1, we also provided examples of most of the scenes
and individuals.
