As shown in Fig. 7, OpenVIS [12] obtains mask proposals for a single frame,
then extract image embeddings from the cropped region is computed dot-product
with text embeddings. OV2Seg [36] obtains frame-level instance queries for
a single frame, and conduct dot-product directly with text embeddings. Our
OVFormer obtains video-level instance queries for a video clip, and generates
feature-aligned class embeddings by unified embedding alignment to facilitate
open-vocabulary classification.
