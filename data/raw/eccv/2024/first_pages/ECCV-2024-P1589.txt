Additional ablation studies

Analysis on the working mechanism of Sur2 f Sur2 f learns two parallel
streams, i.e. the Surface Rendering (SR) with explicit mesh and the Volume
Rendering (VR) with implicit SDF. The learning of both branches is necessary.
As ablated in Tab. A, the performance of both the reconstruction and rendering
degrades without either the learning of SR or that of VR. The learning of SR
helps the VR branch concentrate more attention on the surface, and the learning
of VR promotes a smooth optimization of the surface that supports SR. The
deformation of the surrogate mesh plays a crucial role in bridging the learning
of these two streams, enabling surface-guided sampling for VR and accurate
intersection finding for SR. And The use of different feature networks for VR
and SR benefits twofold. The receptive field of VR is the whole 3D space and
SR is only the surface. They acquire different network capacities for learning.
As ablated in Tab. B, sharing the feature network of VR with SR degenerates
the reconstruction as their converge rates are different. Furthermore, setting up
a feature network with a lower capacity for SR helps accelerate the training and
rendering.
