Overview

The supplementary material is organized as follows:
– In Sec. 2, we introduce more of our method’s implementation details, including the implementation details of Sparse4Dv2 [2] version.
– In Sec. 3, we report more experiment results, including the ablation study of
the location of importance score updating, the performance on the nuScenes
test set and the Waymo Open Dataset [4]. This section shows more about
the properties of our model.
– In Sec. 5, we illustrate more visualizations of our method for an intuitive
understanding, which shows the effectiveness of our method in various scenes.
