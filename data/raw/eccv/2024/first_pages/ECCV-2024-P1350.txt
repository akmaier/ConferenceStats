In the supplementary material, we sequentially present essential details related to
the experimental setup. Section 1 details the orthogonal mask parameters utilized in
the real experiments. Section 2 exhibits the test dataset derived from simulation experiments. In Section 3, the captured raw data from PCADIS and the outcomes subsequent
to alignment are showcased for analysis. In Section 4, we delve into the specifics of our
proposed algorithm SSFCT and present the preprocessing methodology utilized in our
simulation to compare various algorithms. Meanwhile, to enhance reader comprehension regarding snapshot spectral imaging (SSI) methods, including system complexity
and reliance on repeated calibrations, we meticulously examine these methods in Section 5.
