User Study

007

021

We conduct an extensive user study to further evaluate our S3 D-NeRF. Comparison with single-shot methods is conducted on 10 participants with 48 videos
generated by our method and comparison methods. Comparison with NeRFbased methods is carried out on 10 participants with 12 videos. Each participant is required to find the best from videos provided. Three key metrics are
taken in account, including the video fidelity, the audio-lip synchronization, and
the naturalness of the video. Results comparing with single-shot methods and
NeRF methods are shown in Tab.A2 and Tab.A1 respectively. For single-shot
methods, users prefer our results on video fidelity, the naturalness of portrait,
and lip synchronization, indicating the effectiveness of S3 D-NeRF. On the other
hand, our method has shown an competitive performance on video fidelity and
audio-lip synchronization even comparing with NeRF-based methods.
