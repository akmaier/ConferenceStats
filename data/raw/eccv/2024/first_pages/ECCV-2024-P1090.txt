– In Section I, we analyse the memory overhead in SN-Netv2.
– In Section J, we provide additional implementation details on semantic segmentation.

A

Implementation Details of Depth Estimation
Experiments

Based on DPT [9], we conduct experiments on NYUv2 [10] dataset and train
SN-Netv2 by stitching DeiT3-S/L and DeiT3-B/L. Specificially, we train each
model on 4 A100 GPUs with a total batch size of 16. We set the training epochs
to 24. We adopt AdamW optimizer with an initial learning rate of 2 × 10−5 . For
LoRA SL, we use a rank of 4 for all experiments. We utilize common metrics for
evaluting the performance on depth estimation, including δ > 1.25, δ > 1.252 ,
δ > 1.253 (where higher values indicate better performance), as well as AbsRel,
RMSE, and Log10 (where lower values indicate better performance). All other
hyperparameters are set as the default setting in DPT.
