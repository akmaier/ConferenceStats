Distance Measurement for Distributions. After obtaining the Gaussian
distribution of regions, a critical step is to measure the distance between distributions for further contrastive learning. There are three classical Gaussian distribution distance measurement methods: Wasserstein distance [8,10], KullbackLeibler (KL) divergence [2, 11], and Jensen-Shannon (JS) divergence [3]. Using
JS divergence as the distance measurement for Gaussian distributions does not
have a closed-form solution and introduces significant computational complexity
when approximating its solution. Therefore, JS divergence is not suitable for
use. KL divergence is also commonly used as a method to measure the distance
between Gaussian distributions, which can be calculated as:
