Dataset Details

Calgary-Campinas Brain. The Calgary-Campinas brain dataset [3] provides
47 cases of training data and 20 cases of validation data. Each case is 12channel, T1-weighted, 3D GRE (gradient-recalled echo), 1 mm isotropic sagittal
acquired on a clinical MR scanner (Discovery MR750; General Electric Healthcare, Waukesha, WI) from healthy subjects (44.5 ± 15.5 years, range 20-80
years). Acquisition parameters were TR/TE/TI = 6.3 ms/2.6 ms/650 ms, with
170 to 180 contiguous 1.00 mm slices and a field of view of 256 mm x 218 mm.
The acquisition matrix size [Nx , Ny , Nz ] for each channel was [256, 218, 170-180],
where x, y, and z denote readout, phase-encoding and slice-encoding directions,
respectively. In the slice-encoding direction kz , only 85% of the k-space data
were collected. We split 20 cases of validation data into 10 cases for validation
and another 10 cases for testing. Poisson disc sampling with acceleration factors
of 5 or 10 is used in the dataset.
CMRxRecon Cardiac. The CMRxRecon cardiac dataset [6] includes both
fully sampled dynamic cine and T1/T2 mapping datasets. Each dataset consists
of 120 cases of training data and 60 cases of validation data. For cine data, the
TrueFISP readout was used. The collected images include short-axis (SAX), twochamber (2CH), three-chamber (3CH) and four-chamber(4CH) long-axis (LAX)
views. The spatial resolution is 2.0 mm⇥2.0 mm, slice thickness is 8.0 mm, and
slice gap is 4.0 mm. Typically 5 ⇠ 10 slices were acquired for SAX cine, while a
single slice was acquired for other views. The cardiac cycle was segmented into
15 ⇠ 25 phases with a temporal resolution of 50 ms. For mapping data, T1 mapping was conducted using a modified look-locker inversion recovery (MOLLI)
sequence, which acquired nine images with diﬀerent T1 weightings (T1w). T2
mapping was performed using a T2-prepared (T2prep)-FLASH sequence with
three T2 weightings (T2w); both T1 and T2 data were acquired in SAX view
at the end of diastole with ECG triggering. All datasets were acquired on a
Siemens 3T MRI scanner (MAGNETOM Vida) from healthy volunteers. Equispaced cartesian sampling with acceleration factors of 4, 8 or 10 was used in the
dataset.
