Then, we point out the limitation of FADformer in Section 2. After that, we
discuss the effectiveness of FADformer on relevant rain removal tasks, including
high-resolution rain streak removal in Section 3.1, rain drop removal in Section

compared to other methods. In Section 6, we illustrate the low computational
burden of FFT operation and our inference speed. In Sections 7 and 8, we visualize and analyze the enhancement brought by FFCM and PGFN, respectively.
