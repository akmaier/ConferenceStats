2. Cubic B-Spline Formulation

assume that the four control knots are uniformly sampled
at a fixed time interval and the trajectory begins with T0 at
time t = 0 and terminates with T3 at time t = τ , where τ
represents the exposure time. We introduce the parameter
u = τt , where u lies within the interval [0, 1). Based on the
De Boor-Cox formula [3], the matrix representation of the
cumulative basis B̃(u) can be written as
