More Details

1.1

Training Setup

In terms of training particulars, we set a maximum of 50 epochs and use a batch
size of 4. The training process employs the Adam optimizer with a learning rate
of 1e^{-4} . All our experiments are conducted on an NVIDIA RTX 3080 GPU, and
we implement our method using the PyTorch framework.
1.2

Details of Comparison Methods

For the point cloud feature extraction algorithms adopted in comparison methods, namely PointNet++ [3], DGCNN [6], PRA-Net [1], and PointConv [9], we
tailor their outputs to cater to both keypoint estimation and correspondence estimation tasks. Specifically, we bifurcate their decoder into two distinct branches:
one is dedicated to generating a shared heatmap, and the other is responsible for
predicting the semantic label to each point. The shared heatmap for ground truth
is derived by selecting the maximum value from the multi-heatmaps. Meanwhile,
the semantic label attributed to each point in the point cloud is determined based
on the nearest keypoint’s semantic label.
