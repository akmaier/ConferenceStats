Additional Ablation Study Results

To further verify the effectiveness of the Knowledge Aggregation module in the
proposed MTKD method, we replace it with a weighted multi-teacher knowledge
distillation method (v8) [3], which was previously developed for multi-task learning. Following the same experimental setup of the ablation study in the main
paper, we still use the Urban100 dataset and the RCAN_lightweight model (for
the ×4 ISR task) in this experiment. The results shown in Table 1 indicate
that the employed Knowledge Aggregation module method does offer improved
performance over (v8).
