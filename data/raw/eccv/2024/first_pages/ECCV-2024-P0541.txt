Tables 11 and 12 present the comparison results using the K-400 pretrained
model on the few-shot and base-to-novel settings. All the models are first pretrained on the Kinetics-400 dataset and subsequently fine-tuned on each dataset.
TC-CLIP demonstrates superior performance over all other methods by significant margins. Particularly in the base-to-novel setup, TC-CLIP outperforms
ViFi-CLIP [12] with notable gaps of 3.5%p, 6%p, and 4.8%p in the base, novel,
and harmonic mean (HM) on average, respectively.
