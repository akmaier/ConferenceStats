Extended Experimental Results

The main manuscript contains only the RMSE and SNR metrics. In this supplemental material we provide the extended experimental results that include
MAE, RMSE, R and SNR metrics. As these are extended versions of the same
results from the main paper, we maintain the same table numbering. Results for
Illumination, Movement and Concealment scenarios, are shown in Tabs. 2 to 4.
