In this subsection, we learn a neural swapping network to generate the meaningful
combinatorial object images with different styles. The swapping part in the

convolutional layers and two fully connected layers with the parameter ψ. The
architecture of the swapping network consists of three convolutional layers with
a 3 × 3 kernel size, followed by two fully connected layers.
