Overview

This supplementary material addresses gaps not thoroughly covered in the main
document by offering detailed statistics and showcasing selected image examples
from our dataset. It includes comparisons of proper and improper actions captured by fixed cameras, as well as contrasts between drone and GoPro images.
The contents are organized as follows:

1. Overview (Current section)
2. Detailed Statistics
3. FixedCamera’s Example Images
4. Drone and GoPro’s Comparison

Detailed Statistics

In this section, we present the statistical information of the dataset. Specifically,
we summarize the number of clips for each category of action, broken down by
the type of device used for recording (FixedCamera, Drone, GoPro) in Table 1.
Based on Table 1, the distribution of video clips across device types is as follows:
FixedCamera accounts for 84.57%, Drone for 14.12%, and GoPro for 1.31% of
the total clips. Notably, the resolution for each device type is distinct, with the
FixedCamera capturing at 3840x2160, the Drone at 5120x2700, and the GoPro
at 1920x1080. The frame rate for all videos across these devices is consistently
set at 30 FPS.
