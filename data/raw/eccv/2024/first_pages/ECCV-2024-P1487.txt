A

Whole-body to Part Motions Discretization

The HumanML3D [2] and KIT-ML [5] datasets utilize the SMPL [4] and MMM [6]
Human Models, respectively. These datasets include joints related to whole-body
motion, excluding hand joints, as depicted in Fig. 1 and Fig. 2. In addition, HumanML3D utilizes 22 joints from the SMPL human model, while the widely-used
preprocessed KIT-ML benchmark, provided by [2], comprises 21 joints.

ParCo’s 6-Part Division Our ParCo divides the whole body into six parts:
R.Leg, L.Leg, R.Arm, L.Arm, Backbone, and Root. Specific partitioning details
for HumanML3D and KIT-ML are illustrated in Fig. 1 and Fig. 2. Both R.Arm
and L.Arm include the 9-th joint for HumanML3D (3-th joint for KIT-ML). The
inclusion of the joint in both arms is due to its role as a key point connecting
the arms and the backbone, providing positional information for the arms relative to this connection point. When reconstructing the whole-body motion from
part motions, we obtain three predictions of this joint from R.Arm, L.Arm, and
Backbone. We use the average of these three values as the final prediction.
