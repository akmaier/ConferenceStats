A

Further Implementation Details

The source code of SemiVL is provided at github.com/google-research/semivl.
For further information on the environment setup and experiment execution,
please refer to README.md. SemiVL is implemented in PyTorch. It is based on
the implementations of UniMatch [4], MaskCLIP [5], ZegCLIP [6], and MMSegmentation [1].
When comparing different fine-tuning strategies in Tab. 8 of the main paper,
we use the same hyperparameters as [6] for DPT, apply LoRA with rank 8 to
Wqkvo , and use Adapters with a channel reduction factor of 16. For all variants,
we separately tuned the backbone learning rate.

B

SemiVL with Other Semi-Supervised Methods

SemiVL can also be used to improve other baselines than UniMatch due to its
complementary approach. For example, we also combine it with FixMatch [3],
where it shows comparable improvements to UniMatch (see Tab. S1).
