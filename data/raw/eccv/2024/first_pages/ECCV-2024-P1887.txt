Additional Details on the proposed HOI-Synth Data
Generation Pipeline and Simulator

This section reports additional details on the proposed data generation pipeline
and simulator (Figure 1).
1. We select a random hand-object grasp (Figure 1-a) from the DexGraspNet dataset [18], a collection of 1.32 million robotic grasps generated with
an accelerated differentiable force closure estimator. DexGraspNet contains
5,355 untextured and convex-decomposed versions of 3D models from the
ShapeNet [2, 12], YCB [1], Big-BIRD [14], Grasp [6], KIT [7] and Google’s
Scanned Objects [4]. We recover original textured objects from such datasets
to obtain more realistic hand-object interactions. We also convert the hand
pose included in DexGraspNet from the ShadowHand format [13] to the
SyntheticHumans conventions.
