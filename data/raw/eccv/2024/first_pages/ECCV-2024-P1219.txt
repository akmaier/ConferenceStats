Deformable Feature Alignment

In this section, we make an exhaustive exposition of deformable feature alignment. Most previous works for referring image segmentation employ cross attention mechanisms to model the relationship between language and image. This
approach highlights the visual entity pertinent to textual descriptions, wherein
the incorporation of deformable mechanisms serves to accommodate geometric
variations of objects and direct attention towards salient regions enriched with
relevant features. In the TOG task, the region of interest is devoid of specific
visual entities, instead emphasizing conformity with linguistic cues and compatibility with other visual entities. Consequently, our proposed deformable strategy
diﬀers fundamentally from previous deformable mechanisms.
