A.1

wOCC Training Loss for other Employed OCC Models

We have discussed the wOCC training loss of STG-NF [1] in the paper. In what
follows, we discuss the wOCC training loss of other employed OCC models:
AE [6] and Jigsaw [5].
AE [6]. The basic building block for AE is the snippet feature X extracted
by I3D. For improving AE to wOCC, we define its wOCC training loss by:
  L_{wocc} = (1-w_X)\|X-f_{AE}(X)\|_{2}^{2} 

(1)

where wX is the weight of X and fAE (X) returns the reconstructed X by AE.
Jigsaw [5]. As for Jigsaw, the basic building block is a jigsaw puzzle X. The
jigsaw puzzle is processed in two streams: shuffled in spatial and temporal. Spatially, each frame is decomposed into n × n patches which are then shuffled. All
the frames share the same permutation meanwhile are kept in chronological order. Temporally, Jigsaw shuffles the frame sequence containing l frames without
disorganizing the spatial content. Its wOCC training loss based on cross-entropy
(CE) loss is defined as:
  L_{wOCC} = \begin {cases} (1-w_X)\frac {1}{l} \sum _{i=1}^l CE(t_i, \hat {t_i}), & X \in Q_t \\ (1-w_X)\frac {1}{n^2} \sum _{j=1}^{n^2} CE(s_j, \hat {s_j}), & X \in Q_s \end {cases}, 

(2)

where wX is the weight of X, and Qs and Qt respectively denote the sets of
spatial and temporal jigsaw puzzles. What’s more, ti and tˆi are the groundtruth and predicted positions of a frame in the original sequence respectively,
and sj and sˆj are the ground-truth and predicted locations of a patch in the
original spatial splitting grid, respectively.
