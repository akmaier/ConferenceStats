More Experimental Results on Fine-grained Corruption
Robustness Benchmarks

In the main paper, we present the performance of 8 cross-view geo-localization
models, including CVM-Net [1], OriCNN [2], SAFA [3], CVFT [5], DSM [4],
L2LTR [7], TransGeo [9], and GeoDTR [8]. For the fine-grained corruption robustness benchmarks, CVUSA-C and CVACT_val-C, specifically focus on R@1
performance. Within this section, we show the experimental results of R@5,
R@10, and R@1% on CVUSA-C in Tables 1, 2, and 3, and on CVACT_val-C
in Tables 4, 5, and 6.
Clearly, the trend of the models on R@5, R@10, and R@1% performance
shows the same trend as the models on R@1 performance based on the overall
experimental results we obtained. As a result, the relevant analysis regarding
R@1 in the main paper remains applicable to R@5, R@10, and R@1% and is
not reiterated here.
