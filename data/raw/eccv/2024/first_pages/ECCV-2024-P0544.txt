More on Experiments with ViT-B/16

A.1

Implementation Details

In this section, we detail the implementation of our experiments utilizing the
CLIP pre-trained ViT-B/16 model:
Network. The network’s output dimension is aligned with the 512-dimensional
CLIP features, thereby obviating the need for input feature downsampling. The
latent dimensions are tailored to each dataset: 256 for PACS, 448 for OfficeHome,
and 512 for VLCS and DomainNet, to accommodate the variety of categories
and complexity of datasets. The weight parameter α is adjusted to 0.208 for
PACS, 0.056 for VLCS, 0.14 for OfficeHome, and 0.2 for DomainNet, while it is
consistently maintained at 1 throughout the training phase.
