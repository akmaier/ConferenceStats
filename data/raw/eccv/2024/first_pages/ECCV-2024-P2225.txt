A

Limitations & Future Works

Due to computational resource constraints, we did not conduct experiments on
larger datasets (ImageNet-21K and JFT300M), larger models (more than 100M
parameters), and other modalities (text, video, and audio). In future works, we
will investigate the generalization of our approach to these domains and tasks.

More Comparisons and Ablation Studies

Comparison with previous attentions. We replace our partial attention with
previous attention designs and other experimental settings are kept unchanged.
Table 1 reports the performances of the proposed methods and other integrated
methods. PartialFormer-B1 outperforms other attention designs while running
faster. Typically, our B1 outperforms HiLo attention [19] by 1.5% while running
1.8× faster, Bi-level Routing attention [36] by 1.2% with 1.6× faster, and Vicinity attention [21] by 2.9% while running 1.3× faster. It verifies that our attention
method enables better feature extraction.
