Conditional Coverage Experiments

As described in Sec. 2, conformal prediction typically provides only marginal
coverage guarantees. In this section, we empirically evaluate different forms of
conditional coverage. To do this, we use the Monte-Carlo testing procedure described in Sec. 4 with T = 10000, R = 8, p = 32, and α = 0.05.
