Hyperparameter details

In Section 4.2 of the main paper, we introduce a novel approach to data selection characterized by a variable-length selection schedule. This schedule is
devised to initiate data selection after more extended epochs in the initial phase
of training and progressively increase the frequency of subset selection as the
training process unfolds. Specifically, we begin subset selection after 80 epochs,
subsequently conducting selections at intervals of 60 epochs, with the frequency
gradually decreasing to 10 epochs as the training nears completion. This dynamic
scheduling strategy aims to adaptively adjust the data subset selection process
