Supplementary Material: Open-Vocabulary
RGB-Thermal Semantic Segmentation

In this supplementary material, we provide more experiment results to show
the effectiveness of the open-vocabulary RGB-T semantic segmentation framework (OpenRSS). In the following sections, we present the evaluation results for
per-category on each dataset. Then, we display more visualization results on the
MCubeS datasets. Lastly, more detailed studies are presented.
