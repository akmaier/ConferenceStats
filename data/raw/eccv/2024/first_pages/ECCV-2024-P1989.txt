the 3D mesh loss:
  \mathcal {L}_{mesh}^1=||{\bf \hat {V}}-f_{\beta }({\bf \hat {X}})||_2 \label {eq:loss_}

where V̂ is the ground-truth mesh, X̂ is the ground-truth skeleton, and β is the

we have:
