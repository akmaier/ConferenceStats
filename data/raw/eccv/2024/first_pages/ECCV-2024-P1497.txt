Baseline Implementation Details

In this section, we provide detailed explanations of our simple vision-based joint
perception and prediction (PnP) baseline model introduced in Sec. 5.3 of our
main paper. The overall architecture is illustrated in Figure 1.
RNN-based Ground Truth Encoder. In this step, we adopt Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) [1] as the RNN encoder and encode temporal information of
ground truth trajectories of the agents, which we denote as Y . Since this process
uses ground truth trajectories, it is only operated in the training step of the
model. Then, these features are concatenated with the agent features X from
the perception stage and are fed to the CVAE Network.
CVAE Network. The CVAE Network converts the agent features and the encoded ground truth future trajectory features into the probability distributions
with two networks P and Q. In the training step, the Kullback-Leibler divergence (KLD) loss between these distributions are minimized as follows:
