Implementation Details

As discussed in the main paper the SMILe framework proposes an architecture
agnostic approach and adopts several backbones - Faster-RCNN [8] and ViT
[4, 6]. We conduct experiments on PASCAL-VOC [3] and COCO [5] datasets.
For VOC, the input batch size to the network (both Faster-RCNN and ViT based
approaches) is set to 16 and 2 in the base training and few-shot adaptation stages
