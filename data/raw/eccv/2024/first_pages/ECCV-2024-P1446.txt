B

Training Parameters

B.1

Imagenet-50/100/200

The training process is similar to [3], using a 2-GPU setup, an SGD optimizer
with a momentum of 0.9, a batch size of 32 per GPU, input resolution 224×224,
weight decay of 4·10−5 , and a 5-epoch linear warmup followed by cosine annealing
for 100 epochs. The initial learning rate was adjusted to 0.025, taking the number
of GPUs into consideration. The implementation is based on [8].
