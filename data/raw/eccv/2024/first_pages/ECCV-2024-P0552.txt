A.1

The closed-form projection operation

Recall from (7) that Projw∈S (a) indicates the projection of a constant a onto
the constraint set S. This projection operation is defined as solving the auxiliary minimization problem Projw∈S (a) = arg minw∈S ∥w − a∥22 , where S =
{w|w ∈ [0, 1], 1⊤ w = m}. It is worth noting that we have relaxed the original
binary constraint into its continuous counterpart, with w ∈ [0, 1]. The relaxed
constraint is given by the intersection of the box constraint w ∈ [0, 1] and the
hyperplane 1⊤ w = m.
According to [15, Proposition 1], the solution of the above projection problem
yields
  \mathrm {Proj}_{\mathbf w \in \gS } (\mathbf a) =P_{[\mathbf {0,1}]}[\mathbf {a}-\lambda \mathbf {1}],

(9)

where the variable λ is given by the root of the equation 1⊤ P[0,1] [a − λ1] = m,
and P[0,1] is an element-wise thresholding function
  P_{[\mathbf {0,1}]}[x_i] = \left \{ \begin {array}{ll} 0, & x < 0 \\ x, & x \in [0, 1] \\ 1, & x > 1 \end {array} \right .

(10)

for the ith entry of a vector x. We also remark that finding the root of the nonlinear equation with respect to λ can be achieved using the bi-section method [1].
