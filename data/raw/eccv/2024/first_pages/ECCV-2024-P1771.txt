Additional Experiments

1.1

Efficiency of Label-constrained Training

To better show that our method can alleviate the data limitation for smallscale STR datasets. we further finetune our Uformer-B+TMIM with 10% (274
samples) and 20% (549 samples) of the SCUT-EnsText [5] training set. We also
list the results of Uformer-B without pretraining as the baseline. The results
are shown in Tab. 1. It is clear that the baseline model without TMIM heavily
relies on the labeled sample number: 4.21↓ (30.96 vs 35.17) and 3.18↓ (31.99 vs
35.17) performance drop with 10% and 20% data. While our TMIM pretrained
model only has 0.26↓ and 0.16↓. Especially, our TMIM can use only 10% training
data to reach over 37.00 PSNR. With 20% training data, it can further get 37.19
PSNR which achieves SOTA PSNR performance in Table 6 from the main paper.
Additionally, compared with the baseline models, our TMIM can obtain larger
improvements with fewer training data (6.13↑ for 10% data and 5.20↑ for 20%
data.)
