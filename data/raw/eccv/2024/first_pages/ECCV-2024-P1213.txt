Here, we provide the implementation details, Dierential Mapper (DM) architecture, and delve deeper into the Dierential Information Tracking Path (DITP).
Implementation Details. All experiments were conducted using PyTorch 1.11.0
on four NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 GPUs. For training the deformation network, we utilized the AdamW optimizer [4] with a batch size of 8. Exponential
decay rates were set to β1 = 0.9 and β2 = 0.999, with an initial learning rate of
3 × 10−5 . Training was performed for 100 epochs, with the learning rate reduced
by a factor of 0.2 every 20 epochs. The hyperparameter λstyle was set to 100.
For training the synthesis network, we also used the AdamW optimizer with
a batch size of 8. Exponential decay rates were β1 = 0.9 and β2 = 0.999. Training
was carried out for 40 epochs, with a learning rate set to 1 × 10−5 . Hyperparameters γdif , γM AE , and γP REC were set to 0.5, 0.1, and 0.05, respectively.
