I.1

Dataset Source and Filtration

The Stack Exchange data is hosted at https://archive.org/details/stackexchange in XML
format. We started with 330,705 candidate visual questions. After removing visual questions without an accepted answer, it resulted in 165,766 visual questions. As mentioned
in the main paper, we then conducted two filtering steps. After removing visual questions
with scores of 0 or less for either the question or answer, we had 119,177 visual questions. Next, after removing visual questions with multiple images, we had 85,573 visual
questions. Subsequently, removing visual questions with visual answers, left 65,849
