In the supplementary materials, we share the source code and the proof of
Theorem 1, we also provide additional experimental results. The detailed code
is available in the compressed file FedRA_code.zip. The overall logic of FedRA
is straightforward, with the main differences from existing methods lying in the
model distribution and aggregation processes, as illustrated in Algorithm 1. In
comparison to InclusiveFL, FedRA does not introduce additional costs during
model distribution and has a more concise aggregation process.
