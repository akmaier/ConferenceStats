In this supplementary material, we introduce more details about the MMDMC
dataset and the proposed method, including the data license for MMD characters, model architecture and illustration of the apparel. We also provide more
qualitative results with a video demo.

A. Details of the MMDMC Dataset.
We show in Figure 2 for sample ground truth character-motion pairs from the
dataset. The MMDMC dataset features diverse and complex types of characters and apparel. More importantly, the apparel can realistically deform under
physical dynamics, which makes the dataset amendable for learning high-quality
motion transfer with realistic apparel animation.
Moreover, in order to be compatible with the motion capture data, we align
MMD [1] body joints with the SMPLH [10] human model used in the AMASS [7]
dataset. Specifically, we remove "spine3" bone animation in the motion capture
data as this bone does not exist in MMD characters. In addition, we remove all
level 3 finger animations to reduce the number of body joints, resulting in total
J = 40 body joints. All characters share this template skeleton, varying only on
the scale of each bone. Such body skeleton is commonly adopted in the game
and animation industry, e.g. also used in the Mixamo [2] dataset, hence models
trained on the MMDMC dataset can be easily applied to relevant applications
without post-processing on skeleton structures. For the motion, we collect from
the DanceDB archive in the AMASS dataset. Rendering for the character-motion
sequences is done in the Blender3 [4].
