Overview

In this document, we provide additional experiments and analyses. In particular:
– Section 2 provides additional visualizations of the selected samples with the
highest/lowest latent density scores across different generative models and
datasets.
– Section 3 provides additional results of applying our quality assessment
method on other domains and modalities.
– Section 4 shows the latent density scores for images at different truncation
levels.
– Section 6 provides visualizations of the latent space of different generative
models.
– Section 5 provides additional analysis on the choice of hyper-parameter σ in
our score function.
– Section 7 justifies the choice of our score function.
– Section 8 provides quantitative results of the efficiency gain of our method.
– Section 9 includes a user study of our method.

Additional Results on Other Domains and Modalities

3.1

Quality Assessment on 3D Shapes

In Figure 6, we show additional quality assessment results on generated 3D
shapes using our proposed latent density score. The 3D shapes are generated
via a StyleGAN2-based [9] 3D shape generation framework, SDF-StyleGAN [15]
trained on ShapeNet Core V1 [3]. As shown in the figure, the generated samples
with high scores have better visual quality with meaningful object structures.
The generated samples with low scores, in contrast, exhibit irregular objects
shapes and severe geometry distortion.
