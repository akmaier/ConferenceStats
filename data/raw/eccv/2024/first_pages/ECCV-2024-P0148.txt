Implementation Details

Network design. We adopt a neural network similar to OccNet [13] and it
contains 8 layers of MLP where each layer has 256 nodes. There is a skip connection in the fourth layer as employed in DeepSDF [14] and ReLU [1] activation
functions in the last two layers of MLP.
Training and inference settings. We utilize the Adam optimizer [10], setting
an initial learning rate of 0.001 and employing a cosine learning rate schedule
with 1k warm-up iterations. For the shape reconstructions including ABC [11],
FAMOUS [7], ShapeNet [4], and SRB [16] datasets, we train the neural network
for 40k iterations, and we use a resolution of 2563 for the marching cubes algorithm [12]. For the 3D Scene dataset, we train the neural network for 100k
iterations to fully converge on each scene. Since there exist open surfaces, we use
a resolution of 5123 to extract the 0.001 level set as the reconstructed mesh. If not
stated otherwise, all other methods of comparison will use consistent settings.
