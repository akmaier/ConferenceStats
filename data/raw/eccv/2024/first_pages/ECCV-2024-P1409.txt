1.1

Comparison on the CHAMELEON Dataset

This section presents a comparative analysis of our proposed method against
existing state-of-the-art (SOTA) techniques on the CHAMELEON dataset [8].
The CHAMELEON dataset, specifically designed for Camouflaged Object Detection (COD), includes 76 instances of camouflaged animals along with their
corresponding annotations. Unlike other methods, our approach, dubbed FocusDiffuser, was evaluated without any additional training on this dataset. The
comparative results, as summarized in Tab. 1, highlight the superior performance
of FocusDiffuser over the current SOTAs.
