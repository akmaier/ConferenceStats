This supplementary document is organized as follows:
Section 1 provides the PSNR results of different methods on ×2 event-based
VSR on CED and the SSIM results of different methods on ×2 and ×4 eventbased VSR on CED. More qualitative comparisons are also included in this part.
Section 2 provides more detailed ablation studies.
Section 3 provides more details on applying the EAA and EAF units to
different VSR networks.
Section 4 provides additional experimental results and discussions.
Section 5 provides related work on parameter-efficient tuning methods.
