A.1

Effectiveness of DDPM to WSSS

Our research drew inspiration from empirical observation that features obtained
from a pretrained DDPM, illustrated in Fig. 1a from the main paper and examples in Fig. A2, can provide high-quality localization information. As shown
in Fig. A1, ViT-CAMs (A) exhibit increased false positives from lack of consideration for spatial context (locality). In contrast, Diffusion-CAMs (Adif f )
obtained through the proposed LFCA exhibit precise CAMs with lower false positives. Supervising ViT-CAMs A with the well-localized Diffusion-CAMs Adif f
leads to an improvement of ViT-CAMs. Furthermore, handling objects with
high background correlation, such as train-railway, with only classification labels is challenging in WSSS. Indicated by significant improvements in train and
boat segmentation performance in Tab. A1, and the K-means clusters shown
in Fig. A2, we can analyze that DDPM features exhibit a certain level of debiasing effect to ViT-CAMs.
