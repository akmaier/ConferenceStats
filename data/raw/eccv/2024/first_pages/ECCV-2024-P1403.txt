Deriving the New Embedding

In this section, we present a detailed derivation of the closed-form new embedding.
Let W old denote the projection matrices of the original U-Net before UCE’s
editing, W new represent the projection matrices after UCE’s editing, c denote
the embedding of "nudity", and c′ signify our derived embedding. If we can find a
c′ such that W new c′ closely resembles W old c, then c′ can guide the edited model
to generate nude images like how c guides the original model. Specifically, the
objective function is formulated as follows:

below. The square of the 2-norm is convex, and linear transformation maintains
its convexity. Therefore, Eq. (A.1) represents a convex function, possessing a
unique global minimum solution c′ . This solution can be obtained by setting the
gradient of Eq. (A.1) to zero:
