Details in the UniKPT Dataset

As shown in Fig. 1, we demonstrate that each dataset only focuses on a single
super-category (e.g., “human only” and “animal only”), making it challenging to
achieve keypoint generalization when using them individually. Additionally, these
datasets have significant differences in the quality, quantity, and appearance
styles of keypoint annotations. Therefore, we are motivated to unify all the
datasets into one called UniKPT, following the below guidelines:

(a) COCO (Human-only)

(d) Deepfashion2 (Cloth-only)*

(b) Human-Art (Human-only)

(e) OneHand10K (Hand-only)

(g) AP36K (Animal-only)

(j) Keypoint-5 (Rigid objects-only)

(h) AP10K (Animal-only)

(k) Carfusion (Rigid car-only)*

(c) 300W-Face (Face-only)

(f) Animal Kindom (Animal-only)

(i) AnimalWeb (Animal face-only)

(l) Vinegar Fly (only) (m) Desert Locust (only) (n) MacaquePose (only)

Fig. 1: Visualization the unified dataset (UniKPT) for each original dataset. * means
the two datasets are included in MP-100 [15].
