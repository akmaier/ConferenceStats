This supplementary material covers: the ablation study of our part-based
VQ-VQE embedding (Sec. A); an experiments of our multi-joint control results
(Sec. B); the performance of our method using incompleted trajecotries (Sec. C);
the runtime of different joint control strategy (Sec. D); an ablation study with
IK based solution (Sec. E); results of using only one modality input (Sec. F);
more implementation details (Sec. G); and more qualitative results (Sec. H);.
We also include a video showing more visual results in our supplementary. We
highly encourage our readers to view the supplementary video for our qualitative
comparison and results.
