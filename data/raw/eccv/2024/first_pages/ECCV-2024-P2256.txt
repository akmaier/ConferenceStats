Overview of the Supplementary

In this supplementary file, we provide the following information, which we hope
will shed deeper insight into our contributions:
– Training Details
– Training Efficiency Analysis
– Experiments with More Configurations
– Experiments on Other Tasks
– Experiments for Model Self-enhancement
– Distillation with More Powerful Teacher
– Difference between Output Distribution of Different Structure Models
For the reference in the manuscript in Simple to Complex Model Distillation,
please refer to section 6. For the reference in the manuscript in Comparison with
Stronger Teacher, please refer to section 8.
We first highlight our advantages: Different from previous teacher models
in distillation, which are either complex or require additional modalities, and
are difficult to obtain for high-performance student models, we propose a novel
approach to simplify teachers, eliminate the need for extra modalities, and can be
effectively applied to high-performance students. Comparisons are shown below.
