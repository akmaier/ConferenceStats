Example visualizations
As the visualization results Figure 5 (Cityscapes → Foggy-Cityscapes) illustrate,
SFUT and AdaBN + Fixed SF-FM can efficiently detect objects that the source
pre-trained model ignores, especially shrouded people in fog, stacked bicycles,
and incomplete cars. AdaBN also shows a significantly better performance than
the source model.
