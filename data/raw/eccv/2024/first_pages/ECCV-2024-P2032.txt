As discussed in the main paper, we report our benchmarking results on three
datasets: CelebA, CIFAR10, and FairFace. We apply several defense algorithms
(studied in other papers) and attack algorithms (introduced in this paper) to
evaluate the performance of each technique using four different reconstruction
metrics: 1) SSIM, 2) L1, 3) L2, and 4) PSNR. For each entry in the reported
results, we conduct multiple experiments by adjusting the hyperparameter that
controls the privacy-utility tradeoff. This process generates several attack results
for each privacy hyper-parameter for each defense technique. In the tables below,
we report only the area under the curve (AUC) for these experiments, as the total
number of experiments amounts to several hundreds for each dataset. Due to
the extensive computation time required, a few defense techniques are currently
missing from our benchmark experiments. However, all these defense algorithms
are implemented in our benchmark and available for evaluation. As more computational resources become available and the benchmark evolves, we will continue
to conduct additional experiments to provide a comprehensive assessment of
the various techniques. We have organized all of our benchmark, datasets, and
evaluation code in the same repository with corresponding hyper-parameters here
- https://https://github.com/aidecentralized/InferenceBenchmark.
