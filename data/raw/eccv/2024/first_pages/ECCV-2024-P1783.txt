Training Settings

details of our training approach are shown in Tab 1. First, in the initial stage,
we apply affine and thin-spline transformations to static images [3, 6, 8, 9, 11] for
synthesizing video clips consisting of three frames. We pre-train the model on
these generated sequences, setting the segmentation target to one and the length
of sampled sequences is 3 in this phase. Then, in the second stage, we conduct
further train the model on the BL30K [2] dataset, where the segmentation target is set to three. Finally, in the main training stage, we train the model on
