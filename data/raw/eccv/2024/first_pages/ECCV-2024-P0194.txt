Additional Implementation Details

1.1

Metrics

We utilize the same evaluation metrics as InterGen. (1) FID: measuring the latent
distribution distance between the generated dataset and the real dataset. (2) R
Precision: measuring the text motion matching, indicates the probability that the
real text appears in the Top-k (1, 2, and 3) after sorting. (3) Diversity: measuring
latent variance. (4) Multimodality (MModality): measuring diversity within the
same text. (5) Multi-modal distance (MM Dist): measuring the distance between
motions and text features.
