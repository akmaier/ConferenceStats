DIHM Hardware Setup

Our prototype is depicted in Fig. 1: we use a narrow-band, wide-range tunable
laser light source (a NKT Photonics SuperK laser) combined with a Laser Line
Tunable Filter (LLTF) device to emit coherent light in the visible range (1-2.5 nm
bandwidth). The illumination is directed from the tip of a single-mode fiber, and
a detector-filling light cone is achieved by maintaining an illumination-to-object
