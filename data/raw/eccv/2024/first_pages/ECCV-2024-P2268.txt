The comprehensive analysis of time consumption is presented in Tab.1. Our
detection techniques exhibit ultra-real-time performance during inference, resulting in negligible additional time requirements of 0.06% (FTT) and 0.25%
(CDA) when compared to the original generation process. For the localization
and the mitigation, although these stages may involve longer execution times,
it is important to note that our detection phase has effectively filtered out a
small subset of samples with triggers. Thus, the whole computation cost is well
controlled. Except for CDA, all proposed methods are train-free, leaving a low
cost for training.
