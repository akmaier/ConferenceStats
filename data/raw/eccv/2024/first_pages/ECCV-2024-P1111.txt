Overview

In this supplementary material, we provide additional details of our method and
experiments, including:
– The experimental settings and results of the toy experiment presented in
Section 1.
– The method to obtain the disparity distribution and the occluded mask from
the ground-truth disparity map.
– The trade-off between accuracy and efficiency.
– The data capacity of FormerStereo when more synthetic samples are used
for training.
– The results of Robust Vision Challenges.
– The zero-shot generalization claim for FormerStereo.
– More visualization results and failure cases.
