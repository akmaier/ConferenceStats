In order to clearly contrast the semantic segmentation boundaries between
LERF and our method, we adopted the visualization technique from the LERF
paper. Specifically, we normalized the regions where the relevance map probability exceeded 0.5, as shown in the second row of the above Fig. 1. Here, we
present the relevance maps without normalization. It can be observed that our
relevance maps exhibit stronger contrast and clearer boundaries compared to
the unprocessed ones.
