Visualization of Joint-specific Attention

Figure I is the heatmaps of category-related joint-specific attention Ac for each
general exercise category in the E3V-K5 test set. The six subplots correspond to
six general categories of exercises, titled above each subplot. The x-axis of each
subplot represents joint indices, and their specific correspondence with human
body joints is illustrated in Figure II. The y-axis represents sample indices, thus
each row of the subplots represents an Ac of a specific sample. The brighter the
heatmap, the larger the Ac value, indicating that the joint is more important
for its exercise category.
