S1

Additional Experiments

Along with the evaluations on SR networks of scale ×4 discussed in the main manuscript,
we also assess our framework on networks of scale ×2. As shown in Table S1, our
framework outperforms existing SR quantization methods in terms of both PSNR and
SSIM, demonstrating its effectiveness on scale ×2 SR networks. Specifically, the PSNR
gain on Set5 is 0.28 dB on EDSR, 0.74 dB on RDN, and 0.25 dB on SwinIR. These
results confirm that our framework is effective for both CNN- and Transformer-based
SR networks of scale 2.
