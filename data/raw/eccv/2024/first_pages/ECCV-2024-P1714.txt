In this supplementary materials, we provide supplementary information to
our paper “SAH-SCI: Self-Supervised Adapter for Efficient Hyperspectral Snapshot Compressive Imaging”. Contents in this document include:
– Sec. 1 gives more details about employed training and testing datasets.
– Sec. 2 provides additional detailed quantitative evaluation results for each
test scene where full fine-tuning results are also included.
– Sec. 3 provides additional qualitative results about simulated and real data.

1

Training and Testing Details

1.1

Training Datasets

The training datasets contain ICVL [1] with 201 hyperspectral images, Harvard
[4] with 77 hyperspectral images and NTIRE2022 [2] with 950 hyperspectral
images, where the images from ICVL are padded to 1392 × 1300 × 28. We use
both the validation set and training set from NTIRE2022 as training data.
1.2

Testing Datasets

We randomly select five scenes of size 256 × 256 × 28 in each dataset as testing
datasets. The test scenes used in the simulation are shown in Fig. 1. For the real
dataset, we use 5 scenes with 660 × 660 × 28 resolution captured in TSA-Net [9].
The reference RGB images and measurements are shown in Fig. 2.
