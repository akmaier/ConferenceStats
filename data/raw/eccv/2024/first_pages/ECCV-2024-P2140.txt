Implementation Details

We provide a detailed setting of our method. The loss function consists of
weighted sum of free space loss Lf s , middle of the truncation region loss LT -m ,
tail of the truncation region loss LT -t , depth loss Ld and color loss Lc such as:
  \mathcal {L} = \lambda _{fs}\mathcal {L}_{fs} + \lambda _{T\text {-}m}\mathcal {L}_{T\text {-}m} + \lambda _{T\text {-}t}\mathcal {L}_{T\text {-}t} + \lambda _{d}\mathcal {L}_{d} + \lambda _{c}\mathcal {L}_{c} 

(1)

where {λf s , λT -m , λT -t , λd , λc } are the weighting coefficients. We set λf s = 5, λT -m
= 200, λT -t = 10, λd = 0.1, λc = 10 in mapping process. During tracking process, we set λf s = 10, λT -m = 200, λT -t = 50, λd = 1, λc = 5. For the scenes
from Replica [4], we perform 15 optimization iterations for mapping. For camera tracking, 8 optimization iterations are performed. We perform 30 iterations
for mapping and 16 iterations for tracking in ScanNet [1], TUM RGB-D [5].
We initiate the learning rate of 0.001 for the camera poses, i.e. rotation and
translation {R, t}, in the first 8 iterations, and set 0.0005 in the remaining 8
iterations during tracking in ScanNet [1] and TUM RGB-D [5]. We use a learning rate of 0.001 for the MLP decoders and a learning rate of 0.005 for the axis
features. All learnable parameters are optimized by Adam [2]. The number of
randomly selected rays is 4000 in mapping and 2000 in tracking for Replica [4]
and ScanNet [1] scenes. For TUM RGB-D [5], we choose random 5000 rays for
each iteration.

Effect of Representation Methods

To verify our presentation capabilities, we perform scene representation using
tri-plane, CP decomposition, and our SA decomposition as the tensorial radius
field. In our experiments, we use two-layer MLP for geometric decoders MLPg
and appearance decoders MLPa . MLPg and MLPa have 16 and 128 hidden layer
channels, respectively. For rendering view-dependent color, we employ positional
encoding γ to appearance feature fa and view direction vd . Geometric feature fg
and appearance feature fa each have 16 and 48 feature channels. So factorized
tensorial radiance field can be expressed as
