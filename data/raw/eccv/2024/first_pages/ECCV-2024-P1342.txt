Gradient Derivation of MDWA-Loss for Unactivated
AUs (Negative Samples)

To better understand the principle of MDWA-Loss, we analyze the gradients of
unactivated AUs with respect to the output Z ∈ RN from the classifier. Z is
further processed through a Sigmoid activation function to obtain the predicted
probability p ∈ (0, 1)N and this process can be expressed as
  p = \frac {1}{1 + e^{-Z}}. 

(1)

For simplicity, we omit the index i corresponding to AUs.
The part related to unactivated AUs in MDWA-Loss can be extracted as
  f = - p_{m} ^{\gamma } \log (1 - p_{m}), 

(2)

where pm = max(p − m, 0) and m ∈ [0, 1] is the truncation margin. Substituting
pm yields the segmented f as
  f = \begin {cases} 0, & p < m \\ - (p - m)^{\gamma } \log (1 - p + m), & p \geq m \end {cases}. 

(3)

df
When p < m, the gradient of unactivated AUs dZ
= 0.
When p ≥ m, the gradient of unactivated AUs can be calculated through

  \label {dfdZ} \frac {\mathrm {d} f}{\mathrm {d} Z} = \frac {\partial f}{\partial p} \frac {\partial p}{\partial Z}.
