Comparison with our Consistency Baseline

Tables S1 and S2 present quantitative comparisons between the proposed domain
adaptation method (MC-PanDA) and our consistency baseline (BC). We observe
that our method consistently outperforms the baseline, which suggests a clear
advantage of the proposed contributions. The advantage is the largest in Table S1
where the supervision is available only for the synthetic domain.
