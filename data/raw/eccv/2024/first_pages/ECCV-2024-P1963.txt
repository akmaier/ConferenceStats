Performance test with highly sparse data sampled from
ScanNet200 dataset

We show in Table 2 that even though extreme data sparsity harms the performance of the model, self-distillation still outperforms auto-labeling when it
comes to recalling unknown classes while being significantly better in alleviating
catastrophic forgetting compared to exemplar replay. We hypothesize that using
a frozen model as a teacher for self-distillation increases the robustness of the
model when dealing with highly sparse data, as it continues to generate highquality pseudo-labels throughout the training. On the other hand, auto labeling
shows inferior performance due to its reliance on the model being trained for
pseudo-label generation, which results in low-quality pseudo-label during training due to performance degradation resulting from extreme data sparsity.
