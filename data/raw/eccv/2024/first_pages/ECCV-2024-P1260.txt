evaluation was conducted on the Cityscapes (‘C’) or IDD (‘I’) dataset. The harmonic
mean (‘H’), representing adaptation ability across the two domains, is considered as
the primary metric. Bold text highlights the best harmonic mean results, while underlined text indicates the second-best results. ‘†’ signifies only merging backbones while
