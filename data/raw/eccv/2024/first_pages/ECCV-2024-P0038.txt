Abstract. In this work, we address the task of point cloud denoising using a novel framework adapting Diﬀusion Schrödinger bridges
to unstructured data like point sets. Unlike previous works that predict point-wise displacements from point features or learned noise
distributions, our method learns an optimal transport plan between
paired point clouds. In experiments on object datasets such as the
PU-Net dataset and real-world datasets like ScanNet++ and ARKitScenes, P2P-Bridge improves by a notable margin over existing methods. Although our method demonstrates promising results utilizing solely
point coordinates, we demonstrate that incorporating additional features like RGB information and point-wise DINOV2 features further
improves the results.Code and pretrained networks are available at
https://github.com/matvogel/P2P-Bridge.
Keywords: Point Cloud Denoising · Diﬀusion Models · Schrödinger
