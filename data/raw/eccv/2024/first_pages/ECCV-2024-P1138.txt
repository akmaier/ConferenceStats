visual features. To ensure that each extracted proposal feature is aligned with
the mask proposals, we generate the attention bias B simultaneously with the
mask proposals. This bias is then fed into the self-attention mechanism of the
Semantic Decoder to extract features corresponding to the masks, as illustrated
