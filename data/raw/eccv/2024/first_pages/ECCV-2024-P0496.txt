More Experiments On New Split

StreamMapNet [6] found an overlap of over 54% locations between the training
and validation sets, including nuScenes [1] and Argoverse2 [5], and therefore
proposed a new way to split the datasets. To verify the generalization of our
method, we provide the results for the two datasets at the 30 m perceptual
range under the new split.
