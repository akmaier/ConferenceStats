2.1

Hyperparameters

The resolution of the road surface mesh is set to 0.1m, with a mesh range extending 15m on each side of the vehicle trajectory. Each vertex stores coordinates
p, semantics sem, and implicit color encoding lc . The coordinates are in three
dimensions (x, y, z), semantics include five categories: lane marking, curb, manhole, road, and background. The color encoding has a dimension of 16. (x, y) is
encoded into a 22-dimensional coordinate vector. The elevation residual MLP
consists of eight layers with a width of 128, using the ReLU activation function.
The color MLPs consist of two layers with a width of 16, with the ReLU activation function in the intermediate layer and the Sigmoid activation function in
the output layer.
