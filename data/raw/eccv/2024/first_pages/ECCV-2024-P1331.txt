the performance variations according to the hash code dimension L. When the

smaller L effectively handles intra-class variation, while a model with larger L has
high representation capability [2]. To demonstrate the resilience of our method
to the hash code dimension, we vary the value of L from 8 to 16. Our method exhibits robust performance for the known classes regardless of the code dimension.
