Additional Experiments and Discussions

1.1

Motion Estimation Comparisons

To further demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed TLXNet in motion estimation, we compare our motion estimation modules with existing baselines. We
select the warping-based interpolation branch in TimeLens [4] and CBMNet [1],
denoting TimeLens Flow and CBMNet Flow. We degrade our model by directly
interpolating with the flow coming from TLXNet and the one after up-sampling,
naming TLXNet Flow and TLXNe Up-Flow.
We provide numeric comparisons between different flow-based interpolation
branches on GOPRO [5] dataset for ×16 interpolation in Tab. 1. As shown, our
TimeLens-XL module is effective in estimating large motions, which drives our
network to outperform the full TimeLens model even if only flow is utilized.
