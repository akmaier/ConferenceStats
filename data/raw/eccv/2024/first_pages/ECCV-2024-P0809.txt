Proof of Theorem 2

Proof. For any i = 1, 2, · · · , n1 , j = 1, 2, · · · , n2 , and k = 1, 2, · · · , n3 , we have
  & \|\mathcal {X}(i,j,k) - \mathcal {X}(i,j,k-1)\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ = & \|((\mathcal {A} \bigtriangleup \mathcal {B}) \times _3 f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}))(i,j) - ((\mathcal {A} \bigtriangleup \mathcal {B}) \times _3 f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)}))(i,j)\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ = & \|((\mathcal {A} \bigtriangleup \mathcal {B}) \times _3 (f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})))(i,j)\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \|\mathcal {A} \bigtriangleup \mathcal {B}\|_{\ell _{1}} \|f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \|\mathcal {A}\|_{\ell _{1}} \|\mathcal {B}\|_{\ell _{1}} \|f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \gamma _1 \gamma _2 \|f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})\|_{\ell _{1}}.

Since σ(·) is Lipschitz continuous, thus, for any x, y, we have
  \|\sigma (x)-\sigma (y)\|_{\ell _{1}}\leq \delta \|x-y\|_{\ell _{1}}. 
Thus,
  & \|f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - f_{\theta }(\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})\|_{\ell _{1}}\\ = & \|\textbf {H}_l(\sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k)}))) - \textbf {H}_l(\sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k-1)}))) \|_{\ell _{1}} \\ = & \|\textbf {H}_l(\sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k)})) - \sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k-1)}))) \|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \|\textbf {H}_l\|_{\ell _{1}}\|\sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k)})) - \sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})) \|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \gamma _3 \|\sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k)})) - \sigma (\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})) \|_{\ell _{1}}\\ \leq & \gamma _3 \delta \|\textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - \textbf {H}_{l-1}\cdots \sigma (\textbf {H}_1\textbf {z}_{(k-1)}) \|_{\ell _{1}}\\ & \cdots \\ \leq & \gamma _3^{l} \delta ^{l-1}\|\textbf {z}_{(k)} - \textbf {z}_{(k-1)} \|_{\ell _{1}} = \frac {\gamma _3^{l} \delta ^{l-1}}{n_3} ,

where {Hi }li=1 are the learnable weights of the MLP.
Combining the above inequalities, we have
  \|\mathcal {X}(i,j,\textbf {z}_{(k)}) - \mathcal {X}(i,j,\textbf {z}_{(k-1)})\|_{\ell _{1}} \leq \frac { \gamma _1 \gamma _2\gamma _3^{l} \delta ^{l-1}}{n_3}.
