Overview
In this material, we first introduce more implementation details regarding the
spatial linear layer and relative position encoding in Sec. 1. More ablation studies
are then conducted in Sec. 2 to further investigate the HiT-SR design. Following that, we provide more visual comparisons in challenging scenes to validate
the superiority of our proposed methods in Sec. 3. Finally, we summarize the
strengths and limitations of our approach and discuss potential future directions
in Sec. 4.
