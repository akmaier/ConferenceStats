Supplementary Material

Appendix
– In Appendix 1, we present performance-computation comparisons with our
EDAFormer and other transformer-based state-of-the-art models.
– In Appendix 2, we provide the computational analysis of our method in the
attention block.
– In Appendix 3, we present fair comparisons of semantic segmentation decoder
structures with the same backbone.
– In Appendix 4, we present the comparisons of computations and performance
on various reduction ratios.
– In Appendix 5, we present the FPS comparison with other segmentation
models.
– In Appendix 6, we provide the in-depth analysis for the effectiveness of our
embedding-free structure.
– In Appendix 7, we provide the additional visualizations of features before
and after applying our ISR.
– In Appendix 8, we provide qualitative results compared with the proposed
model and previous state-of-the-art models on ADE20K, Cityscapes and
COCO-Stuff datasets.
