A.1

Further Comparisons

Comparison on more real-world datasets. We further compare our method
with the top two baselines from Tab. 2 of the main paper and the latest model on
additional real-world datasets, such as GT-Rain [2], O-HAZE [1], and URHI [10].
In the case of URHI dataset, since the ground-truth images are not provided,
a paired data from O-HAZE is used for adaptation where they share the same
weather type and our method allows the support set to be obtained at different
times or locations but under the same weather degradation pattern with the
query. We also added the non-reference metrics such as NIQE and BRISQUE.
Tab. A and Fig. A show that our method consistently outperforms the baselines
on the additional real-world datasets quantitatively and qualitatively.
