Visualization of Foreground Proposals and Deformable
Offsetting

We demonstrate the chosen crucial positions in which we construct the sparse
HR BEV queries in Fig. 1. As shown in the middle of Fig. 1, the red spots
indicate the positions that are likely to contain objects. The initial proposals
are close to each other. After applying the deformable offsetting, the proposals
dispersed to the regions of interest and covered broader regions, as demonstrated
in the right of Fig. 1.
