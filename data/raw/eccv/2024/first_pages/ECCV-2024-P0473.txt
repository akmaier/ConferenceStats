Network Details

In this section, we present network details of the diffusion prior model and triplane diffusion model.
Diffusion Prior Model The network architecture of the diffusion prior model is
a MLP with skip connections between layers at different depths as shown in Fig.
1. The diffusion backbone consists of multiple ResBlocks. In each block, image
embedding is injected into the block through concatenation, and the timestep
embedding is injected through addition.
