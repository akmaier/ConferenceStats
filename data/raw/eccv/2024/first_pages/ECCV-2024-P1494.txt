A.1

Details of the Dataset

We create a high-fidelity 3D head dataset for research use, containing 100 subjects, and each of them is rigged into 52 blendshapes bases. The 3D heads are
rendered by 72 head-centric virtual cameras that cover 3 pitch angles and 24
horizontal rotation angles. Fig. 1 shows a subset of views of a single subject
with one expression from our dataset. Fig. 9 showcases the 52 standard expressions generated in our dataset, consistent with the blendshapes bases defined by
ARKit [1]. Fig. 2 presents more identities in our dataset.
