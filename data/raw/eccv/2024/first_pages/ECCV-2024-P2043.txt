Introduction of CorrNet3D

CorrNet3D [8] is an unsupervised end-to-end learning framework to learn the
dense correspondence between 3D shapes based on the deformation-like reconstruction to release the need for annotated data. Specifically, as shown in Fig.
1, CorrNet3D mainly consists of three modules, i.e., deep feature embedding,
correspondence indicator and symmetric deformer. It first learns the pointwise
feature representations of a pair of raw point clouds, and then employs indicator
to generate a learnable correspondence matrix used to permute the input pair.
Finally, based on the reconstruction loss and additional regularization terms,
the symmetric deformer transforms the two permuted point clouds to each other
to construct the unsupervised learning of the correspondence. Note that Fig. 1
comes from CorrNet3D [8].
