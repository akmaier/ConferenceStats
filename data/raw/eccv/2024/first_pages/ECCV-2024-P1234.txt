In our proposed image deblurring network, we adopt the baseline conv block
mentioned in [1] as our fundamental block. As shown in Figure A1 (a), it contains
layernorm layer, convolution layer, GELU layer and Channel Attention layer
(CA). Skip connection is used to reduce the problem of gradient propagation. The
decoder includes pixelshuffle layer to upsample the features, then uses baseline
conv Block to reconstruct the image.
