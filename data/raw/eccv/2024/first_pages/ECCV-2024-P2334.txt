As previously elucidated in Sec. 3.2, we introduce the parameter as a measure
of tolerance for errors occurring in traditional LDD utilizing FTL. In Sec. 3.2,
we illustrate the implementation of = 2 in our experiments. In this subsection,
we additionally explore the eﬀect of varying within the range of [0, 5], allowing

traditional methods have commonly employed NS = 50 as the optimal parameter
for experiments conducted on CIFAR-10 with IPC=1. Consequently, we extend
our experimental investigations to specifically focus on the parameter NS = 50.
