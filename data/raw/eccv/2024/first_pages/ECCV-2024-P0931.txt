A

Conceptual Comparisons

We provide a conceptual comparison to highlight the differences from existing
methods. Se-CFF [7] fuses multi-density event stacks within a single timeframe.
Other methods [6, 10] use recurrent architecture to aggregate feature-level temporal information. As shown in Table A1, conceptually, our method explicitly
retrieves temporal information based on flow rather than relying on an implicit
feature-level approach. This approach offers the advantage of reusing the computed flow in both feature and cost volume. Furthermore, a key difference lies
in our cost volume aggregation, which is essential and task-dependent for stereo
matching.
