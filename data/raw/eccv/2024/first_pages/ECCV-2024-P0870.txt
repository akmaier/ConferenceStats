The Derivation Details in Deep Unfolding

Derivation of Eq. (5) : Follow the Eq. (4) in the paper, the GAP alternatively
solving two sub-problems. The sub-problem of x can be written as
  \xv ^{k+1}&=\underset {\xv }{\arg \min } \frac {1}{2}\|\xv -\vv ^{k} \|_{2}^{2}+\frac {\lambda }{2}\left \|\Av \xv -\boldsymbol {y} \right \|_{2}^{2}, \label {eq:prox_x}

(S1)

where λ is a penalty factor. Then get the Lagrangian function of Eq. (S1) is
  \label {eq:gap-lag} L(\xv , \lambda ) = \frac {1}{2}|| \xv - \vv ^k ||_2^2 + \lambda (\Av \xv -\yv ).

(S2)

Then the optimal conditions of Eq. (S2) are
  \label {eq:gap-01} \frac {\partial }{\partial \xv } L(\xv , \lambda )=(\xv -\vv ^k) + \Av ^\top \lambda =0, \\ \label {eq:gap-02} \frac {\partial }{\partial \lambda } L(\xv , \lambda )=\Av \xv -\yv =0.
(S4)
According to Eq. (S3) and (S4), we have
  \begin {aligned} \lambda = - (\Av \Av ^\top )^{-1}(\yv - \Av \vv ^k). \label {eq:lambda} \end {aligned}

(S5)

Replace the Eq. (S5) in the Eq. (S4)
  \xv ^{(k+1)} = \vv ^k + \Av ^\top (\Av \Av ^\top )^{-1}(\yv - \Av \vv ^k).
The Eq. (S2) is the Eq. (5) in the paper.
