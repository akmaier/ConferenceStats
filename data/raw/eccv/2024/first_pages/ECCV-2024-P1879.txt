In the supplementary material, we first provide more implementation details

the proposed WA-SAM attention in Sec. 2. We back our comparison against the
typical shadow removal image datasets in Sec. 3, providing additional samples

method.

Architecture details

Fig. 1 provides a graphical representation of the block design used in the encoderdecoder stages of the proposed IFBlend model. As these blocks solve different
sub-tasks, they are characterized by different designs. Thus, the block processing
low-frequency information has a simple design, based on a strided 4×4 convolution, followed by BatchNorm, activation function, and a Dropout layer. Naturally, the strided convolution performing down-scaling in the encoder stage will
be replaced by the counterpart transposed convolution in the decoder stage,
performing the upscaling of the propagated features.
