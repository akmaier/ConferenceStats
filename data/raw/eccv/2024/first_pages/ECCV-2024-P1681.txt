Inference time analysis. As outlined in the main paper, one of the main
benefits of training CoDE from scratch is that this allows for more freedom
in architectural choices. This ultimately results in a smaller and more efficient
model compared to state-of-the-art detectors.

single image at a time (“single sample”) and the latter considering the batching of
