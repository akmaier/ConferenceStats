Societal Impact

DynamiCrafter, an innovative open-domain image animation technology, holds
the potential to transform the content creation and film industries by providing
unparalleled flexibility and creativity, thus offering significant commercial benefits. Traditional image animation methods are labor-intensive and expensive,
but DynamiCrafter can ease these challenges by allowing designers to animate
images using text prompts. Additionally, DynamiCrafter can potentially revolutionize the education industry by generating unique animations from book
illustrations for teaching purposes.
However, it is essential to recognize that DynamiCrafter also serves as a
dual-use technology with inherent societal risks. Similar to previous generative
models like VideoCrafter1 [4], VideoComposer [19] and SVD [2], DynamiCrafter
inherits the implicit knowledge embedded in the pre-trained model (e.g., Stable
Diffusion and VideoCrafter1) and the training dataset (e.g., WebVid10M [1] and
LAION [16]). Potential issues may involve the propagation of social biases and
the generation of offensive content.
As a research-oriented project for animating open-domain images using video
diffusion priors, DynamiCrafter’s main focus is on scientific exploration and establishing proof of concept.
