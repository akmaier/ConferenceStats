A. Datasets Settings
In the main paper, we follow the GCD protocol [12] to determine the number of
known classes in the dataset and the number of labeled data samples selected
from these classes. We conduct extensive experiments across four generic image
classification datasets, namely, CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 [3], ImageNet-100, and
ImageNet-1K [1], and four fine-grained image classification datasets, specifically,
CUB [13], Stanford Cars [2], Oxford Pets [8], and Flowers102 [7]. Detailed dataset
information is provided in Table 1.
