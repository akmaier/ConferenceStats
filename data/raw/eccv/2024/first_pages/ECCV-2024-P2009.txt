Additional Background

Face recognition (FR) [17,26] is the process of identifying and verifying the identity of individuals based on their facial features. FR includes several computer
vision processes: (1) face detection [28, 33], which is the process of locating the
pixels that contain a face in an examined image; (2) face alignment [22,35], which
is the process of aligning the geometric structures of a face, such as the landmarks of the nose, mouth, and eyes, with those in an examined image; (3) face
verification [2,36], which is the process of determining whether an examined pair
of face images belong to the same individual; and (4) face identification [16, 23],
which is the process of identifying the identity of an individual in a given image
out of a given set of identities. When an input image is fed into the FR system,
the image is processed by performing face detection and face alignment. Then,
the individual in the processed image is identified (face identification) by iteratively performing face verification across all the identities’ images in the dataset.
In this work, we focused on performing an external information leakage (EIL)
attack that targets the face verification model.
