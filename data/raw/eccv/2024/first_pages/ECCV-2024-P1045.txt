More Experimental Results

1.1

Grid-based Distance Fields V.S. Continuous Distance Fields on
SDFs

In Sec. 4.5 of the main paper, we demonstrated the effectiveness of our pointbased AutoEncoder design for shape embedding of UDFs compared to the gridbased approach [2]. Here, we further highlight the advantages of continuous
distance fields in learning SDFs.
Specifically, we follow SDFusion [2] to first make the car objects in ShapeNet [1]
dataset watertight, and compare our point-based AutoEncoder with grid-based
AutoEncoder used in SDFusion for learning SDFs. As shown in Fig. 1, our
pipeline can be adjusted to learn continuous signed distance field and produce
higher-quality surfaces than the previous method.
