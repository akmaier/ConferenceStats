Appendix

Overview. In this section, we first introduce more implementation details and
training settings of our model. To further substantiate the generalizability and
effectiveness of our DAQ design, we integrate the DAQ into an alternative video
segmentation framework, GenVIS [2], and conduct experiments on the OVIS [4]
dataset. Additionally, we provide more ablation studies to verify the effectiveness
of the detailed components of the DAQ design. Lastly, we present a collection of
qualitative results and supplementary video files to demonstrate the full performance of our method in challenging scenes.
