Overview
Due to page limitation of the main submission, we provide additional results and
analysis in this Supp, including:
– Our skip connection removal study on Vision Transformers (Sec. 1).
– Detailed analysis and additional empirical results for RepVGG [8] under MI
study (Sec. 2).
– Additional discussion and results for our MI-resilient architectures (Sec. 3).
– User study (Sec. 4).
– Discussion on architectures without skip connection (Sec. 5)
– The detailed experimental setting for skip connection removal (Sec. 6) and
MI attack (Sec. 7).
– Further discussion on related works (Sec. 8).
– The limitation (Sec. 9) and ethical consideration (Sec. 10) of our work.
