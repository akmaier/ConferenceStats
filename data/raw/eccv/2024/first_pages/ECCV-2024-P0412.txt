Before we compute the ZMP, we first compute the body Center of Mass (CoM)
by adapting the CoM formulation of Tripathi et al . [4] to dynamic humans.
For every sequence, we use their body part segmentation and the differentiable
“close-translate-fill” [4] to compute per-part volumes V Pi by splitting the mesh in
the first frame into 10 parts. Using the per-part volumes, the CoM is calculated
for time instance t, as a volume weighted-average of NU = 6890 mesh vertex
points.
