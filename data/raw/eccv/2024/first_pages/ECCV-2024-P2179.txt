Wrinkle Variation Given Different Noise

Our wrinkle generation component is based on diffusion model, which produces
different results given different Gaussian noise. For the same base mesh, it can
generate slightly different but always plausible wrinkles by varying the Gaussian
noise as shown in Figure 1.
