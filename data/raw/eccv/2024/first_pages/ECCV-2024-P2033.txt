Benchmarks

one proposed by [4,6], cannot adequately assess our method. Therefore, we base
our work on DreamBooth [6], maintaining consistency with its settings, and use
30 categories of objects, including both objects and living subjects. In order to
generate scene images, we consider various indoor and outdoor lighting scenarios
