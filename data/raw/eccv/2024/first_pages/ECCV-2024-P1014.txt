Derivation of Eq. 12

As discussed in Eq. 12 of Sec. 3.3 in the main paper, we apply the classifier-free
guidance (CFG) on noise estimation with two conditions: the textual prompt c
and the intermediate texture map Ûti . The original text guided diffusion model
targets at learning P (xt |c) where xt denotes the noisy latent feature at time
step t. Now we extend the target of the original diffusion model to P (xit |c, ÛtN ),
which has an additional condition ÛtN to constrain the generated xit to be
view-consistent. We assume P (c|xit , ÛtN ) = P (c|xit ). Following Bayes’ theorem,
P (xit |c, ÛtN ) can be reformulated as
  \setcounter {equation}{13} P(x_t^i|c, \hat {U}_{t}^N) = \frac {P(x_t^i) P(c|x_t^i) P(\hat {U}_{t}^N|x_t^i)}{P(c, \hat {U}_{t}^N)}. \label {eqn:bayes} 

(13)

By taking logarithm on both sides of the above equation, we get
  \begin {aligned} \log (P(x_t^i|c, \hat {U}_{t}^N)) =& \log (P(x_t^i)) + \log (P(c|x_t^i))\\ &+ \log (P(\hat {U}_{t}^N|x_t^i)) - log(P(c, \hat {U}_{t}^N)). \label {eqn:log} \end {aligned} 

(14)
