A

Summary

In the supplementary material, we first provide a thorough procedure for updating
the variational parameters in Section B, and then introduce some knowledge
related to the distributions used in our paper in Section C. The demonstration
of properties of the truncated Dirichlet Process (DP) is included in Section
D. Moreover, we show additional experimental results in Section E on the
effectiveness of our method and reveal more details about our experimental
implementation and time complexity in Section F. Lastly, a detailed description
of the baseline methods is given in Section G.
