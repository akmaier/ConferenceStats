A

Validation of Normality Prior

To further confirm the applicability of the normality prior to videos beyond
standard benchmarks, we collected 50 abnormal videos from YouTube using
keywords like “fighting” and “violent acts”, and observed that 98% of the start
and end frames in these videos are normal. We included the links to these videos
at the end of the supplementary material.
