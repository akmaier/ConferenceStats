Computational Complexity of Multipartite-based Dynamic Masked
Convolution Building on the technique outlined in Section 4.2, Equation 16
and Equation 17 provide an instantiation of intra-node BayesNet via multipartitebased dynamic masked convolution. Here we further provide a PyTorch6 -style
pseudo-code implementation in Algorithm 1 to clarify the implementation details. Measuring with MAC, the complexity of the multipartite-based dynamic
masked convolution could be calculated by counting the MACs in convolution
operations by:
