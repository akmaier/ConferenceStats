Supplementary Material
In this supplementary material, we provide the following elements:
– A description of our method to improve the surface reconstruction from
SuGaR [2].
– Additional details about our strategy to initialize the Frosting layer.
– A description of our method to automatically adjust Gaussians’ parameters
when deforming, editing, or animating our representation.
– Two ablation studies demonstrating (a) how our method to improve the surface reconstruction from SuGaR [2] helps to enhance rendering performance,
and (b) how our strategy to compute an adaptive thickness of the Frosting
later is necessary to reach top rendering performance.
We also provide a video that offers an overview of the approach and showcases
additional qualitative results. Specifically, the video demonstrates how Frosting
can be used to edit, combine or animate Gaussian Splatting representations.

Improving surface reconstruction

We improve the surface reconstruction method from SuGaR by proposing a way
to automatically adjust the hyperparameter of the Poisson surface reconstruction [3] stage.
Poisson surface reconstruction first recovers an underlying occupancy field
χ : R3 7→ [0, 1] and applies a marching algorithm on χ, which allows for a much
better mesh reconstruction than the density function. This approach allows for
high scalability as the marching algorithm is applied only in voxels located close
to the point cloud.
