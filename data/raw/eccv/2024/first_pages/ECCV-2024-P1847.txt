Datasets and Settings

Datasets. Our approach is evaluated on 9 person ReID datasets, including 5 large-scale datasets: Market1501
(M) [12], MSMT17 (MS) [9], CUHK02 (C2) [3], CUHK03 (C3) [4], and CUHK-SYSU (CS) [10], and 4 small-scale
datasets: PRID [2], GRID [8], VIPeR [1], and iLIDs [13]. The statistics of them are shown in Table 1. We do not
use DukeMTMC-reID (D) [14] since it has been retracted. Other datasets are acquired from official and reputable
sources, ensuring that they have been made publicly accessible for research purposes in accordance with the terms
and conditions set by their respective providers.
Evaluation Protocols. For more clarity, we list the three evaluation protocols proposed in our main paper
in Table 2. For Protocol-1, all images including both training and testing sets from four large-scale datasets (i.e.,
M, MS, C2, C3) are used for training, and the four small-scale datasets (i.e., PRID, GRID, VIPeR, iLIDs) are used
for testing. For Protocol-2 and Protocol-3, following the leave-one-out setting in [11], CS is excluded as a target
domain due to its images all come from one camera view. Additionally, under Protocol-2, only the training sets of
the source domains are used for training, while all images are exploited under Protocol-3.
