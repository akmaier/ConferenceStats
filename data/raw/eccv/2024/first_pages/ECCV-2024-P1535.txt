Additional Results of Long-time Videos

This long-term video dataset [1] comprises three videos with a total frame count
exceeding 7000 frames. Longer videos necessitate models to maintain sustained
memory of target features. We validated the performance of the OneVOS-D
version on this dataset as shown in Table. 2. The results demonstrate that,
owing to the ability of Dynamic Tokens Selector to adaptively choose the most
pertinent features and construct a dynamic memory, the model maintains highprecision segmentation performance even for extremely long videos.
