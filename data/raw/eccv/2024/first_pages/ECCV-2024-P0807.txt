The training parameters of YOLOv9 are shown in Table 1. We fully follow
the settings of YOLOv7 AF [10], which is to use SGD optimizer to train 500
epochs. We rst warm-up for 3 epochs and only update the bias during the
warm-up stage. Next we step down from the initial learning rate 0.01 to 0.0001
in linear decay manner, and the data augmentation settings are listed in the
bottom part of Table 1. We shut down mosaic data augmentation operations on
the last 15 epochs.
