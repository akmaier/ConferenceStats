Further Details on Dynamic Retraining-Updating

For the student’s prediction uncertainty u ∈ RB×Q×C of an image batch xb ∈
Dhis , we simply take the average of elements in u to get the scalar value that
roughly represents the uncertainty value. A lower uncertainty value signifies
higher adaptability to the target domain. To compare uncertainty feedback Ut =
{ut } of the current student with Uhis = {uhis } from the historical student over
the historical data Dhis , we compare each pair of elements in Ut and Uhis . If the
probability that elements in Ut are smaller than the corresponding elements in
Uhis exceeds 0.5, it indicates the current student model has evolved.
