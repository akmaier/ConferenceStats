Discussed in main Section 4.1 (Details).
For completeness, fairness, and robustness, we also assessed methods using
recent metrics designed for open-set scenarios: Area Under the Open-Set Classification Rate (OSCR) curve (AUOSCR) [2] in Tab. 1 and Open Area Under
the ROC Curve (OpenAUC) [23] in Tab. 2. As explained in the main paper, neither metric offers a threshold to evaluate operational performance. Nevertheless,
PreMax still outperforms all methods on both metrics/architectures.
