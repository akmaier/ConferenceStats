Outline
This supplementary material provides additional analysis and visual examples.
It is organized as follows:
– Section A analyzes the consequences of using an incorrect P3-Mask to deobfuscate protected facial images.
– Section B reports the quantitative comparison of image quality with existing
methods.
– Section C provides ablation studies of Chameleon to understand its contributing components.
– Section D analyzes different choices of hyperparameters.
– Section E includes discussions on partially protected gallery datasets and
future directions.
– Section F provides additional results and visual examples excluded from the
main paper due to the space limit.

A

FR Service Usability

We have shown in Section 6.2 that the P3-Mask of the user can be used to
enable FR on protected images by de-obfuscation. The same mask must be
used. Otherwise, the FR accuracy cannot be restored, and the facial images can
be corrupted. Table 10 provides visual examples of the correct (5th column)
and incorrect unmasking (6th column). The correctly unmasked facial images
are visually identical to the unprotected ones (3rd column). The reconstruction
error is bounded by the hyperparameter ϵ in Equation 2. The incorrect key for
unmasking can significantly worsen the image quality, which can be a reason for
the additional performance degradation in Table 6.
