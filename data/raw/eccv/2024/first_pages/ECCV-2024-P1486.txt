Overview
In this supplementary material we provide the following:
– A detailed review of preliminaries involved, including motion field, differential geometry and event-based normal flow (Sec. I).
– Additional details about differential homography (Sec. II).
– Details of dataset and evaluation metrics utilized (Sec. III).
– Implementation details of the proposed algorithms (Sec. IV).
– More evaluations on the proposed methods, including an analysis of numerical stability, and the performance of the continuous-time nonlinear solver in
textured scenes. (Sec. V).

I

Supplement to Preliminaries

We supplement Sec. 3 of the main paper by providing a detailed review on
three important concepts, including motion field (Sec. I.A), differential geometry
across multiple views (Sec. I.B), and event-based normal flow (Sec. I.C).
