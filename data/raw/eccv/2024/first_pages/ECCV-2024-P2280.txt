More Details in Zero-Shot Image Restoration

Typical image restoration tasks usually have simple A and A† , we give some
examples following:
Colorization. A = [1/3, 1/3, 1/3] converts each RGB channel pixel [r, g, b]T into
a grayscale value [r/3+g/3+g/3]. A simple pseudo-inverse A† is A† = [1, 1, 1]T ,
that satisies AA† A = A.
2
Super Resolution. For SR with scale n, we can set A ∈ R1×n as a average2
pooling operator [1/n2 , · · · , 1/n2 ]. A simple pseudo-inverse A† ∈ Rn ×1 is A† =
[1, · · · , 1]T .
Inpainting. For A is a mask operator, simply let A† = A, we can have AA† A =
A.

Experiment Setting Details in Image Generation

Evaluation Metric. We employ the FID score as the metric in most experiments. We compute the FID score between 50k generated samples and training
images. On CelebA HQ we compute 30k generated samples and training images
due to the dataset only containing 30k images.
Dataset Details. For STL-10, we follow the procedure in AutoGAN and DCVAE, and resize the STL-10 images to 32 × 32.
VAE architecture. The backbone similar to that proposed in FastGAN [35] is
used in experiments.
Ablations in Tab. 1. The VAE and Flow use the same architecture as EC-VAE
and EC-Flow, except the EBM is not needed in VAE and Flow.
