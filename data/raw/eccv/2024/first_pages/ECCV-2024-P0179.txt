In Fig. 2 of the main paper, we show the details of training event vision model
and the design of our proposed two triggers. For training a backdoored event
vision model, we need first to generate some poisoned samples by Fig. 2 (c) or
(d) of the main paper. Then, we can follow the pipeline shown in Fig. S1 to train
a victim model and evaluate the attacking performance. The backdoored model
can correctly classify benign event streams, such as the motorbike and airplane
shown in the first row of Fig. S1. However, once the attacker injects the specific
trigger into event samples, this model will output the predetermined label. For
instance, the poisoned motorbike and ferry (in the second row of Fig. S1) are all
misclassified as accordions. This kind of potential risk could severely impact the
performance of autonomous driving systems.
