A

More Stable Diffusion Variants

We apply AccDiffusion on other LDMs, specifically Stable Diffusion 1.5 (SD
1.5) [5] and Stable Diffusion 2.1 [6] (SD 2.1). As shown in Fig. 1, AccDiffusion
successfully generates higher-resolution images without repetition. It is important to note that the results of AccDiffusion depend on the prior knowledge
of LDMs, and the performance of SD 1.5 and SD 2.1 is inferior to SDXL [4].
Therefore, the fidelity of their results are less astonishing than those on SDXL.
