Defining Allowed Perturbation Set S

Conventionally face detection and alignment are performed before training the
FR module, ensuring the consistency of landmarks’ positions across all training
instances according to the alignment template. Therefore, the initial positions of
landmarks (up , vp ) match that of the alignment template. Additionally, one can
determine the final positions of landmarks after applying the invertible affine
transformation Tθ , as described in Equation 4 of Manuscript. Consequently, the
displacement vector’s upper bound f is calculable by considering the transformation components’ upper bounds, namely the maximum permissible rotation,
translation, and scaling:
  \overline {\mathbf {f}}_p = (u_p^{'}-u_p,v_p^{'}-v_p); \quad (\haxis ', \vaxis ') = T^{-1}_{\overline {\theta }}(P_{\haxis }(j),P_{\vaxis }(i)),

(1)

where θ denotes the upper bound for the transformation parameters, I.e., maximum rotation, scaling and translation.
In the same way, the displacement vector is calculated during the training
for defining S:
  {\mathbf {f}}_p^{\theta } = (u_p^{'}-u_p,v_p^{'}-v_p); \quad (\haxis ', \vaxis ') = T^{-1}_{{\theta }}(P_{\haxis }(j),P_{\vaxis }(i)).

(2)

Finally, f p = ||f p ||2 and we define the allowed transformation using Equation 8
of Manuscript without requiring landmark estimation.
