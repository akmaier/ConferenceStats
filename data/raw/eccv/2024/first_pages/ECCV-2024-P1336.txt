A

Loss function

The overall loss function is defined as:
  L =&\sum _{i=1}^{M} (\lambda _{cls}L_{cls}^{i}+\lambda _{coord}L_{coord}^{i} +\lambda _{ang}L_{ang}^{i} +\lambda _{ras}L_{ras}^{i}),

(1)

where Lcls , Lcoord , Lang , and Lras are classification loss, coordinate loss, angle
loss, and rasterization loss respectively. And λcls , λcoord , λang , and λras are the
corresponding loss weights.
The vertex label classification loss is defined as:
  L_{cls}^{i} = -\frac {1}{N}\sum _{n=1}^{N}L_{ce}(l_{n}^{i},l_{n}^{\sigma (i)}),

(2)

where Lce indicates a binary cross-entropy.
The coordinate loss for coordinate regression is defined as:
  L_{coord}^{i} = \frac {1}{N}\sum _{m=1}^{N}d(v_{m}^{i},v_{m}^{\sigma (i)}), i \leq M_{gt}.

(3)

The angle loss calculates the angle difference between two vertices. Since the
vertices are densely sampled and angles show the spatial position relationship
between adjacent vertices, the introduction of angle loss is crucial for better
supervision of the vertex spatial position:
  L_{ang}^{i}=\frac {1}{N}\sum _{n=1}^{N}|ang_{n}^{i} - ang_{n}^{\sigma (i)}|, i \leq M_{gt},

(4)

σ(i)

where angni and angn are the cosine values of the vertex angle in the ground
truth and matching polygon.
The rasterization loss computes the Dice loss [3] between rasterized images
of ground truth and our predictions, supervising the polygon sequences from an
image perspective:
  L_{ras} = Dice(ras(R_{i}, R_{\sigma (i)})), i \leq M_{gt},

(5)

where ras(·) means the rasterized image from room polygon sequences with a
differentiable rasterizer proposed in [2].
