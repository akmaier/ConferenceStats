Detailed Background of Diffusion Models

A.1

Diffusion Models

In this section, we will provide a detailed background of diffusion probabilistic
models (DPMs) [1, 11]. DPMs usually contain a forward diffusion process that
gradually adds noise to the clean data and a backward denoising process that
progressively removes the noise to obtain the cleaned data. The diffusion process
can be defined either discretely [1] or continuously [11]. We will focus on the latter
since continuous DPMs are usually used in the context of DPM samplers [6,7,14].

(diffusion) process gradually adds noise via:
