A1

Additional quantitative results

A1.1

Comparison with data poisoning method

We provide quantitative results of MIST [27], a data poisoning method for
defending against adaptation. We show the CLIP values after Textual Inversion
adaptation in Fig. A1. We observe a gap between the two lines in purse and
glasses which indicates MIST can prevent the model from learning personalized
concepts in some datasets. However, compared with IMMA, MIST is less robust
and fails in other datasets, e.g., car.
