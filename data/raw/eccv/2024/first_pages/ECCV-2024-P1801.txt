Introduction of Diffusion Model

noise through a fixed Markov Chain of length T formulated as:
 \label {eq:q_sample} \begin {aligned} & q(\vx _{1:T} | \vx _0) = \prod _{t=1}^{T} q(\vx _t | \vx _{t-1}), \\ & q(\vx _t | \vx _{t-1}) = \mcn (\vx _t; \sqrt {\alpha _t} \vx _{t-1}, \beta _t \mbfi ), \end {aligned}

βt , with αt = 1 − βt . The generative process involves an inverted process
p(xt−1 |xt ) approximated by the Gaussian model:
 \label {eq:p_sample} \begin {aligned} & p(\vx _0) = p(\vx _t) \prod _{t=1}^T p(\vx _{t-1} | \vx _t), \\ & p(\vx _{t-1} | \vx _t) = \mcn (\vx _{t-1} | \vmu _t(\vx _t), \sigma _t^2 \mbfi ), \end {aligned}
