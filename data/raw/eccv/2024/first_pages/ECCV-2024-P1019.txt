Overview
The goal of this supplementary material is to provide additional technical details
of the proposed meshing and integration pipeline and to present further evaluation results. To simplify navigation, we follow the structure of the manuscript.
The implementation can be found under moritzheep.github.io/adaptive-screenmeshing.
