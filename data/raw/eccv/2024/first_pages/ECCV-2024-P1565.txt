Datasets

In this section, we provide a more detailed introduction to three benchmark
datasets Epic-Kitchens-100 [1], EGO4D [2], and EGTEA Gaze+ [5], showcasing our annotated data results (Fig. 1). We individually label each hand’s
forthcoming trajectories for each dataset on the last observation frame. On
EGO4D [2], we annotate interaction hotspots for both hands, while on EpicKitchens-100 [1] and EGTEA Gaze+ [5], only one hand’s interaction hotspots
are labeled in the same sample.
1.1

Epic-Kitchens-100 [1]

Epic-Kitchens-100 [1] dataset includes considerable kitchen-related activities,
and we adopt the annotations collected by Liu et al. [6]. The training set comprises
24148 samples, while the validation set contains 3513 samples.
1.2

EGO4D [2]

EGO4D [2] encompasses a broader range of daily scenarios compared to EpicKitchens-100, bringing more difficulties for this task. On the EGO4D dataset [2],
we collect 7345 training samples and 2418 validation samples.
