Abstract. Due to the space limit, we provide some implementation details and extra comparative results of our PTTD profile in this supplementary material. Specifically, the content of this document can be
summarized as follows:
– First of all, more implementation details are provided, such as the
calculation of MOS and the modification details of pre-trained models (e.g., Dehazeformer and NAFNet).
– We provide additional supplementary experiments to prove the generalization capability of our PTTD on models pre-trained on different synthetic datasets.
– We perform a comprehensive investigation into the selection of ys ,
and reveal the performance improvement by searching the optimal
one for every single input.
– We provide additional ablation experiments to validate the effectiveness of some key components, which are necessary in our PTTD.
– User study and more qualitative results are provided to show the
robustness and effectiveness of our PTTD.

Calculation of MOS

In our PTTD, whether a hazy scenes is varicolored or not is determined by
the value of MOS [8]. To avoid the influence of haze-free regions on color cast
estimation, we restrict the MOS computation exclusively to local hazy regions,
which can be formulated as:
