Global transformation

Figure 1 shows the details of global transformation architecture. As mentioned
in Section 4.1 the global transformer in our setup has the same architecture
as [1] except that we removed the attention layers and reduced the number of
downsampling blocks (Conv k5s2, representing a convolution with the kernel size
5 and the stride 2) from 4 blocks to 3 blocks. We also added 12 tanh to bound
the output of encoder between [ 0.5, 0.5].
