Additional Details about DEVIAS

In this section, we provide details of DEVIAS. In our implementation, we employ
ViT-Base [12] as the encoder.
Data augmentation. In DEVIAS, we augment input videos to diversify actionscene combinations. Previous works [11, 38] have demonstrated that scene augmentation by mixing up using a human mask improves action recognition. We
mix a video Vi with another video Vj within a minibatch, by cut-and-paste
operation as follows:
  \label {eq:Data_augmentation} \tilde {\V }_i = \V _i \odot \Tilde {\H }_i + \V _j \odot (1-\Tilde {\H }_i), 

(1)

where , H̃i is a pseudo-human mask of the video Vi extracted by an off-theshelf method [11]. This simple scenario augmentation can diversify action-scene
combinations in a training dataset.
Scene model. In cases where the dataset does not provide ground truth scene
labels, we obtain scene labels by running a frozen off-the-shelf scene recognition
model. We opt to use a ViT-Base as a frozen scene model to generate pseudo
scene labels for each video in the action recognition datasets we use. We prepare
the frozen scene model by training a ViT-Base on the Places365 [37] dataset.
