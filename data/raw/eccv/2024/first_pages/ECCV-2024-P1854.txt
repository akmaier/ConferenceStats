Prompts to SAM

Segment Anything Model (SAM) is a powerful segmentation tool with zero-shot
generalization and fine-grained segmentation abilities. In Pro2SAM, we introduce SAM to weakly supervised object localization (WSOL) to improve the
performance of localization. To prove the effectiveness and advancement of our
grid point prompts, we set two comparison experiments, as follows:
• Single Point Prompt: We employ the point as a prompt, where the point
is generated by the mask prompt via computing the center point of the
connected region.
• Single Box Prompt: We employ the box as a prompt, where the box is
generated by the mask prompt via computing the minimum bounding box
of the connected region.
The structures of the two variants are shown in Fig. 1. Note that the grid point
prompts used in Pro2SAM contain 32 × 32 points, which maximumly avoids
the omission of the target. We conduct comparison experiments on benchmark
CUB-200-2011 and ILSVRC datasets in terms of IOU50, IOU60, IOU70, IOU80,
and IOU90, and the comparison results are shown in Table. 1 and Fig. 2.
From Table. 1, Grid Point Prompts outperform the Single Point/Box Prompts.
On CUB-200-2011, Grid Point Prompts get 5.33%, 8.25%, 9.84%, 9.35%, and
6.42% performance gains compared with the sub-optimal Single Box Prompt in
terms of IOU50, IOU60, IOU70, IOU80, and IOU90, respectively. Similarly, Grid
Point Prompts still achieve 7.8%, 7.62%, 8.0%, 8.38%, and 7.87% performance
