Baseline Method

We conducted a comprehensive comparison of our proposed method with popular and state-of-the-art face anti-spoofing methods in three main categories:
1) Domain Generalization: MADDG (CVPR’19) [16], MDDR (CVPR’20) [22],
DRDG (IJCAI’21) [14], SSDG-R (CVPR’20) [9], SSAN-R (CVPR’22) [26], PatchNet (CVPR’22) [20], HFN+MP (TIFS’22) [1], CIFAS (ICME’22) [15], DiVT-M
(WACV’22) [12], DGUA-FAS (ICIP’23) [4] and TTDG-V [32];
2) Domain Adaptation: 1 GDA (ECCV’22) [33], RFMeta (AAAI’20) [17],
Self-DA (AAAI’20) [24], ANRL (ACM MM’21) [13], D²AM (AAAI’21) [2], NASFAS (TPAMI’20) [28], ADDA (CVPR’17) [19], DRCN (ECCV’16) [3], DupGAN
(CVPR’18) [5], KSA (TIFS’18) [11], DR-UDA (TIFS’20) [23], ADA (ICB’19) [21],
USDAN-Un (PR’21) [10], and CDFTN-L (AAAI’23) [29];
3) Representation Learning for FAS: LDCformer (ICIP’23) [7], DC-CDN
(IJCAI’21) [27], TranFAS (TBIOM’22) [25], RAEDFL (ACPR’21) [8] and ViT
(ECCV’22) [6].
