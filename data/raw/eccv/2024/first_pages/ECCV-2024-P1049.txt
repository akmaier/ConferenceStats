We perform an extensive ablation study to verify the design decisions of
our method. We start from the closest baseline PerspectiveNet + ParamNet [9],
trained on 360 cities, and train 5 diﬀerent models on our OpenPano dataset. We
evaluate the trained models on the MegaDepth [14] and LaMAR [21] test splits
and on the 2k test images sampled from OpenPano . We report the median and
AUC of angular gravity and FoV errors.
