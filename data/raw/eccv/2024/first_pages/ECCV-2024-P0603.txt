Analysis of The Separated Features

To further analyze the performance of the FSM and the semantic information
learned by the three features: head pose feature H, gaze feature G, and gazeirrelevant feature I, we look into the outputs of the three auxiliary tasks themselves. Their output errors/accuracy are 6.61◦ , 3.21◦ and 100% on DE , and
12.72◦ , 6.42◦ and 100% on DG , respectively, which can be considered quite good.
This partially demonstrates the validity of our learnt features of H, G and I.
