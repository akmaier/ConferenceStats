Dataset Details

For the SUN RGB-D dataset [4], we employ version v1, comprising 10,335
RGB-D images, accessible at https://rgbd.cs.princeton.edu/. As for the
ScanNetv2 dataset, we utilize the ScanNet25k version, which extracts frames
approximately every 100 frames from the original ScanNetv2 dataset [1]. For
additional details and the official release for download, please refer to https:
//github.com/ScanNet/ScanNet/tree/master.

Boudning Box Regression Loss

To provide a more detailed representation of the 3D bounding box B, we decompose it into components B = [c, s, ac , ar ], where c ∈ R3 and s ∈ R3 represent
the center and size of the bounding box, respectively. ac ∈ [0, 1]12 denotes the
quantized angle class and ar represents the residual for the angle of the bounding
box. Additionally, let B̂ = [ĉ, ŝ, âc , âr ] represent the predicted bounding box. In
line with prior research [2, 3], we utilize L1 loss for the center and size, Huber
regression loss for the angular residuals, and cross-entropy loss for the angular
classification. The bounding box regression loss is defined as follows:
  \mathcal {L}_{box} = \alpha _1 \| \hat {\textbf {c}} - \textbf {c} \| + \alpha _2 \| \hat {\textbf {s}} - \textbf {s} \| + \alpha _3 \| \hat {\text {a}}_r - \text {a}_r \|_{\mathrm {huber}} + \alpha _4 \mathrm {H}(\hat {\textbf {a}}_c, \textbf {a}_c), 

(1)

where α1 , α2 , α3 , and α4 are hyperparameters, which are 5.0, 1.0, 0.5 and 0.1
respectively. H(x, y) = −y log x denotes the cross-entropy loss.
