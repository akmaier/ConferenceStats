Mutual Information

Here, we theoretically prove the effect of TCM on video continuity by calculating inter-frame mutual information (MI) [3]. Given the current frame Xt and
the previous frame Xt−1 , with the current frame adjusted by TUnet as X̂t , we
can calculate the mutual information between Xt and Xt−1 , and the mutual
information between X̂t and Xt−1 :
  \begin {aligned} MI(X_{t-1},X_t)&=H(X_t)-H(X_t|X_{t-1})\\ &=H(X_t)+\sum _{x_i^{t-1}}p(x_i^{t-1}\sum _{x_j^t}p(x_j^t|x_i^{t-1})\log (p(x_j^t|x_i^{t-1}))\\ &=H(X_t)+\frac {1}{HW}\sum _{x_i^{t-1},x_j^t}p(x_j^t|x_i^{t-1})\log p(x_j^t|x_i^{t-1}),\\ MI(X_{t-1},\hat {X}_t)=&=H(\hat {X}_t)-H(\hat {X}_t|X_{t-1})\\ &=H(\hat {X}_t)+\sum _{x_i^{t-1}}p(x_i^{t-1}\sum _{\hat {x}_j^t}p(\hat {x}_j^t|x_i^{t-1})\log (p(\hat {x}_j^t|x_i^{t-1}))\\ &=H(\hat {X}_t)+\frac {1}{HW}\sum _{x_i^{t-1},\hat {x}_j^t}p(\hat {x}_j^t|x_i^{t-1})\log p(\hat {x}_j^t|x_i^{t-1}),\\ \end {aligned} \label {MI}
