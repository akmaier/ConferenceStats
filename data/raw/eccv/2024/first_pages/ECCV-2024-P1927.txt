A

Results on Small-scaled ID Datasets

In addition to experiments with the large-scale ImageNet-1K ID dataset, we also
report results on smaller-scaled ID datasets following [5]. As shown in Tab. A1,
performance on these smaller datasets is nearing saturation, with our method
and NegLabel both achieving nearly perfect ID/OOD discrimination. On more
challenging tasks (e.g., using ImageNet-1K as the ID dataset and in the nearOOD detection scenario), our method shows a more pronounced improvement,
as shown in the main paper.
