Motion Data

As stated in the main manuscript, we mostly follow the setting in the original
paper of TMR [9]. For pose representation, we converted each frame of J joints
the in the motion sequence M into a 263 dimensional vector consisting of (rva ,
rvx , rvz , rh , jp , jv , jr , f), where rva , rvx , rvz and rh are the angular velocity
around y axis, velocity in x direction, velocity in z direction and height of the root
on the XZ-plane, respectively. jp ∈ R3(J−1) , jv ∈ R3J indicate the positions and
velocities of the J joints. jr ∈ R6(J−1) denotes the six-dimensional representation
of rotation for each joint. f ∈ R4 are the contact state of the feet.
For chronological event shuffling, we select sequences that contain multiple
events. In HumanML3D dataset, multiple events are detected in 13,044 out of
23,384 sequences, 811 out of 1,460 sequences, 2,677 out of 4,380 sequences in the
training, validation, and test sets, respectively.
