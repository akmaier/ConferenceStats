Detailed Data Appendices

To further aid in understanding, a few examples from COM Kitchens are provided with the supplementary material in the examples directory. Samples include (i) unedited recorded videos, (ii) annotations for Japanese recipes, (iii)
annotations for translated English recipes, and (iv) constructed visual action
graphs. Besides, we also provide a video wall (videowall.mp4) to overview the
unedited videos, which demonstrates the diversity of our dataset.
