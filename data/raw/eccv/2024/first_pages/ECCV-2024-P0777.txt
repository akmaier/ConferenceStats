Balance of Loss Terms. Here, we specify the weights of terms in Eq. (10),
Eq. (11) and Eq. (12) in the main paper. The weights α1−3 in Eq. (10) are to
balance the objectives in supervised training for labeled data, {ωi }i=0,1,2,3 in
Eq. (11) are for balancing the focus of ℓ1 loss on wavelet channels {LL,HL,LH,HH}
accordingly, and λ1−3 of Eq. (12) balance the optimization aims in unsupervised
training. The values of α1−3 and {ωi }i=0,1,2,3 are shown in Tab. 4, and we use
λ3 = 0.01 in Eq. (12). The discussion of λ1−2 is in Sec. D.a.
