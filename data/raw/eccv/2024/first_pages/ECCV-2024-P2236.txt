Additional Components Analysis

In the main paper, we conduct a quantitative analysis of the impact of removing
the core components, namely SGE and Noise Perturbation, on model performance. Here, we provide a qualitative analysis of the removal of these components and their effects on model generation. As shown in Figure 2, omitting
each process significantly influences the ability of the model to generate images.
Without SGE and Noise Perturbation, our model effectively becomes an unconditional diffusion model (first row). If we replace SGE with minimal noise
(second row), the generated images appear to have more details, however, most
of these details are illogical. Removing the noise perturbation entirely (third
row) reduced the process to the same setting as reconstruction.
