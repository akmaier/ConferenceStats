Pose-dependent animation

In this stage, the goal is to estimate the pose parameters θ̂ from the original video,
while keeping parameters optimized in geometry and texture optimization fixed.
Following HybrIK [3], we employ a ResNet network to extract features from each
video frame. These features are then processed through deconvolution layers to
generate 3D heatmaps, which are subsequently converted into 3D poses P using
a softargmax function. Concurrently, the features are fed into MLP layers to
estimate twist angles S. The canonical human template T̂, which characterizes
the desired body shape and appearance under the rest pose, is obtained from the
geometry and texture optimization stage. Finally, HybrIK takes P, S, and T̂ as
inputs to estimate the pose parameters θ̂, which parameterize the 3D rotations
of the kinematic joints, enabling the animation of the human body with the
desired poses.
