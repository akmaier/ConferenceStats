Dataset

CIFAR-100/10-LT. CIFAR-100/10-LT is a variant of CIFAR-100/10 [4] dataset
with imbalanced class distributions. The original CIFAR-100/10 dataset comprises 50,000 training images and 10,000 validation images of size 32 × 32, and
consists of 100/10 classes. To ensure a fair comparison, we follow the same longtailed version as used in the previous works [13, 15]. In our experiments, we use
the Imbalance Factor (IF) β to quantify the degree of class imbalance in the
data, which is defined as β = Nmax /Nmin . In our experiments, we evaluate our
approach on CIFAR-100-LT datasets with imbalance factors set to 100, 50, and
10, respectively. The results of CIFAR-10-LT are reported in Appendix.
ImageNet-LT and Places-LT. ImageNet-LT and Places-LT are the long-tailed
variants of the popular ImageNet-2012 [1] and the large-scale scene classification
dataset Places [17], respectively. Liu et al. [8] proposed these datasets and we
follow their work for a comparable evaluation. We sample subsets of the datasets
following the Pareto distribution with the power value γ = 6, as done in their
work. ImageNet-LT comprises 115.8K images belonging to 1000 categories, with
an imbalance factor of β = 1280/5. Similarly, Places-LT contains 184.5K images
from 365 categories, with an imbalance factor of β = 4980/5.
iNaturalist 2018. iNaturalist [3] is a large-scale real-world dataset specifically
designed for long-tailed visual recognition, characterized by an extremely imbalanced class distribution. The dataset consists of 437.5K training images and
24.4K validation images belonging to 8142 categories. The presence of finegrained categories further exacerbates the already challenging task of recognition [14].
