1.1

Depth Maps Generation

The general model to generate hazy frames can be formulated as [2]:
  F(x)_t = \alpha (x)_tB(x)_t + (1-\alpha (x)_t)A_t, 

(1)

where F (x) is the frame with haze and B(x) is the rain-free frame. α(x) is
the atmosphere transmission. A is the atmospheric light. t ∈ {1, 2, ...N } and t
represents the time-step. Here, α = e−βd(t) , where β represents the depth map
and and d(t) represents the atmosphere scattering parameter. As the depth map
is required to generate the hazy frame, method of [6] is used to estimate the
depth map. The way of generating frames with rain accumulation is similar to
the way of generating hazy frames. Therefore, depth maps are also required to
generate frames with both rain streaks and rain accumulation.
