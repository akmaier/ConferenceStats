Overview

The overview of this supplementary is as follows:
– In Section 2, we provide additional background details about diffusion-guided
editing.
– Section 3 contains the details of the data generation process using target captions, architecture details, as well as training and inference. In Algorithm 1,
we summarize our proposed method.
– In Section 4, we provide the details of the user study.
– Additional qualitative and quantitative results are in Section 5.
– Section 6 contains some discussions on the uniqueness of the Free-Editor.

Text-to-Image (T2I) Diffusion Model. The Stable Diffusion (SD) model [21]
operates within the latent space of an autoencoder D(E(·)), specifically VQGAN [7] or VQ-VAE [31]. Here, E denotes the encoder compressing an RGB
image x into a low-resolution latent z = E(x), recoverable using the decoder
x ∼ D(z). The diffusion forward process includes iterative addition of Gaussian
noise to the signal z as follows:
