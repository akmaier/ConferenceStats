B

Implementation details

For the classification task, we follow the default optimizer of other optimizationbased TTA methods when combined with each of them. We conduct a grid
search for the learning rate with values of 1e-4, 1e-5, 1e-6, and 1e-7, and use a
constant learning rate without scheduling. For the semantic segmentation task,
We conduct a grid search for the learning rate with values of 1e-4 and 1e-5, and
keep the other hyperparameters consistent with TTN [3].
