Unit Vector Simplification We have proved that the composition of the
transformation ϖ, projecting onto the unit sphere, and the transformation φ,
projecting onto the tangent plane, is equivalent to the single transformation φ
directly projecting onto the tangent plane:

  \begin {split} \left (\varphi \circ \varpi \right )\left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right ) &=\varphi \left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}{\left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}^{\top }{\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right )^{-1/2}}\right )\\ &={\mathbf {x}^{'}}{\left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}^{\top }{\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right )^{-1/2}}\left (\mathbf {x_0}^{\top }\left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}{\left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}^{\top }{\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right )^{-1/2}}\right )\right )^{-1}\\ &={\mathbf {x}^{'}} \left (\mathbf {x_0}^{\top }{\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right )^{-1}\\ &=\varphi \left ({\mathbf {x}^{'}}\right )\text {.} \end {split}
