Abstract. We propose a new segmentation or clustering model that
combines Markov Random Field (MRF) and Normalized Cut (NC)
objectives. Both NC and MRF models are widely used in machine learning and computer vision, but they were not combined before due to
signiﬁcant diﬀerences in the corresponding optimization, e.g. spectral
relaxation and combinatorial max-ﬂow techniques. On the one hand, we
show that many common applications for multi-label MRF segmentation
energies can beneﬁt from a high-order NC term, e.g. enforcing balanced
clustering of arbitrary high-dimensional image features combining color,
texture, location, depth, motion, etc. On the other hand, standard NC
applications beneﬁt from an inclusion of common pairwise or higherorder MRF constraints, e.g. edge alignment, bin-consistency, label cost,
etc. To address NC+MRF energy, we propose two eﬃcient multi-label
combinatorial optimization techniques, spectral cut and kernel cut, using
new unary bounds for diﬀerent NC formulations.
