Abstract
The rapid progress of photorealistic synthesis techniques have reached at a critical point where the boundary between real and manipulated images starts to blur.
Thus, benchmarking and advancing digital forgery analysis have become a pressing issue. However, existing face
forgery datasets either have limited diversity or only support coarse-grained analysis.
To counter this emerging threat, we construct the
ForgeryNet dataset, an extremely large face forgery dataset
