Abstract
Generative adversarial networks (GANs) can generate
high-quality images from sampled latent codes. Recent
works attempt to edit an image by manipulating its underlying latent code, but rarely go beyond the basic task of attribute adjustment. We propose the first method that enables
manipulation with multidimensional condition such as keypoints and captions. Specifically, we design an algorithm
that searches for a new latent code that satisfies the target
condition based on the Surrogate Gradient Field (SGF) induced by an auxiliary mapping network. For quantitative
comparison, we propose a metric to evaluate the disentanglement of manipulation methods. Thorough experimental
