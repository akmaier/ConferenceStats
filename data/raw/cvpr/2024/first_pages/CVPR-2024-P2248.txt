Deep unfolding networks (DUNs), renowned for their interpretability and superior performance, have invigorated
the realm of compressive sensing (CS). Nonetheless, existing DUNs frequently suffer from issues related to insufficient feature extraction and feature attrition during the iterative steps. In this paper, we propose Unrolling Fixedpoint Continuous Network (UFC-Net), a novel deep CS
framework motivated by the traditional fixed-point continuous optimization algorithm. Specifically, we introduce
Convolution-guided Attention Module (CAM) to serve as a
critical constituent within the reconstruction phase, encompassing tailored components such as Multi-head Attention
Residual Block (MARB), Auxiliary Iterative Reconstruction
Block (AIRB), etc. MARB effectively integrates multi-head
attention mechanisms with convolution to reinforce feature extraction, transcending the confinement of localized
attributes and facilitating the apprehension of long-range
correlations. Meanwhile, AIRB introduces auxiliary variables, significantly bolstering the preservation of features
within each iterative stage. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our proposed UFC-Net achieves remarkable performance both on image CS and CS-magnetic resonance
imaging (CS-MRI) in contrast to state-of-the-art methods.
