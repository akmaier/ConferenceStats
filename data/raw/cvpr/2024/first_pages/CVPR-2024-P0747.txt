Abstract
Despite recent progress in diffusion-based video editing, existing methods are limited to short-length videos due
to the contradiction between long-range consistency and
frame-wise editing. Prior attempts to address this challenge by introducing video-2D representations encounter
significant difficulties with large motion- and view-change
videos, especially in human-centric scenarios. To overcome
