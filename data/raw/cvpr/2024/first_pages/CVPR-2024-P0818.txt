egocentric videos of human manipulation process, as well as the corresponding 3D-pose annotation and task specification.

Abstract
We present OAK I NK 2, a dataset of bimanual object manipulation tasks for complex daily activities. In pursuit
of constructing the complex tasks into a structured representation, OAK I NK 2 introduces three level of abstraction
to organize the manipulation tasks: Affordance, Primitive Task, and Complex Task. OAK I NK 2 features on an
