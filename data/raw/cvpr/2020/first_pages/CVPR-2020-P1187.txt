Visual relocalization estimates the absolute pose of the
camera in a known scene from an image or multiple images.
It plays an important role in various applications including
robotics, autonomous driving, and virtual/augmented reality. In the last two decades, many geometry-based algorithms [37, 38, 23, 30, 29, 35] have been proposed. Recent studies formulate the task as a regression problem using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent
Neural Networks (RNNs). Most of them estimate the camera pose from a single image [18, 25, 17, 19, 5, 46, 44, 16,
4]. Therefore, their performances degrade severely due to
visual ambiguities in challenging conditions, e.g., textureless regions, repetitive textures, local similarities, dynamic
