Abstract
We present Pippo, a generative model capable of producing 1K resolution dense turnaround videos of a person
from a single casually clicked photo. Pippo is a multi-view
diffusion transformer and does not require any additional

inputs — e.g., a ﬁtted parametric model or camera parameters of the input image. We pre-train Pippo on 3B human images without captions, and conduct multi-view mid-training
and post-training on studio captured humans. During midtraining, to quickly absorb the studio dataset, we denoise
