Abstract
As 3D content creation continues to grow, transferring semantic textures between 3D meshes remains a significant
challenge in computer graphics. While recent methods
leverage text-to-image diffusion models for texturing, they
often struggle to preserve the appearance of the source texture during texture transfer. We present T RI T EX, a novel
approach that learns a volumetric texture field from a single textured mesh by mapping semantic features to surface
colors. Using an efficient triplane-based architecture, our
method enables semantic-aware texture transfer to a novel
target mesh. Despite training on just one example, it generalizes effectively to diverse shapes within the same category. Extensive evaluation on our newly created benchmark dataset shows that T RI T EX achieves superior texture
transfer quality and fast inference times compared to existing methods. Our approach advances single-example texture transfer, providing a practical solution for maintaining
visual coherence across related 3D models in applications
like game development and simulation.
