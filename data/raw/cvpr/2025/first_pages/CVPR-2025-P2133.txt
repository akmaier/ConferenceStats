This paper explores scene afﬁnity (AIScene), namely intrascene consistency and inter-scene correlation, for semisupervised LiDAR semantic segmentation in driving scenes.
Adopting teacher-student training, AIScene employs a
teacher network to generate pseudo-labeled scenes from unlabeled data, which then supervise the student network’s
learning. Unlike most methods that include all points in
pseudo-labeled scenes for forward propagation but only
pseudo-labeled points for backpropagation, AIScene removes points without pseudo-labels, ensuring consistency
in both forward and backward propagation within the
scene. This simple point erasure strategy effectively prevents unsupervised, semantically ambiguous points (excluded in backpropagation) from affecting the learning of
pseudo-labeled points. Moreover, AIScene incorporates
patch-based data augmentation, mixing multiple scenes
at both scene and instance levels. Compared to existing augmentation techniques that typically perform scenelevel mixing between two scenes, our method enhances the
semantic diversity of labeled (or pseudo-labeled) scenes,
thereby improving the semi-supervised performance of segmentation models. Experiments show that AIScene outperforms previous methods on two popular benchmarks across
four settings, achieving notable improvements of 1.9% and
2.1% in the most challenging 1% labeled data. The code
will be released at https://github.com/azhuantou/AIScene.
