Abstract
Poster design is a critical medium for visual communication. Prior work has explored automatic poster design using deep learning techniques, but these approaches
lack text accuracy, user customization, and aesthetic appeal, limiting their applicability in artistic domains such as
movies and exhibitions, where both clear content delivery
and visual impact are essential. To address these limitations, we present POSTA: a modular framework powered
by diffusion models and multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for customized artistic poster generation. The

framework consists of three modules. Background Diffusion creates a themed background based on user input. Design MLLM then generates layout and typography elements
that align with and complement the background style. Finally, to enhance the poster’s aesthetic appeal, ArtText Diffusion applies additional stylization to key text elements.
The final result is a visually cohesive and appealing poster,
with a fully modular process that allows for complete customization. To train our models, we develop the PosterArt dataset, comprising high-quality artistic posters annotated with layout, typography, and pixel-level stylized text
