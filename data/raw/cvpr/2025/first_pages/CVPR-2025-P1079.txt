Abstract
We present CaricatureBooth, a system that transforms
caricature creation into a simple interactive experience – as
easy as using a photo booth! A key challenge in caricature
generation is two-fold: the scarcity of high-quality caricature data and the difficulty in enabling precise creative

control over the exaggeration process while maintaining
identity. Prior approaches either require large-scale caricature and photo data or lack intuitive mechanisms for users
to guide the deformation without losing identity. We address the data scarcity by synthesising training data through
