Cross-platform adaptation in event-based dense perception
is crucial for deploying event cameras across diverse settings, such as vehicles, drones, and quadrupeds, each with
unique motion dynamics, viewpoints, and class distributions. In this work, we introduce EventFly, a framework
for robust cross-platform adaptation in event camera perception. Our approach comprises three key components:
i) Event Activation Prior (EAP), which identifies highactivation regions in the target domain to minimize prediction entropy, fostering confident, domain-adaptive predictions; ii) EventBlend, a data-mixing strategy that integrates
source and target event voxel grids based on EAP-driven
similarity and density maps, enhancing feature alignment;
