Abstract
Diffusion models pre-trained on large-scale paired imagetext data achieve significant success in image editing. To
convey more fine-grained visual details, subject-driven editing integrates subjects in user-provided reference images
into existing scenes. However, it is challenging to obtain
photorealistic results, which simulate contextual interactions, such as reflections, illumination, and shadows, induced by merging the target object into the source image.
To address this issue, we propose PS-Diffusion, which ensures realistic and consistent object-scene blending while
maintaining the invariance of subject appearance during
