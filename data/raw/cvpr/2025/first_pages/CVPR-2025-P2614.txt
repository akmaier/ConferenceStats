Abstract
Human image generation is a key focus in image synthesis due to its broad applications, but even slight inaccuracies in anatomy, pose, or details can compromise realism.
To address these challenges, we explore Direct Preference

Optimization (DPO), which trains models to generate preferred (winning) images while diverging from non-preferred
(losing) ones. However, conventional DPO methods use
generated images as winning images, limiting realism. To
