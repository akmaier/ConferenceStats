Figure 1. We demonstrate that curvature-aware preconditioners can significantly accelerate training convergence for neural fields
with suitable activations. Specifically, ESGD (a curvature-aware preconditioned gradient descent algorithm [14]) improves convergence
for wavelet, sine, and Gaussian activations, while Adam performs better for ReLU network with positional encoding (ReLU(PE)). In
a 2D image reconstruction task (Fig. 1a), a wavelet-based neural field trained with ESGD achieves 29.36 PSNR (Fig. 1e) at the 10th
epoch, outperforming Adam’s 26.61 PSNR (Fig. 1d). In a 3D binary occupancy task (Fig. 1b), ESGD enables faster convergence for sine
activations, as shown by the improved armadillo mesh reconstruction at the 1000th epoch (Fig. 1g vs. Fig. 1f). In a Neural Radiance Fields
(NeRF) task (Fig. 1c), a Gaussian-based model trained with ESGD achieves lower reconstruction error (Fig. 1i, 0.0695) compared to Adam
(Fig. 1h, 0.0743), as seen in the error map (darker areas indicate lower error; zoom in 4× for clarity).
