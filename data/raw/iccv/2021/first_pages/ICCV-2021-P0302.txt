Abstract
Effectively structuring deep knowledge plays a pivotal
role in transfer from teacher to student, especially in semantic vision tasks. In this paper, we present a simple knowledge structure to exploit and encode information inside the
detection system to facilitate detector knowledge distillation. Specifically, aiming at solving the feature imbalance
problem while further excavating the missing relation inside
semantic instances, we design a graph whose nodes correspond to instance proposal-level features and edges represent the relation between nodes. To further refine this graph,
we design an adaptive background loss weight to reduce
node noise and background samples mining to prune trivial
edges. We transfer the entire graph as encoded knowledge
representation from teacher to student, capturing local and
global information simultaneously.
We achieve new state-of-the-art results on the challenging COCO object detection task with diverse studentteacher pairs on both one- and two-stage detectors. We
also experiment with instance segmentation to demonstrate
robustness of our method. It is notable that distilled
