To simultaneously overcome these two problems, we propose a novel self-supervised learning framework using a
truncated triplet loss. Precisely, we employ a triplet loss
tending to maximize the relative distance between the positive pair and negative pairs to address the under-clustering
problem; and we construct the negative pair by selecting a
negative sample deputy from all negative samples to avoid
the over-clustering problem, guaranteed by the Bernoulli
Distribution model. We extensively evaluate our framework
in several large-scale benchmarks (e.g., ImageNet, SYSU30k, and COCO). The results demonstrate our model’s superiority (e.g., the learning efficiency) over the latest stateof-the-art methods by a clear margin. See Codes1 .
