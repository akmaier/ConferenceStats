Diffusion models have been applied to 3D LiDAR scene
completion due to their strong training stability and high
completion quality. However, the slow sampling speed limits the practical application of diffusion-based scene completion models since autonomous vehicles require an efficient perception of surrounding environments. This paper
proposes a novel distillation method tailored for 3D LiDAR scene completion models, dubbed ScoreLiDAR, which
achieves efficient yet high-quality scene completion. ScoreLiDAR enables the distilled model to sample in significantly fewer steps after distillation. To improve completion quality, we also introduce a novel Structural Loss,
