Abstract
Recognizing arbitrary or previously unseen categories is essential for comprehensive real-world 3D scene understanding. Currently, all existing methods rely on 2D or textual
modalities during training or together at inference. This
highlights the clear absence of a model capable of processing 3D data alone for learning semantics end-to-end,
along with the necessary data to train such a model. Meanwhile, 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has emerged as the
de facto standard for 3D scene representation across various vision tasks. However, effectively integrating semantic
reasoning into 3DGS in a generalizable manner remains an
open challenge. To address these limitations, we introduce
