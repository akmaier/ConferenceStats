We present FlowSeek, a novel framework for optical flow requiring minimal hardware resources for training. FlowSeek
marries the latest advances on the design space of optical flow networks with cutting-edge single-image depth

parametrization, implementing a compact, yet accurate architecture. FlowSeek is trained on a single consumer-grade
