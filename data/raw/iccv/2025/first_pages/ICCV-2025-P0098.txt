Figure 1. We present FlashDepth, a video depth estimation model that processes high-resolution streaming videos in real-time. Given an
in-the-wild video shown above (left column: original input and output; right column: zoomed-in), our method processes it in a streaming
fashion at 2044→1148 resolution at 24 FPS. In contrast, the state-of-the-art video depth model, DepthCrafter [25], runs at 2.1 FPS at
1024→576 resolution and requires optimizing 110 images at once. Concurrent works like CUT3R [54] supports streaming inputs (512→288
at 14 FPS) but produces blurry depth; Video Depth Anything (VidDepthAny) [8] runs at 24 FPS at 924→518 resolution but requires
optimizing 32 images at once. Our predicted depth maps are noticeably sharper than all baselines, capturing thin structures such as tree
leaves and fur. Zoom in for best viewing results.

A versatile video depth estimation model should (1) be
accurate and consistent across frames, (2) produce highresolution depth maps, and (3) support real-time streaming.

requirements, performing depth estimation on a 2044→1148
