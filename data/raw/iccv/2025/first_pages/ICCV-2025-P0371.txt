Figure 1. We propose WonderPlay, a framework that takes a single image and actions as inputs, and then generates dynamic 3D
scenes that depict the consequence of the actions. WonderPlay allows users to interact with various scenes of diverse physical materials,
e.g., the hat and wine glass (rigid body), the hair (thin strands), the steam (gas), the mushroom (elastic), honey (liquid), and more. See
https://kyleleey.github.io/WonderPlay/ for interactive video results.
