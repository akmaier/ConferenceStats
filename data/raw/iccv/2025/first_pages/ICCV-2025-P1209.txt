Abstract
We introduce StreamDiffusion, a real-time diffusion
pipeline designed for streaming image generation. Existing
diffusion models are adept at creating images from text or image prompts, yet they often fall short in real-time interaction.
This limitation becomes particularly evident in scenarios involving continuous input, such as augmented/virtual reality,
