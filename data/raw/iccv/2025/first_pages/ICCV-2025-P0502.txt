Recently, learned image compression (LIC) models have
achieved remarkable rate-distortion (RD) performance, yet
their high computational complexity severely limits practical deployment. To overcome this challenge, we propose a
novel Stage-wise Modular Distillation framework, SMoDi,
which efficiently compresses LIC models while preserving
RD performance. This framework treats each stage of LIC
models as an independent sub-task, mirroring the teacher
model’s task decomposition to the student, thereby simplifying knowledge transfer. We identify two crucial factors determining the effectiveness of knowledge distillation: student
model construction and loss function design. Specifically, we
first propose Teacher-Guided Student Model Construction,

1. Introduction
The rapid growth of digital image data demands efficient
compression methods. Deep neural network–based learned
image compression (LIC) has recently delivered significant
advances in rate-distortion (RD) performance compared to
traditional compression methods, achieving enhanced compression efficiency and storage savings [3, 4, 26, 45, 46, 63].
