Abstract
Interactive 3D generation is gaining momentum and capturing extensive attention for its potential to create immersive
virtual experiences. However, a critical challenge in cur-

rent 3D generation technologies lies in achieving real-time
interactivity. To address this issue, we introduce WonderTurbo, the first real-time interactive 3D scene generation
framework capable of generating novel perspectives of 3D
scenes within 0.72 seconds. Specifically, WonderTurbo accelerates both geometric and appearance modeling in 3D
