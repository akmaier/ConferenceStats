Abstract
Recent video-language models have shown great potential for video understanding, but still struggle with accurate
temporal grounding for event-level perception. We observe
that two main factors in video understanding (i.e., temporal
grounding and textual response) form a logical hierarchy:
accurate temporal evidence grounding lays the foundation
for reliable textual response. However, existing works typically handle these two tasks in a coupled manner without
a clear logical structure, leading to sub-optimal objectives.
We address this from a factorized learning perspective. We
first propose D2 VLM, a framework that decouples the learning of these two tasks while also emphasizing their inherent
dependency. We adopt a “grounding then answering with
evidence referencing” paradigm and introduce evidence tokens for evidence grounding, which emphasize event-level
