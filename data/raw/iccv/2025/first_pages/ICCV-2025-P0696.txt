Embodied Question Answering (EQA) is a challenging
task in embodied intelligence that requires agents to dynamically explore 3D environments, actively gather visual
information, and perform multi-step reasoning to answer
questions. However, current EQA approaches suffer from
critical limitations in exploration efﬁciency, dataset design,
and evaluation metrics. Moreover, existing datasets often introduce biases or prior knowledge, leading to disembodied reasoning, while frontier-based exploration strategies struggle in cluttered environments and fail to ensure
ﬁne-grained exploration of task-relevant areas. To address
