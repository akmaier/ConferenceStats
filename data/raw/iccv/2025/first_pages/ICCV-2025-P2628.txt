Abstract
The Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture has excelled in
Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs), yet its potential
in real-time open-vocabulary object detectors, which also
leverage large-scale vision-language datasets but smaller
models, remains unexplored. This work investigates this
domain, revealing intriguing insights. In the shallow lay-

ers, experts tend to cooperate with diverse peers to expand
the search space. While in the deeper layers, fixed collaborative structures emerge, where each expert maintains 2-3
fixed partners and distinct expert combinations are specialized in processing specific patterns. Concretely, we propose Dynamic-DINO, which extends Grounding DINO 1.5
Edge from a dense model to a dynamic inference framework via an efficient MoE-Tuning strategy. Additionally,
