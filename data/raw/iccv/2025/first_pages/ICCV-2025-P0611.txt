Federated learning research has recently shifted from Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to Vision Transformers (ViTs) due to their superior capacity. ViTs training demands higher computational resources due to the lack of

this paper, we

leverages masked images to enable efﬁcient, full-parameter
training on resource-constrained clients, offering substantial beneﬁts for learning on heterogeneous data. In general, we patchify images and randomly mask a portion of
the patches, observing that excluding them from training
has minimal impact on performance while substantially reĊ

data

respectively. The local modules are trained on unmasked
image patches, while the global module is trained on intermediate patch features uploaded from the local client,

erase client data distribution privacy. We analyze the computational complexity and privacy protection of EFTViT.
Extensive experiments on popular benchmarks show that

5.6×, cuts local training time by up to 3.1×, and achieves
up to 2.46% accuracy improvement compared to existing
