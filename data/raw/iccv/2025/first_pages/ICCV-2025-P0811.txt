billion tokens from global geospatial data. The model digests multi-scale representations at pixel-level and token-level simultaneously.
TerraMindv1 unlocks (i) generation, (ii) zero-shot and finetuning applications, and (iii) ”Thinking-in-Modalities” finetuning and inference.

We present TerraMind, the first any-to-any generative, multimodal deep learning model for Earth observation (EO).
