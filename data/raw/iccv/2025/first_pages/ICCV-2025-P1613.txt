Abstract
Diffusion models have been recognized for their ability to
generate images that are not only visually appealing but also

of high artistic quality. As a result, Layout-to-Image (L2I)
generation has been proposed to leverage region-specific
positions and descriptions to enable more precise and controllable generation. However, previous methods primarily
