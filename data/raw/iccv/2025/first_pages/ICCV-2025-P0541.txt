Dataset Distillation (DD) aims to generate a compact synthetic dataset that enables models to achieve performance
comparable to training on the full large dataset, significantly reducing computational costs. Drawing from optimal
transport theory, we introduce WMDD (Wasserstein Metricbased Dataset Distillation), a straightforward yet powerful
method that employs the Wasserstein metric to enhance distribution matching.
