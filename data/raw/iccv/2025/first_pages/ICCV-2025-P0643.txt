Humans possess an exceptional ability to imagine 4D
scenes, encompassing both motion and 3D geometry, from
a single still image. This ability is rooted in our accumulated observations of similar scenes and an intuitive understanding of physics. In this paper, we aim to replicate
this capacity in neural networks, specifically focusing on
natural fluid imagery. Existing methods for this task typically employ simplistic 2D motion estimators to animate the
image, leading to motion predictions that often defy physical principles, resulting in unrealistic animations. Our approach introduces a novel method for generating 4D scenes
with physics-consistent animation from a single image. We
