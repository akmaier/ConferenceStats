Abstract
This paper presents the Implicit Knowledge Distillation
Diffusion Transformer (IKDDiT), a groundbreaking model
tailored for photolithography overlay map generation in
semiconductor manufacturing. IKDDiT effectively
addresses the challenges of open-vocabulary overlay map
generation by integrating pre-trained image-text encoders,
diffusion models, and masked transformers. Utilizing
advanced text-to-image diffusion and image-text
discriminative models, it generates high-fidelity overlay
maps across multiple photolithography layers,
significantly mitigating overlay misregistration errors and
minimizing productivity losses caused by wafer rework.
Key innovations include an implicit knowledge distillation
framework that refines inter-image alignment by
decoupling discriminative and generative tasks via an
implicit discriminator, as well as a gated cross-attention
mechanism to enhance generative performance.

1. Introduction
The process of fabricating circuit patterns on silicon
wafers in semiconductor manufacturing fundamentally
relies on multilayer lithography. This technique involves
applying a photoresist coating to the wafer, followed by
light projection through a photomask containing the
requisite circuit patterns. Chip fabrication often involves
over 60 photolithography steps, making precise alignment
between circuit layers crucial. Any misalignment can result
in defects across the entire wafer [1], and the cumulative
effect of overlay misregistration errors (OMEs) makes
accurate alignment increasingly critical as more layers are
added.
