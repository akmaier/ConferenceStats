Robot foundation models, particularly Vision-LanguageAction (VLA) models, have garnered significant attention
for their ability to enhance robot policy learning, greatly
improving robot’s generalization and robustness. OpenAI’s
recent model, O1, showcased impressive capabilities in
solving complex problems by utilizing extensive reasoning
chains. This prompts an important question: can robot
models achieve better performance in multi-task, complex
environments by reviewing prior observations and then providing task-specific reasoning to guide action prediction?
