We present FinMMR, a novel bilingual multimodal benchmark tailored to evaluate the reasoning capabilities of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) in financial numerical reasoning tasks. Compared to existing benchmarks,
our work introduces three significant advancements. (1)
Multimodality: We meticulously transform existing financial reasoning benchmarks, and construct novel questions
from the latest Chinese financial research reports. FinMMR comprises 4.3K questions and 8.7K images spanning
14 categories, including tables, bar charts, and ownership
structure charts. (2) Comprehensiveness: FinMMR encompasses 14 financial subdomains, including corporate
finance, banking, and industry analysis, significantly exceeding existing benchmarks in financial domain knowledge
breadth. (3) Challenge: Models are required to perform

large language models (MLLMs) [5, 19, 46] also exhibit
