Vision-based geolocation techniques that establish spatial
correspondences between smaller query images and larger
georeferenced images have gained significant attention. Existing approaches typically employ a separate ”retrievethen-match” paradigm, whereas such paradigms suffer
from computational inefficiency or precision limitations. To
this end, we propose TopicGeo, a unified framework for
direct and precise query-to-reference image matching via
three key innovations. The textual object semantics, called
topics, distilled from CLIP prompt learning are embedded
into the geolocation framework to eliminate intra-class and
inter-class distribution discrepancies while also enhancing
processing efficiency. Center-based adaptive label assignment and outlier rejection mechanisms as a joint retrievalmatching optimization strategy ensure task-coherent feature
learning and precise spatial correspondences. A multi-level
fine matching pipeline is introduced to refine matching from
