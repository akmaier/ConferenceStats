Despite signiﬁcant advancements in vision-language
models (VLMs), there lack effective approaches to enhance
response quality by scaling inference-time computation.
This capability is known to be a core step towards the selfimproving models in recent large language model studies.
In this paper, we present Vision Value Model (VisVM) that
can guide VLM inference-time search to generate responses
with better visual comprehension. Speciﬁcally, VisVM not
only evaluates the generated sentence quality in the current search step, but also anticipates the quality of subsequent sentences that may result from the current step,
thus providing a long-term value. In this way, VisVM
