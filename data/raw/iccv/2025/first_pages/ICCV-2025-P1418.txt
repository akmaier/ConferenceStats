Abstract
We present a novel perspective on learning video embedders for generative modeling: rather than requiring an
exact reproduction of an input video, an effective embedder should focus on synthesizing visually plausible reconstructions. This relaxed criterion enables substantial improvements in compression ratios without compromising the

quality of downstream generative models. Specifically, we
propose replacing the conventional encoder-decoder video
embedder with an encoder-generator framework that employs a diffusion transformer (DiT) to synthesize missing
details from a compact latent space. Therein, we develop
a dedicated latent conditioning module to condition the
DiT decoder on the encoded video latent embedding. Our
