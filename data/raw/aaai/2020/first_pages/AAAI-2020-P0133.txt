By modeling student knowledge, adaptive learning
environments can personalize delivery of problem
scenarios, hints, scaffolding, and feedback to create
student learning experiences that are more effective than
one-size-fits-all approaches (VanLehn, 2011). However,
predictive student modeling is a challenging machine
learning task because student data is often noisy,
heterogeneous, and expensive to collect (Bosch et al.
2016).
