Abstract
The Augmented Lagragian Method (ALM) and Alternating Direction Method of Multiplier (ADMM) have
been powerful optimization methods for general convex programming subject to linear constraint. We consider the convex problem whose objective consists
of a smooth part and a nonsmooth but simple part.
We propose the Fast Proximal Augmented Lagragian
Method (Fast PALM) which achieves the convergence
rate O(1/K 2 ), compared with O(1/K) by the traditional PALM. In order to further reduce the per-iteration
complexity and handle the multi-blocks problem, we
propose the Fast Proximal ADMM with Parallel Splitting (Fast PL-ADMM-PS) method. It also partially improves the rate related to the smooth part of the objective function. Experimental results on both synthesized
and real world data demonstrate that our fast methods
signiﬁcantly improve the previous PALM and ADMM.
