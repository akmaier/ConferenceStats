Retraction Note to:
Pan-Sharpening with Customized Transformer and Invertible Neural Network
The authors of this paper have formally requested its retraction. This request has been reviewed and approved by AAAI Press, along with the Program and General Chairs of AAAI
2022.
During recent verification tests (as of June 2025), the authors identified significant discrepancies between their current experimental results and those originally reported in the
paper. A subsequent investigation revealed that a subset of the test samples had been inadvertently included in the training set. This data leakage led to artificially inflated performance metrics that cannot be reproduced when using correctly partitioned datasets.
In light of these findings, the authors have concluded that retracting the paper is necessary to prevent the continued dissemination of potentially misleading results.
AAAI Press and the Program and General Chairs of AAAI 2022 fully support this decision. The authors sincerely apologize for any confusion or concern this publication may
have caused within the research community.
July 30th, 2025
