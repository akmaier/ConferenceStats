Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable in-context learning
capabilities across diverse applications. In this work, we explore the effectiveness
of LLMs for generating realistic synthetic tabular data, identifying key prompt design elements to optimize performance. We introduce EPIC, a novel approach that
leverages balanced, grouped data samples and consistent formatting with unique
variable mapping to guide LLMs in generating accurate synthetic data across all
classes, even for imbalanced datasets. Evaluations on real-world datasets show
that EPIC achieves state-of-the-art machine learning classification performance,
significantly improving generation efficiency. These findings highlight the effectiveness of EPIC for synthetic tabular data generation, particularly in addressing
class imbalance. Our source code for our work is available here.
