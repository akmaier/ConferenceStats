Abstract
Recently, a series of diffusion-aware distillation algorithms have emerged to alleviate the computational overhead associated with the multi-step inference process
of Diffusion Models (DMs). Current distillation techniques often dichotomize
into two distinct aspects: i) ODE Trajectory Preservation; and ii) ODE Trajectory
