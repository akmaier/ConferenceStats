Abstract
Physical Human-Scene Interaction (HSI) plays a crucial role in numerous applications. However, existing HSI techniques are limited to specific object dynamics and
privileged information, which prevents the development of more comprehensive
applications. To address this limitation, we introduce HumanVLA for general
object rearrangement directed by practical vision and language. A teacher-student
framework is utilized to develop HumanVLA. A state-based teacher policy is
trained first using goal-conditioned reinforcement learning and adversarial motion prior. Then, it is distilled into a vision-language-action model via behavior
cloning. We propose several key insights to facilitate the large -scale learning process. To support general object rearrangement by physical humanoid, we introduce
a novel Human-in-the-Room dataset encompassing various rearrangement tasks.
Through extensive experiments and analysis, we demonstrate the effectiveness of
the proposed approach.
