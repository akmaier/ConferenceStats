Abstract
The paper proves generalization results for a class of stochastic learning algorithms.
The method applies whenever the algorithm generates an absolutely continuous
distribution relative to some a-priori measure and the Radon Nikodym derivative
has subgaussian concentration. Applications are bounds for the Gibbs algorithm
and randomizations of stable deterministic algorithms as well as PAC-Bayesian
bounds with data-dependent priors.
