Abstract
This paper introduces Bifröst, a novel 3D-aware framework that is built upon diffusion models to perform instruction-based image composition. Previous methods
concentrate on image compositing at the 2D level, which fall short in handling
complex spatial relationships (e.g., occlusion). Bifröst addresses these issues by
training MLLM as a 2.5D location predictor and integrating depth maps as an
extra condition during the generation process to bridge the gap between 2D and
