Abstract
Seams, distortions, wasted UVspace, vertex-duplication, and varying resolution
over the surface are the most prominent issues of the standard UV-based texturing
of meshes. These issues are particularly acute when automatic UV-unwrapping
techniques are used. For this reason, instead of generating textures in automatically
generated UV-planes like most state-of-the-art methods, we propose to represent
textures as coloured point-clouds whose colours are generated by a denoising
diffusion probabilistic model constrained to operate on the surface of 3D objects.
Our sampling and resolution agnostic generative model heavily relies on heat
diffusion over the surface of the meshes for spatial communication between points.
To enable processing of arbitrarily sampled point-cloud textures and ensure longdistance texture consistency we introduce a fast re-sampling of the mesh spectral
properties used during the heat diffusion and introduce a novel heat-diffusionbased self-attention mechanism. Our code and pre-trained models are available at
github.com/simofoti/UV3-TeD.
