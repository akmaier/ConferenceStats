Abstract
We present a novel alignment-before-generation approach to tackle the challenging task of generating general 3D shapes based on 2D images or texts. Directly
learning a conditional generative model from images or texts to 3D shapes is
prone to produce inconsistent results with the conditions because 3D shapes have
an additional dimension whose distribution significantly differs from that of 2D
images and texts. To bridge the domain gap among the three modalities and facilitate multi-modal-conditioned 3D shape generation, we explore representing
3D shapes in a shape-image-text-aligned space. Our framework comprises two
