Sequences of millions of bytes are ubiquitous;
for example, music, image, or video files typically consist of multiple megabytes. However,
large transformer decoders (LLMs) typically
only use several thousand tokens of context
(Brown et al., 2020; Zhang et al., 2022a)—both
because of the quadratic cost of self-attention
but also, more importantly, the cost of large feedforward networks per-position. This severely
limits the set of tasks where LLMs can be applied.
