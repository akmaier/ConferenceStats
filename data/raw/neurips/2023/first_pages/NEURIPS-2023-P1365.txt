Abstract
Text-guided image editing is widely needed in daily life, ranging from personal
use to professional applications such as Photoshop. However, existing methods
are either zero-shot or trained on an automatically synthesized dataset, which
contains a high volume of noise. Thus, they still require lots of manual tuning to produce desirable outcomes in practice. To address this issue, we introduce M AGIC B RUSH (https://osu-nlp-group.github.io/MagicBrush/),
the first large-scale, manually annotated dataset for instruction-guided real image
editing that covers diverse scenarios: single-turn, multi-turn, mask-provided, and
mask-free editing. M AGIC B RUSH comprises over 10K manually annotated triplets
(source image, instruction, target image), which supports trainining large-scale
text-guided image editing models. We fine-tune InstructPix2Pix on M AGIC B RUSH
