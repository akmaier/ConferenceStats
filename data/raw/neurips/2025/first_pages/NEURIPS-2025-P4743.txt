Abstract
Generative depth estimation methods leverage the rich visual priors stored in
pre-trained text-to-image diffusion models, demonstrating astonishing zero-shot
capability. However, parameter updates during training lead to catastrophic degradation in the image generation capability of the pre-trained model. We introduce
M ERGE, a unified model for image generation and depth estimation, starting from
a fixed pre-trained text-to-image model. M ERGE demonstrates that the pre-trained
text-to-image model can do more than image generation, but also expand to depth
estimation effortlessly. Specifically, M ERGE introduces a play-and-plug framework
that enables seamless switching between image generation and depth estimation
