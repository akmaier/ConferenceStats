Abstract
Today, people can easily record memorable moments, ranging from concerts, sports
events, lectures, family gatherings, and birthday parties with multiple consumer
cameras. However, synchronizing these cross-camera streams remains challenging.
Existing methods assume controlled settings, specific targets, manual correction,
or costly hardware. We present VisualSync, an optimization framework based on
multi-view dynamics that aligns unposed, unsynchronized videos at millisecond
accuracy. Our key insight is that any moving 3D point, when co-visible in two
cameras, obeys epipolar constraints once properly synchronized. To exploit this,
