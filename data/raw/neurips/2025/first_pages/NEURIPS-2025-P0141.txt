Abstract
Recent research on motion generation has shown significant progress in generating
semantically aligned motion with singular semantics. However, when employing these models to create composite sequences containing multiple semantically
generated motion clips, they often struggle to preserve the continuity of motion
dynamics at the transition boundaries between clips, resulting in awkward transitions and abrupt artifacts. To address these challenges, we present Compositional
Phase Diffusion, which leverages the Semantic Phase Diffusion Module (SPDM)
and Transitional Phase Diffusion Module (TPDM) to progressively incorporate
semantic guidance and phase details from adjacent motion clips into the diffusion
process. Specifically, SPDM and TPDM operate within the latent motion frequency
domain established by the pre-trained Action-Centric Motion Phase Autoencoder
