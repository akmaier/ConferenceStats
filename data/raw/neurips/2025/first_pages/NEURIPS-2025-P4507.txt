Abstract
With the rapid advancement of generative models, general-purpose generation
has gained increasing attention as a promising approach to unify diverse tasks
across modalities within a single system. Despite this progress, existing opensource frameworks often remain fragile and struggle to support complex real-world
applications due to the lack of structured workflow planning and execution-level
feedback. To address these limitations, we present ComfyMind, a collaborative
AI system designed to enable robust and scalable general-purpose generation,
