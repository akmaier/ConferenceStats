Figure 1: We present 4KAgent, an agentic image super-resolution generalist designed to universally upscale
any image to 4K, regardless of input type, degradation level, or domain. That is, 4KAgent effectively restores
diverse imagery, spanning from natural scenes, severely degraded captures (e.g., old photos), human/pet
portraits, AI-generated content (AIGC), as well as specialized scientific imaging domains, such as remote
sensing, fluorescence microscopy, pathology, and various medical modalities like X-ray, ultrasound, and
funduscopy—all without the need for any re-training or domain-specific adaptation.
