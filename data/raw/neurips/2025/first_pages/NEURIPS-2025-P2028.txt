Abstract
3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has emerged as a powerful technique for radiance
field rendering, but it typically requires millions of redundant Gaussian primitives,
overwhelming memory and rendering budgets. Existing compaction approaches
address this by pruning Gaussians based on heuristic importance scores, without
global fidelity guarantee. To bridge this gap, we propose a novel optimal transport
perspective that casts 3DGS compaction as global Gaussian mixture reduction.
Specifically, we first minimize the composite transport divergence over a KDtree partition to produce a compact geometric representation, and then decouple
appearance from geometry by fine-tuning color and opacity attributes with far fewer
Gaussian primitives. Experiments on benchmark datasets show that our method
(i) yields negligible loss in rendering quality (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) compared to
vanilla 3DGS with only 10% Gaussians; and (ii) consistently outperforms stateof-the-art 3DGS compaction techniques. Notably, our method is applicable to
any stage of vanilla or accelerated 3DGS pipelines, providing an efficient and
agnostic pathway to lightweight neural rendering. The code is publicly available at
