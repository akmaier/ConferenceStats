Abstract
Simultaneous understanding and 3D reconstruction plays an important role in
developing end-to-end embodied intelligent systems. To achieve this, recent approaches resort to 2D-to-3D feature alignment paradigm, which leads to limited
3D understanding capability and potential semantic information loss. In light
of this, we propose SIU3R, the first alignment-free framework for generalizable
simultaneous understanding and 3D reconstruction from unposed images. Specifically, SIU3R bridges reconstruction and understanding tasks via pixel-aligned 3D
representation, and unifies multiple understanding (segmentation) tasks into a set
