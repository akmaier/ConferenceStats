Abstract
Leveraging diverse robotic data for pretraining remains a critical challenge. Existing methods typically model the dataset’s action distribution using simple observations as inputs. However, these inputs are often incomplete, resulting in a dispersed
conditional action distribution—an issue we refer to as coordinate system chaos
and state chaos. This inconsistency significantly hampers pretraining efficiency. To
address this, we propose 4D-VLA, a novel approach that effectively integrates 4D
