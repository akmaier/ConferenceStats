Abstract
Diffusion-based models have shown great promise in molecular generation but
often require a large number of sampling steps to generate valid samples. In this
paper, we introduce a novel Straight-Line Diffusion Model (SLDM) to tackle this
problem, by formulating the diffusion process to follow a linear trajectory. The
proposed process aligns well with the noise sensitivity characteristic of molecular
structures and uniformly distributes reconstruction effort across the generative
process, thus enhancing learning efficiency and efficacy. Consequently, SLDM
achieves state-of-the-art performance on 3D molecule generation benchmarks,
delivering a 100-fold improvement in sampling efficiency.1
