Abstract
Modern interactive applications increasingly demand dynamic 3D content, yet the
transformation of static 3D models into animated assets constitutes a significant bottleneck in content creation pipelines. While recent advances in generative AI have
revolutionized static 3D model creation, rigging and animation continue to depend
heavily on expert intervention. We present Puppeteer, a comprehensive framework
that addresses both automatic rigging and animation for diverse 3D objects. Our
system first predicts plausible skeletal structures via an auto-regressive transformer
that introduces a joint-based tokenization strategy for compact representation and a
hierarchical ordering methodology with stochastic perturbation that enhances bidirectional learning capabilities. It then infers skinning weights via an attention-based
