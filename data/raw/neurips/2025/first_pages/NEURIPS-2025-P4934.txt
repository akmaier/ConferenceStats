Abstract
Text-guided 3D editing aims to precisely edit semantically relevant local 3D regions, which has significant potential for various practical applications ranging
from 3D games to film production. Existing methods typically follow a viewindiscriminate paradigm: editing 2D views indiscriminately and projecting them
back into 3D space. However, they overlook the different cross-view interdependencies, resulting in inconsistent multi-view editing. In this study, we argue that
ideal consistent 3D editing can be achieved through a progressive-views paradigm,
