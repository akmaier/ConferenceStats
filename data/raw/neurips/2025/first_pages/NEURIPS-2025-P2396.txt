Abstract
In this work, we address the task of 3D reconstruction in dynamic scenes, where
object motions frequently degrade the quality of previous 3D pointmap regression
methods, such as DUSt3R, that are originally designed for static 3D scene reconstruction. Although these methods provide an elegant and powerful solution in
static settings, they struggle in the presence of dynamic motions that disrupt alignment based solely on camera poses. To overcome this, we propose D2 USt3R that
