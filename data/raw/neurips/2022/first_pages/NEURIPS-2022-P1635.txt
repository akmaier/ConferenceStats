Introduction

Ensuring privacy of users’ data in machine learning algorithms is an important desideratum for
multiple domains, such as social and medical sciences. Differential privacy (DP) has become the gold
standard of privacy-preserving data analysis as it offers formal and quantitative privacy guarantees
and enjoys many attractive properties from an algorithmic design perspective [DR+ 14]. However,
for various basic machine learning tasks, the known risk guarantees are potentially sub-optimal and
pessimistic.
