Abstract
(Distributionally) robust optimization has gained momentum in machine learning
community recently, due to its promising applications in developing generalizable
learning paradigms. In this paper, we derive generalization bounds for robust optimization and Wasserstein robust optimization for Lipschitz and piecewise Hölder
smooth loss functions under both stochastic and adversarial setting, assuming that
the underlying data distribution satisfies transportation-information inequalities.
The proofs are built on new generalization bounds for variation regularization (such
as Lipschitz or gradient regularization) and its connection with robustness.
