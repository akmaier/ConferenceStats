Abstract
In the misspecified kernel ridge regression problem, researchers usually assume the underground
true function fρ∗ ∈ [H]s , a less-smooth interpolation space of a reproducing kernel Hilbert space
(RKHS) H for some s ∈ (0, 1). The existing minimax optimal results require ∥fρ∗ ∥L∞ < ∞ which
implicitly requires s > α0 where α0 ∈ (0, 1) is
the embedding index, a constant depending on H.
Whether the KRR is optimal for all s ∈ (0, 1) is
an outstanding problem lasting for years. In this
paper, we show that KRR is minimax optimal for
any s ∈ (0, 1) when the H is a Sobolev RKHS.
