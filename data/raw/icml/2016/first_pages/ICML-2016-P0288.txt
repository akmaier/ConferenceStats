Abstract
This paper considers the problem of canonicalcorrelation analysis, and more broadly, the generalized eigenvector problem for a pair of symmetric matrices. We consider the setting of finding
top-k canonical/eigen subspace, and solve these
problems through a general framework that simply requires black box access to an approximate
linear system solver. Instantiating this framework with accelerated
