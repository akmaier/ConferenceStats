Recently the state space models (SSMs) with efficient hardware-aware designs, i.e., the Mamba
deep learning model, have shown great potential for long sequence modeling. Meanwhile
building efficient and generic vision backbones
purely upon SSMs is an appealing direction. However, representing visual data is challenging for
SSMs due to the position-sensitivity of visual
data and the requirement of global context for
visual understanding. In this paper, we show that
the reliance on self-attention for visual representation learning is not necessary and propose a
new generic vision backbone with bidirectional
Mamba blocks (Vim), which marks the image
