Finite-sum optimization has been widely studied in previous literature given their wide applicability. Zhang (2004)
solved finite-sum optimization by randomly selecting an index i ∈ [n] and applying stochastic gradient descent (SGD).
More efficient algorithms apply variance reduction, including SAG by Roux et al. (2012), SDCA by Shalev-Shwartz &
Zhang (2013), SVRG by Johnson & Zhang (2013), and also
many other works (Zhang et al., 2013; Defazio et al., 2014;
Xiao & Zhang, 2014; Allen-Zhu & Yuan, 2016; Allen-Zhu
& Hazan, 2016b; Reddi et al., 2016; Shalev-Shwartz, 2016;
