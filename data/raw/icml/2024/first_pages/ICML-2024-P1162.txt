1. Introduction
Autoregressive decoding, the de facto standard for large
language models (LLMs), generates tokens sequentially,
leading to slow and costly generation. Speculative sampling
(Leviathan et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023a) based methods
address this by dividing the process into a low-cost drafting
stage and a parallelized verification stage over the drafted
tokens, allowing for multiple tokens to be validated in a
single LLM pass. These approaches accelerate generation
by producing multiple tokens per pass. More importantly,
