Foundation models have emerged as a promising
approach in time series forecasting (TSF). Existing approaches either repurpose large language
models (LLMs) or build large-scale time series
datasets to develop TSF foundation models for
universal forecasting. However, these methods
face challenges due to the severe cross-domain
gap or in-domain heterogeneity. This paper explores a new road to building a TSF foundation
model from rich, high-quality natural images. Our
key insight is that a visual masked autoencoder,
pre-trained on the ImageNet dataset, can naturally be a numeric series forecaster. By refor1
