model spatial and temporal dependencies, enabling a more
holistic modeling for correlated time series data.

Time series forecasting fundamentally relies on
accurately modeling complex interdependencies
and shared patterns within time series data. Recent advancements, such as Spatio-Temporal
Graph Neural Networks (STGNNs) and Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs), have demonstrated promising results by effectively capturing intricate spatial and temporal dependencies
across diverse real-world datasets. However, these
models typically require large volumes of training data and often struggle in data-scarce scenarios. To address this limitation, we propose
a framework named Few-shot Spatio-Temporal
Large Language Models (FSTLLM), aimed at
enhancing model robustness and predictive performance in few-shot settings. FSTLLM leverages the contextual knowledge embedded in Large
Language Models (LLMs) to provide reasonable
and accurate predictions. In addition, it supports the seamless integration of existing forecasting models to further boost their predicative
capabilities. Experimental results on real-world
datasets demonstrate the adaptability and consistently superior performance of FSTLLM over major baseline models by a significant margin. Our
code is available at: https://github.com/
JIANGYUE61610306/FSTLLM.
