etc. The famous random Fourier feature methods (Rahimi
& Recht, 2007; 2008; Le et al., 2013; Yang et al., 2014;
Yu et al., 2016; Tancik et al., 2020; Li et al., 2020; Cheng

both theory and practice, finding an efficient algorithm to
compute the Fourier transform is of utmost importance.
