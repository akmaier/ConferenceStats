We revisit the recently introduced Local GlivenkoCantelli setting, which studies distributiondependent uniform convergence rates of the Empirical Mean Estimator (EME). In this work, we
investigate generalizations of this setting where
arbitrary estimators are allowed rather than just
the EME. Can a strictly larger class of measures
be learned? Can better risk decay rates be obtained? We provide exhaustive answers to these
questions—which are both negative, provided the
learner is barred from exploiting some infinitedimensional pathologies. On the other hand, allowing such exploits does lead to a strictly larger
class of learnable measures.
