Diffusion Transformers (DiTs) have proven effective in generating high-quality videos but are
hindered by high computational costs. Existing video DiT sampling acceleration methods often rely on costly fine-tuning or exhibit limited
generalization capabilities. We propose Asymmetric Reduction and Restoration (AsymRnR), a
training-free and model-agnostic method to accelerate video DiTs. It builds on the observation
that redundancies of feature tokens in DiTs vary
