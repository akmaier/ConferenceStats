In the context of machine learning, distributed distinct element estimation plays a crucial role in many applications
where data is distributed across multiple nodes or servers.
For instance, in collaborative filtering (Resnick et al., 1994),
such as recommendation systems (Aggarwal, 2016; Koren
et al., 2009), estimating the distinct preferences or behaviors of users across various platforms requires efficient distributed algorithms. Similarly, in anomaly detection (Chandola et al., 2009), identifying rare or novel events across
different data sources—such as network traffic or sensor
data—requires tracking unique occurrences without centralized data aggregation. Distributed distinct element estimation is also relevant in federated learning (McMahan
