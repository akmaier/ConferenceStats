Actor-critic algorithms have emerged as a foundational approach in reinforcement learning (RL), mitigating the deficiencies of both policy-based and value-based approaches
(Sutton and Barto, 2018; Mnih et al., 2016; Haarnoja et al.,
2018). These methods combine two components: an actor,
which directly learns and improves the policy, and a critic,
which evaluates the policy’s quality. Given their popularity,
significant recent progress has been made in understanding their theoretical underpinnings and statistical efficiency,
